About us

The voice of Bangladesh's founders

Startup, business and technology news — verified, in plain language, first.

Voice of Startup Bangladesh is a leading digital platform for startup, business, and technology news, covering the people, ideas, and innovations shaping the region's startup ecosystem — from early-stage founders to funded ventures.

We deliver breaking updates, founder interviews, funding news, and business insights, helping entrepreneurs, investors, and job seekers stay connected to what's happening across Bangladesh's tech and business landscape.

Our mission

To bring accurate, verified and timely information to Bangladeshi entrepreneurs in plain language — so no opportunity is missed.

How we verify

We gather information from government-approved bodies such as BIDA, BEPZA, BEZA, BSCIC, the ICT Ministry, Bangladesh Hi-Tech Park Authority and the PPP Authority.

We cite these bodies as sources; this website is not an official site of any of them.

  • ✔
    Verified sources

    We rely on official bodies such as BIDA, BEPZA, BEZA, BSCIC, the ICT Division, the Hi-Tech Park Authority and the PPP Authority, and name the source on every article.

  • 🏷️
    Clearly labelled ads

    Advertising is always marked “Ad” and kept apart from news. Advertisers do not control our coverage.

  • 🛠️
    Corrections

    If we get something wrong, we fix it. Spotted a mistake? Write to editor@voiceofstartup.bd.

  • 🌐
    Bangla and English

    Every story can be read in Bangla and English. When a version is machine-translated, readers are told.
