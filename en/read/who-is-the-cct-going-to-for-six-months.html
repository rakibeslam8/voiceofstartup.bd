Who is the CCT going to for six months?

By বিশেষ প্রতিবেদক21 November 2026 at 3:17 pm4 min read

Four organizations have submitted tenders for the six-month operation of Chattogram Container Terminal (CCT) of Chattogram Port. Out of this, only one of the 9 organizations currently engaged in container handling at the port has participated. As a result, discussions are going on in the port about who will be responsible for this important terminal.

CCT can load and unload containers from two ships simultaneously. Saif Powertech Ltd. was operating the terminal through competitive bidding. The port's contract with the company expires on November 2. The port authorities ordered Saif Powertech to continue operations for another three weeks on an emergency basis as no new operator was appointed.

After this period, the port decided to appoint an operator on a limited tender basis for six months to keep the terminal operational. The tender was published on November 17. The time to buy the tender was until the next day. After opening the tender last Thursday, it can be seen that four companies have submitted proposals.

I have no say in the matter. We welcome whatever decision the government deems fit. — Tarfdar Ruhul Amin, Managing Director, Saif Powertech.

Lowest price for Everest

According to port sources, the four companies that submitted the tender are Everest Port Services Limited, B&M-Oceania JV, Shamim Enterprises (Pvt) Limited and The Civil Engineers Limited.

Among them, Everest Port Services gave the lowest price. The institution is owned by the family of Shahadat Hossain Selim, Member of Parliament of BNP from Laxmipur-1 (Ramganj) Constituency. The company's proposal for six months is 30 crores and 15 lakhs. Port sources said two of the four bidders did not provide the required security bond. But the new operator will be selected only after technical and financial evaluation, not just the lowest bid.

Among the four companies, Everest Port Services is currently working as a berth operator at a jetty of the General Cargo Berth (GCB) of Chattogram Port. The other three companies did not handle container loading and unloading at Chattogram port.

Secretary of Chattogram Port Syed Refayet Hamim told Prothom Alo that the information about the CCT tender will be officially informed. He also said that the new operator will be appointed by November 23 after the evaluation.

The Port Authority relaxed the conditions with the approval of the Ministry of Shipping. But after the publication of the tender, the time to buy it was only one day. In the end, only one of the 9 companies with experience in handling containers at the port submitted a tender.

Time is short, most of the veterans are out

The Port Authority relaxed the conditions with the approval of the Ministry of Shipping to allow experienced and qualified organizations to participate in the CCT tender. But after the publication of the tender, the time to buy it was only one day. In the end, only one of the 9 companies with experience in handling containers at the port submitted a tender.

Although no one has made an official complaint about this, it has been reported that some other organizations were interested in participating in the tender. However, they could not participate due to delay in receiving tender news and little time for preparation. The port has raised questions about whether experienced firms were given adequate opportunities to participate.

Saif Powertech, currently managing CCT, also did not participate in the tender. Tarfdar Ruhul Amin, managing director of the company, told Prothom Alo, "I have no comment on the matter." We welcome the decision that the government thinks is best.'

Of the five berth operators currently working on the GCB, none other than Everest has bid.

President of Berth Operators, Shiphandling Operators and Terminal Operators Owners Association (Botswa) Fazle Ekram Chowdhury told Prothom Alo, "We have experience, there is no doubt about that. But the news was late and time was short. Because of this, no one except one of us could participate in the tender.

Seven member committee on assessment

Chattogram Port Authority formed a committee of seven members last Wednesday to evaluate the tender. The Member of Ports (Engineering) is the convener of this committee. In addition to port officials, there are also representatives of Chattogram University of Engineering and Technology and local government engineering department.

The committee is scheduled to start tender evaluation from Saturday. The port aims to appoint a new operator for six months by November 23 after the assessment is completed.
