Image-video-audio-writing AI or not, Google will tell

By VOSB Desk3 November 2026 at 6:11 pm1 min read

Google has come up with a new solution to eliminate the confusion about whether an image, video or audio clip is created by artificial intelligence or AI in the online world.

This verification portal named SynthID Detector created by Google is now open to everyone worldwide. The service is made available to general users around the world in English through partnerships with OpenAI, Nvidia, Kakao and soon Apple. As a result, verifying the authenticity of digital assets or content has become easier and more effective than ever before.

The source of any media file can be easily checked through this portal. To use it first you need to login to synthid.com website. Then upload your suspicious or curious image, video or audio file to that portal. The portal will then automatically scan the file to see if the content or content contains any SynthID watermarks provided by Google or partner organizations. This watermark basically acts as an invisible or digital signal, which cannot be detected by the naked eye but can be easily detected with the help of AI detection technology.

According to Google, such tools play a crucial role in preventing deepfakes and AI-related disinformation in the current digital age. Internet users can now easily identify fake or AI-generated media content, which will help maintain accurate information online. This initiative by Google is considered as a big step in the field of digital security. To know more details about this and to get a deep understanding of the functionality of the technology, users can visit the designated website (goo.gle/4rU9uOW) and get the necessary information.
