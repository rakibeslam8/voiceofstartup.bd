The story of Saiful, a successful entrepreneur who left his traditional job and became an agriculturist

By VOSB Desk31 October 2026 at 6:41 pm2 min read

Starting from the soil of Faridpur, 'Bismillah Agro' is now a well-known and reliable agricultural brand in the country. Saiful Islam Saif chose agriculture instead of chasing traditional jobs. From production of paddy, wheat, mustard oil, pure ghee to cattle rearing and running a dairy farm, he has set a shining example of multi-faceted agriculture. Utilizing modern technology, hard work and proper planning, he has proved that if you work with the right intention, the soil does not turn anyone away. With over a million followers on social media, Saif is not just a successful farmer, but a symbol of the simple life.

Neither the income of lakhs of rupees nor the national fame could change Saif's simplicity. One of his statements given in an interview recently caught people's attention. In an emotional voice he said, my only goal in life is to go to heaven. Such a simple spiritual thought of a young entrepreneur avoiding worldly temptations has spread the message of honesty and faith in the net world.

Along with fame, Saif's life also came with tough physical struggles. Earlier this year, it was revealed that he is suffering from complex problems of liver, heart and brain and one of his hands is partially paralyzed. Despite such extreme physical ailments, he had to face the irony of fame. People came from far and wide to see his farm and seek advice. Even with a sick body, he respected the love of people and asked everyone for good health. Fans also took to social media to pray for his speedy recovery.

A new chapter has been added to Saiful's life after all the storms. He got married late last year. The presence of life partner gives him great emotional strength during this difficult time of life. Now somewhat recovered, he is again spending time on the farm, speaking on modern agriculture and attending various seminars. This life struggle of Saiful has proved that the real measure of life is love, honesty and service to creation rather than bank balance or fame.
