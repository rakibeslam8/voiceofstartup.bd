Zahidul returned to the United States and is now a successful agricultural entrepreneur

By VOSB Desk9 November 2026 at 8:43 am5 min read

After spending almost a century and a half abroad, he returned to the country and established himself as an agricultural entrepreneur. Zahidul Islam. Originally, his venture started with the idea of ​​unadulterated crops, later it took a commercial form. Zahidul Islam started farming by buying about 100 bighas of land in Padma Char. Currently, 20 types of crops are being produced in this entrepreneur's farm. Hundreds of products are being made.

Zahidul Islam was born in the family of Rajbari. Admitted to Jhenaidah Cadet College in seventh grade. The dream was to join the army. Get that opportunity at the end of high school. That was in 1982. But luck didn't help, he tore a ligament in his leg while training. Dropped out of the job. He was admitted to medical school the next year on the wishes of his family. The mind did not last there either. After leaving medical school, he was admitted to Dhaka University.

Parents wanted the son to be a full-time employee. But Zahidul was interested in business. For this, after completing his studies, he went into the contracting and import business. After three years of business, he faced various challenges. Understand that he has little experience in business. From that idea, he was again admitted to Dhaka University's IBA.

After completing his MBA in 1990, he started working as a project manager for a private business group. After working there for two and a half years, he moved to the United States in 1992. He went there and first took some courses on information technology. Then he started business by creating an e-commerce website. After spending 20 years in the United States, Zahidul returned to the country in 2012.

Start on your own

After returning to the country in 2012, Zahidul founded a construction sector consulting firm with an architect friend. While working there, progress towards becoming an entrepreneur. Zahidul said, there are questions about the quality of the crops available in Dhaka. So we started small scale farming in the village to get unadulterated crops for the family. That was in 2014. In the beginning, he started cultivating some pulses, wheat, mustard and vegetables in his ancestral house in Rajbari. Good yield at the end of the season. He has met the needs of his family and sold some products to his acquaintances. With this success, he thought of producing crops commercially. In 2015, he founded the agricultural products company Kole-Kapra.

In the beginning, Zahidul Islam was uneasy about where to get land for large-scale crop production. He said, later I saw that there is a huge land lying uncultivated on the border of Pabna and Kushtia near the lap of Padma. He bought about 100 bigha of land there. Some local people were hired to make the land suitable for cultivation. The initial investment in these works was around 60 lakhs. Later took loan from Palli-Karmasahayak Foundation (PKSF). Zahidul said that about five crore rupees have been invested so far in the purchase of land, preparation of land, construction of farms, construction of processing units and cultivation. He bought 100 bighas of land for cultivation. At present, 12 regular and 25 seasonal employees work in his company. His company Kole-Kapra sells an average of Tk 20-25 lakh every year.

crops that grow

Zahidul's farm produces crops such as sugarcane, rice, wheat, several types of pulses, barley, buckwheat, oats, chia seeds, sesame seeds, fenugreek seeds, and kalijira. Apart from this, there is mustard, coriander, onion and garlic. This entrepreneur also cultivates lentil, peas, mashkalai, arahar, mung and helen pulses. Zahidul also made various by-products by processing these crops. They make flour from rice and wheat and sell it. Different types of molasses are made from sugarcane. It has three processing units for these tasks. Apart from this, he sells various types of leaves and flowers for medicinal use, such as aparajita, rosella tea and jaba and leaves of curry, neem, tulsi, sajne and papaya.

Zahidul said that the number of buyers of kole-kapra products is mainly Dhaka-based. Apart from this, a large amount of response is also received from Chittagong region. Along with individual buyers there are also wholesale buyers. He delivers products through courier across the country. Individual buyers mainly order products through WhatsApp groups. Sales and marketing is handled by his wife Julia Yasmeen. Kole-Kapra has its own sales center in Banani, Dhaka.

Agricultural entrepreneur Zahidul has a dream. That is farming in a sustainable manner while protecting the ecosystem. For this he uses natural methods instead of chemical pesticides. He claims that the quality of the crop is correct. Let's give an example. He uses a mixture of 11 types of thita leaves to eliminate crop pests.

But he has some challenges in this business. Some parts of the land bought by the river have been destroyed. Some land has been taken over by local miscreants. Zahidul said that there is a plan to expand business in livestock, fisheries, poultry etc. sectors in the future. He said, 'Eco-sustainable agriculture is very important for our country. It does not require a huge investment. If the government gives policy support and security to entrepreneurs like us, it is possible to expand this work on a community basis.
