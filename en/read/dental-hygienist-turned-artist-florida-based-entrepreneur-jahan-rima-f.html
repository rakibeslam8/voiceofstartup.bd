Dental hygienist-turned-artist, Florida-based entrepreneur Jahan Rima from Sandeep

By VOSB Desk7 November 2026 at 3:57 pm5 min read

Bangladesh girl Masuma Jahan Rima. A dental hygienist in the US by profession. Revealed himself as a writer more than an era ago. As an artist, he has developed a reputation for half a century; And the last name is written as an entrepreneur. He is now the owner of his own business in a large shopping mall in the United States.

Born in Sandwip, Chittagong, Jahan Rima's life path once reached Florida, USA. At one end of that path is medical care, at the other end is literature.

Between art, editing, creativity and now business. Before the end of one introduction, another one came knocking at the door. Talking about his identity, Jahan Rima's answer is quite profound, 'My life is mixed. A dental hygienist by profession; Writer to mind; Artist by passion; And I am an entrepreneur by my own will.

Which identity best describes Jahan Rima? Hearing the question, he laughed and said, "All of them are me, and none of them are just me."

This one sentence captures his entire life philosophy. He never pitted one identity against another; Instead, he has brought forward each form of life. Service worker Rima came forward during Corona. Being involved in the dental hygiene profession, he stood on the front lines—when people all over the world were confined to their homes in search of protection. In 2020, he rushed to the side of affected patients in remote areas. He received a certificate of appreciation from the US non-profit organization 'Remote Area Medical' (RAM) in recognition of standing by people during the epidemic.

Although it is a profession to take care of people's laughter, his identity as a writer was born from the desire to bind people's minds, relationships and lives in words. In 2019, his first story book 'Je Tumi Manhoot Chicho' was published. In 2021, he jointly published the book of poetry 'Ripavane Bivorima' with his twin sister Jahan Ripa. Currently writing the third book 'Anandbari Abbu'. His affinity with writing, however, is older. In 2014, a well-known reciter of the country lent his voice to a recitation and music video based on his poetry. His writings have also been published in various newspapers of the country.

Not only writing, he has been associated with editing and publishing for a long time. He has been in charge of editorial and management of Bengali periodical Ektara based in Orlando, Florida, USA for more than a decade. Apart from this, he is also working as an associate editor of 'Bangla Times 360'.

The Story of Career Change and Entrepreneurship
To understand Jahan Rima's story of becoming an entrepreneur, one has to go back to his education. After completing his graduation in political science in Bangladesh, he completely changed the course of his life. He came to the United States and enrolled in health science and dental hygiene. After a long six-seven years of intensive studies, he started working in an emergency dental clinic.

After that he entered the world of business with his artistic sense and creativity. But he did not confine business to mere profit and loss figures; He has engaged himself as a full-time artist.

About five years ago, his organization Jahn's Artistic World started with a kiosk in one of the largest and busiest shopping malls in Florida, 'The Florida Mall'. There he started offering henna, body art, portrait, caricature and painting services.

Building your own art range in the busy shopping malls of America was not easy. Reminiscing, Jahan Rima said, 'I was very scared at the beginning. If not move? Why did no one before me? On top of that was high rents and uncertainty. At that time, a quote from American futurist Jack Wooldrich gave him courage, "Whatever thrills and scares you at the same time, maybe you should do it."

Although the decision was difficult, he quit his job as an artist at Universal Studios. He also turned down an offer to join Disney World's Morocco Pavilion. Leaving the safe path of returning to dental practice, he focused entirely on the platform he had created. Currently, in addition to 'The Florida Mall', the high-end shopping mall 'Orlando Vineland Premium Outlets' has opened a new branch. He also has a laundromat business at Orlando International Airport (MCO).
"Are you successful?" Jahan Rima laughed and said, "I will not say that I am successful." There is still a long way to go; But I am successful in my own way. Success to me means happiness. If you are happy where you are, then you are successful.'

Jahan Rima also said, 'I am happy to think that we have been able to bring the traditional mehndi art of Bangladesh to the malls of America. We are conveying our tradition of joyous celebration to people of different cultures from different parts of the world.

Apart from henna, Jahan Rima regularly works in oil colour, water colour, one-line art and mixed media. Minimalist one-line art likes to tell a story in a single continuous line. Eager to learn something new, recently learning caricature from a master. Family is the main strength of his long journey. They called their house 'Anandbari'. Jahan Rima said with a smile, 'They are my powerhouse.' Recently, Orlando Voyager Magazine's 'Hidden Gem' series has also published an article about her.

Taking the change positively, Jahan said, 'The whole world now feels like my studio.'

Sandpiper to Florida—the distance is not only a thousand miles; It involves time, change, new language, new culture and the courage to reinvent yourself again and again. However, even in the crowd of so many acquaintances, he did not forget the pull of the pulse.
