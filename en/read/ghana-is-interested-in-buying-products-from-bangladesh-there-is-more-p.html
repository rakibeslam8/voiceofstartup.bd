Ghana keen to import from Bangladesh, sees big potential in six products including medicine and garments

By নিজস্ব প্রতিবেদক18 November 2026 at 10:37 pm2 min read

West African country Ghana is interested in expanding trade with Bangladesh. High Commissioner of Ghana, Kwasi Abiri-Danso, believes that there is great potential for medicines, ready-made clothes, ceramics, plastics, jute and jute products produced in Bangladesh in the country's market.

This afternoon, the administrator of FBCCI, the top organization of businessmen. Ghana's High Commissioner Kwasi Abiri-Danso said this while meeting with Fazlul Haque. This was stated in a press release of FBCCI.

Note that Ghana is one of the exporters of gold and cocoa beans in the world market.

In the meeting, Ghana's high commissioner said that currently the trade between the two countries is only 35 to 40 million dollars, which is very insignificant. He highlighted the need for regular communication and mutual cooperation between the businessmen of both the countries to increase bilateral trade and expand investment.

The High Commissioner said the office of the African Continental Free Trade Area is located in Ghana. Therefore, by increasing trade and investment with Ghana, the opportunity of easy access of Bangladeshi products to the large market of the entire African continent can be expanded, he said.

In order to strengthen Bangladesh-Ghana trade relations, FBCCI administrator emphasized on regular information exchange, direct and online meetings and institutional communication between the top trade organizations of both countries to find specific commercial partners. Fazlul Haque

Fazlul Haque said, Bangladesh has a huge potential to export garments, medicines, leather products, ceramics, plastics and processed food products to Ghana.

Secretary General of FBCCI also attended the meeting. Alamgir, Ghana Minister-Counsellor (Political and Economic) Sakrata Lamini Idrisu, Minister Counselor Muniru Kadiri, Honorary Consular Special Assistant Arpo Khan.
