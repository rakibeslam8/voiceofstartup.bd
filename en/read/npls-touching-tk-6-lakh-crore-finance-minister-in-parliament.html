NPLs touching Tk 6 lakh crore: Finance Minister in Parliament

By VOSB Desk16 November 2026 at 7:42 pm1 min read

The amount of defaulted loans in the country's banking sector has increased to more than 5 lakh 97 thousand 154 crores, which is 32.17 percent of the total loans disbursed by banks.

On the sixth day of the fourth session of the Jatiya Sangsad on Monday (November 16) during the question and answer session, Sirajganj-4 Member of Parliament Md. Finance Minister Amir Khusru Mahmud Chowdhury gave this information in response to Rafiqul Islam Khan's written question.

According to the information given to the Parliament, the total amount of defaulted loans in the banking sector in 2022 was Tk 1 lakh 12 thousand 901 crore 82 lakh, which is 7.64 percent against the total loan disbursed at that time. The next year in 2023, this rate increased to 8.39 percent. In 2024, it reaches 17.83 percent. Last year, in 2025, against the loans given by banks, the rate of defaulted loans was 29.92 percent.

And at the end of September 2026, the total amount of loans of the banks is 18 lakh 56 thousand 169 crore 17 lakh taka; Out of this, the defaulted loan is Tk 5 lakh 97 thousand 154 crore 93 lakh, which is 32.17 percent of the total loan.

Finance Minister Amir Khosru Mahmud Chowdhury said regarding the action plan for recovery of defaulted loans, a time-bound action plan is being implemented to establish good governance in the banking sector, recover loans from big defaulters and prevent future loan fraud.
