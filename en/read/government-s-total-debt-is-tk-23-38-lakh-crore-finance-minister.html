Government's total debt is Tk 23.38 lakh crore: Finance Minister

By স্টার অনলাইন রিপোর্ট20 November 2026 at 2:00 pm2 min read

Finance Minister Amir Khosru Mahmud Chowdhury said that as of June 30, the total government debt of Bangladesh is about Tk 23.38 lakh crore. Today, Monday, he gave this information during the question and answer session in the National Parliament.

Member of Parliament of BNP. In response to a question from Mostafizur Rahman Babul, the finance minister said that among these loans, the foreign loan is Tk 10 lakh 325 crores. And the internal debt is 13 lakh 37 thousand Tk 250 crore. He said that according to the specification law, 8 lakh 8 thousand Tk 778 crore has been allocated for debt repayment including principal and interest payment in the current financial year.

In response to a question from BNP Member of Parliament Farida Yasmin, the Finance Minister said that the current government has repaid foreign debt of 2.95 billion dollars during the period from March to September 2026. He said that it is being ensured that the foreign-funded projects are in line with the goal of implementing the government's election manifesto in addition to restructuring and restoring the fragile economy. Special caution is being exercised in taking foreign loans with high interest rates and rigid short-term terms.

In response to a question from NCP member of parliament Hasnat Abdullah, Amir Khosru Mahmud Chowdhury said that to strengthen the country's economy, the government is committed to ensure the implementation of development projects and the effective use of foreign project assistance. One of the main reasons for this decline is the country's prolonged internal unrest for nearly two years, coupled with adverse global conditions. Due to these reasons, project implementation has been slow, resulting in reduced foreign aid concessions.

The minister said that to deal with this situation and increase aid concessions, the government has taken various steps including meeting with bilateral and multilateral development partners, urging all concerned parties to speed up project implementation and building a framework for regular data collection and monitoring of the implementation progress of project-related measures.
