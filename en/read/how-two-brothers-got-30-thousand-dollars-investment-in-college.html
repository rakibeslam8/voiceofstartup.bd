How two brothers got 30 thousand dollars investment in college

By VOSB Desk18 October 2026 at 1:36 pm5 min read

Pithapithi two brothers - Shafi bin Sultan and Sabiq bin Sultan. In their childhood, they did not have any toy cars or 'Aast'. Both of them used to open the hinges of the car and try to put them together again.

The story seems to start from there. Two brothers are now in college. He has already set up a startup called 'Agentichire'. The main task of this initiative is to enable employers to perform initial screening of candidates through AI technology. The two brothers also confirmed an investment of about 30 thousand dollars for the startup. Their father is Sultan Mohiuddin Bhuiyan and mother Rokhshana Afroz.

Motivation found in Corona

The work of two brothers with robotics started during the Corona period. The father was in 'isolation' in a separate room due to corona. The two brothers then built a 'line-following' robot to deliver food to their father. Two brothers who were schoolboys of that time put black tape on the floor from the kitchen to the father's room. Infrared sensors would guide the robot. Following the black tape, he would reach the destination with the food.

The two brothers started learning programming languages ​​such as C++, Java and web development by visiting YouTube and GitHub during the quarantine period of Corona. They also started making projects together with others. Formed 'Team Akash Pathao'. They have also participated in science festivals, technology fairs and robotics events in Bangladesh. He has gradually reached the international level.

International Stage

In 2025, Shafi and Sabiq participated in a competition called Diamond Challenge. Horn Entrepreneurship of University of Delaware, USA organizes this competition, students of schools and colleges participate. Their project of the service was called 'BlueShield Filter'. The two brothers made it to the top five in the competition by working on a concept for purifying water with carbon nanoparticles.

Next destination is Singapore. Shafi and Sabiq were invited to a youth fellowship associated with the National University of Singapore (NUS). It was a 10-day event with 200 students of higher secondary level. It was there that the two brothers' interest in leadership, innovation and markets was born. Meet people of various levels.

The Birth of AgenticHire

In December 2025, they joined the Founder Accelerator Program of the Youth Founder Summit (YFS) in Singapore. Various activities including bootcamp will last for five days. More than 70 youths from 11 Asian countries participated in it. Hosted by WaveSparks, Singapore Management University's Institute of Innovation and Entrepreneurship and BeyondX Lab. Assisted by the National Youth Council of Singapore.

The main goal of this event was for the youth to get to know each other, find a solution to a problem together, and organize a startup accordingly. And this is where AgenticHire was born.

Bootcamp doesn't end there. The six-month program was quite long. Agentichire had to be made a reality with a team of seven people from different countries. Seed funding also had to be raised. Two brothers confirmed the investment of 30 thousand dollars. Many organizations like Prime Bank, Bank Asia, Guardian Life Insurance, Sportify, Wavesparks and National Youth Council of Singapore have come forward. Other members of the team are also trying to bring in investments of their own.

Agentichire has made it to the top 3 in the event in Singapore after going through various stages. They have also participated in various events related to technology and business in Shanghai, Bangkok and Hong Kong.

Agenthire Key

Many startups don't have a dedicated recruiting team. It can be seen that the founder has to go through the CVs one by one, call or e-mail the candidates. Agentichire is trying to solve this problem. Artificial intelligence will do the primary job of candidate selection here. Not only will they review CVs, but they will also conduct interviews. Then create a written summary about each candidate. But the final hiring decision will be in the hands of the people – this startup ensures that too. Any organization can become a customer of AgenticHire with subscription.

Sabiq Bin Sultan is currently the CEO and co-founder of AgenticHire. Another co-founder and COO is Shafi Bin Sultan. Apart from this, there are Hwang Woo (Co-Founder and CTO) from Singapore, Dania Tokhanova (Head of Marketing) from Kazakhstan, Apuit Pasuwat (Head of Outreach) from Thailand, Moka Sokha (Operations Associate) from Cambodia and Jessie Chu (Product Associate) from Malaysia.

eyes on the future

CEO Sabiq Bin Sultan is currently studying in Class XI of BAF Shaheen College Kurmitola. On the other hand, COO Shafi Bin Sultan is a 12th standard student of St. Joseph Higher Secondary School. How do you juggle studies, startups, and so much? When asked, Sabik said, 'To be honest, I am handling everything perfectly, it would be wrong to say. But yes, as each member of our team is from a different country, even if one is not there, the rest are handling it well.'

Where do you want to see Agentichire in five years? In response to the question, Shafi said, 'We want AgenticHire to make a positive impact around the world. Especially for small businesses and early-stage startups that don't have their own HR team. We also want people to remember where it all started—with the hands of two students from Bangladesh, who decided to build something together with partners from other countries through an accelerator program.'

The two brothers said that they want to study in a world-class university in the future. Business will continue as well. According to them, entrepreneurship can be learned through a combination of study and practical experience.

The team is planning a global launch of AgenticHire in January 2027. Until then, the focus of entrepreneurs is now on improving the quality of the product and reaching potential users. An alumnus of Harvard is guiding them as a mentor throughout this journey.
