Dolly is a successful entrepreneur who quit her job in a foreign company

By VOSB Desk9 November 2026 at 0:57 pm7 min read

Kya Chin Thay (Dolly) left the post of country director of the world famous American company MGF Sourcing and started the buying house business. The time is November 2019. A few workers are also appointed. After that, he traveled Europe-America with luggage in the hope of purchase. It was hoped that the purchase order would be met and the business would turn. But what, Corona came and turned everything upside down.

In March 2020, when the outbreak of Corona increased, Dolly was forced to close the office by sending the employees home. Salaries and office rent were given to employees with the money saved. The office opened after three and a half months. Still not getting any big orders. 9 months passed like this. But did not lose heart. He gave himself the courage. At one point, he got a really big purchase order. The next story is just to move on.

Entrepreneur Dolly's Buying House-Cloth 'R' Us Ltd. currently works with European buyers. The number of employees in the organization is now 12. Dolly is also planning to expand the business soon.

Dolly, a girl from the Rakhine community of Chakria in Cox's Bazar, worked in several world-renowned brands and consumer organizations for two decades before becoming an entrepreneur. He continued his studies as well. He left the family and worked abroad. This is how he developed himself little by little.

Had a long talk with Dolly last Thursday at Cloth 'R' As office in Uttara. During the conversation, he said that his ancestors lived in Chakria, Cox's Bazar, but he was born in Chittagong. Baba Mong Khezari's screen printing and dyeing business was in Chittagong's Dewanbazar. Dolly is the second of six siblings. Mother Aikya Yin was a housewife.

Dolly was admitted to St. Scholastica Girls High School in Patharghata, Chittagong. Everything was going well. Dolly's father's business continues to deteriorate due to lack of modern machinery. At one stage Mong Khezari closed the business and sent the family home to the village. And he came to Dhaka and took a job.

Returning to the village, Dolly enrolled in Harbang Union High School in Chakaria in eighth grade. SSC exam from here. She was admitted to Chittagong Government Women's College in high school. He studied from the hostel. He came to Dhaka after passing HSC. His father advised Dolly to finish her studies quickly and take care of the family. He was sick then. So without honors, Dolly was admitted to BK in Tejgaon College.

In 1997, after completing BCom, he joined the world-famous sourcing company Li&Fang. Dolly said, 'I was offered the position of secretary and assistant merchandiser to the company's country manager. 15,000 for the post of secretary, and 8,000 for the post of merchandiser. Thinking about the future, I interviewed for the position of merchandiser. Being fairly good at English, I got a job opportunity. It is said from the company, if the qualification is not shown within six months, it will be removed. I took the challenge.

After that, Dolly quickly asserted his position. Promotion started. He worked like this for 13 years. At that time he was the Senior Merchandiser Manager of Li&Fang. He continued his studies along with his job. First did LLB. After that he did M.Com from Dhaka College and MBA from Victoria University of Technology.

Dolly lost her father two years after starting work at Liandfang. Then sometime elder sister also died of brain stroke. The entire responsibility of the family fell on Dolly and her brother. Together they continued the education of three younger siblings.

After becoming a senior merchandiser manager at Ling&Fang, Dolly had some career thoughts. Because it is difficult to get promoted there. He got married that day. Two sons have also been born. Finally, he left Ling&Fang and joined the Dhaka office of United States PVH Corporation as a Divisional Merchandiser. Dolly said, "While working at Leandfang, I used to look after the business of the European brand MS Mode. A few months after starting work at PVH, MS called me from Mod. They want to have an office in Dhaka. Offered me to join as Country Head. I started working as a country manager of MS Mode in 2013.

While working here, the idea of becoming an entrepreneur began to emerge for Dolly. He thought to himself, for this it is better to have work experience abroad. So Dolly started looking for a job abroad.

At one stage he got an offer from the US company MGF Sourcing. Post Divisional Manager. Must work in Jakarta, Indonesia. Dolly joined MGF Sourcing in Jakarta in 2015. Dolly used to look after the business of a brand called Express in Jakarta. At one point, he saw that the price at which Express was buying denim pants and shirts, could be produced in Bangladesh at a much lower price. Thoughtfully, he offered the company to transfer the express purchase order to Bangladesh. MGF also agreed.

After working in Jakarta for a year and a half, Dolly came to Dhaka as the Country Director of MGF Sourcing. He brought with him a business worth two million dollars. In the meantime, his mind slowly started to rise from the job. He felt the urge to do something himself. He left the job in May 2019. Started buying house business. But don't give up on it in the beginning. Dolly was in dire straits. No purchase order. Workers have to be paid in lump sum. Then in March 2021, he got his first big purchase order.

About the events of that day, Dolly said, 'My mother died. Preparations are underway to take mother home for the funeral. At that time a call came from Europe. A friend of mine has taken a job in a new company. He said they were having trouble sourcing clothes. Can I help? When he finished I said, my mother is dead. I will contact you after a week.'

Dolly said, 'We got the job of making one lakh pieces of leggings from a European brand. That was our first big purchase order. After that we didn't have any more problems.

Dolly is already recognized as a successful entrepreneur. He received the DHL-Daily Star Bangladesh Business Award last month. Outside of entrepreneurship, Dolly is a runner. Participated in marathons regularly. He also writes.

Buying House mainly acts as a link between foreign brands and domestic factories. দেশে এক হাজারের মতো বায়িং হাউস আছে। When asked whether there are business opportunities for newcomers in this sector, Dolly said, there are about 2500 garment factories in the country. তাদের মধ্যে ৩০০-৪০০ কারখানার মার্কেটিং টিম আছে। বাকিদের কিন্তু নেই। বায়িং হাউসগুলোই তাদের জন্য ব্যবসা নিয়ে আসে। ফলে বায়িং হাউসের ব্যবসার সুযোগ আছে।

When asked what is the advice for those who are interested in buying house business, Dolly said, you have to know your job well. Must have skills. You have to accept that help will not come from anywhere else.

At the end of the conversation, Dolly had a question—a job or a business, which is better. হাসতে হাসতে তিনি বলেন, ‘অবশ্যই ব্যবসা। কারণ, স্বাধীনতা আছে। আবার নিজের সৃজনশীলতা কাজে লাগানোর সুযোগও রয়েছে।’
