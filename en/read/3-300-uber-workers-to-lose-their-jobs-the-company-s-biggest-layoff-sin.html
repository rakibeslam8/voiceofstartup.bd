3,300 Uber workers to lose their jobs: The company's biggest layoff since the coronavirus

By চাকরি-বাকরি প্রতিবেদক19 November 2026 at 4:21 pm2 min read

Ride-sharing service Uber has announced corporate layoffs of 10 percent of its global workforce, or about 3,300 workers. The US company started the biggest layoff since 2020 with the aim of simplifying management, increasing cost efficiency and investing heavily in future technologies.

Uber's Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Khosrowshahi announced this decision in a letter sent to employees last Wednesday. He said these changes are being made to make Uber a simpler and more dynamic company. The affected employees have already been informed. However, in countries where there are separate local labor laws, that rule will be followed.

The company has grown rapidly and achieved business success in the last five years. However, due to this growth, administrative complexity and number of layers within the organization increased. Basically, the management is being arranged to reduce this additional level. Smaller teams are being consolidated and micro-teams under officers are being reduced by about 50 percent.

At the same time, Uber sent a message to the workers to return to the office. From now on, 99 percent of the workers will have to come directly to the office to work. Only less than 1 percent of employees will get the opportunity to work remotely. The rule of coming to work at least three days a week should be followed from now on.

Along with layoffs, Uber plans to invest heavily in new technology. The company has decided to spend 1000 crores (10 billion) dollars to develop a driverless autonomous vehicle called 'Robotaxis'. Uber has already partnered with Wave, an autonomous car maker in the UK.

On the other hand, Uber Eats has taken big steps to dominate the global food delivery market. Recently, they have signed an agreement to buy the German company 'Delivery Hero' for 1,480 million dollars. This investment is to compete with rival apps like DoorDash.

Uber started its journey from San Francisco in 2010. Currently, the company's market value worldwide is more than 15 thousand crores (150 billion). The company has not seen such a large layoff since the Corona pandemic. In 2020, 6,700 workers lost their jobs due to the severe impact of the pandemic.
