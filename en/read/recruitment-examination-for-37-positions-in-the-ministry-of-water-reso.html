Recruitment examination for 37 positions in the Ministry of Water Resources has been suspended

By নিজস্ব প্রতিবেদক19 November 2026 at 8:35 pm1 min read

The previously scheduled written examination for the recruitment to 37 vacant positions in the Ministry of Water Resources has been postponed. The exam was scheduled to be held tomorrow, November 20.

This information was announced in a notice published on the website of the Ministry of Water Resources today, Thursday (November 19).

The notice states that the written examinations for four positions in grades 13 to 20 under the revenue sector of the ministry have been postponed due to unavoidable reasons.The positions are Computer Operator, Stenographer-cum-Computer Operator, Office Assistant-cum-Computer Typist, and Office Assistant.

The notice states that the new date and time of the postponed exams will be announced later. In addition to publishing a notice on the website of the Ministry of Water Resources, the candidates will be informed through text messages (SMS) on their mobile phones.
