Freelancing for wheelchair users, income above lakhs of rupees

By VOSB Desk23 October 2026 at 8:50 pm4 min read

Year 2017 A young man was sitting on the back of a motorcycle in the magical afternoon light. A terrible accident of the moment. That day, which became dark all around, turned the familiar rhythm of Abdul Bached's life upside down. Both legs were paralyzed due to spinal cord injury. A wheelchair became a permanent companion to the nimble legs on which he dreamed of traveling the world.

As the saying goes, 'One accident is the cry of a lifetime.' But Bached erased that cry and started writing a new dictionary of patience and hard work. Today, those wheelchair wheels are no longer a symbol of limitation, rather they are now the wings of success for the chosen ones. Today, Abdul Bached's monthly income from freelancing is one lakh to one and a half lakh taka.

Start of the fight and the power of roots

Bacheh's dream journey began in 2010 after finishing primary school at Atatürk Model Government Primary School in Daganbhuyan, Noakhali. After passing SSC in science department from the same school in 2015, a colorful future was in sight. But in 2018, before the HSC exam from Chaumuhani Government SA College, the accident struck. A year of life stopped in a hospital bed, but Bached did not give up. He overcame his physical limitations and completed his Bachelor's (BSS) degree from Feni Government College in 2023.

Bacheh's greatest strength in this long struggle was his family. Father Sahab Uddin has been in exile for a long time and worked hard for the golden future of his child. মা সেতারা আক্তার ছায়া দিয়েছিলেন বাছেদকে। Elder brother Imran Hussain and younger brother Abu Zafar—both currently working in Saudi Arabia. They were also dedicated to fulfilling the dreams of Mej Bhai Bachde. And younger sister Heba Binte Sahab, who is now studying in the first year of Bachelors (Honours), Bached is also an inspiration to her.

অন্ধকার গলি ও বাস্তবতার দেয়ালWhen he entered the job market in a wheelchair after graduation, Bached saw the harsh reality. Most offices are not accessible to people like him. Even for a job with a salary of 10-15 thousand rupees, he had to fight with infrastructural obstacles. Bached realized that he had to do something out of the ordinary if he wanted to bring Akash into his hands without locking himself inside the walls.গেম থেকে স্বপ্ন দেখা

Bached begged his mother and elder brother for a computer. Got a hobby computer. শুরুতে সারা দিন গেম খেলে সময় কাটত তাঁর। But one day, the sting of conscience suddenly began - will life end like this?

He jumped into the vast sea of ​​the internet after receiving his basic training from Chittagong's Center for Rehabilitation of Paralytics (CRP). Struggled with Shopify, WordPress in the beginning but couldn't get it right. But who is not in the blood to give up, who stops him? Through a friend and elder brother, he got acquainted with Google Ads and Google Merchant Center. At the end of 2021, his real battle preparation started at the hands of Shamim Hussain, the mentor of Skillupper.

Proud Remittance – Warrior

It was not too late to reap the fruits of hard work. Bached started showing his skills on outsourcing job giving and taking websites FiveR and Upwork. By 2025, he took himself to unique heights. After learning conversion tracking and advanced Google Ads, he started offering new services to his old customers.

Today Abdul Bached's monthly income is one lakh to one and a half lakh taka. He is not just a freelancer, he is a 'Certified Professional' and a proud 'Remittance-Warrior'. He is contributing to the country's economy by sitting in his own house, paying taxes regularly. He now has a government remittance certificate in his hand, which is an acknowledgment of his every sweat and sleepless night.

Debt of Love

Abdul Bached told Prothom Alo, 'I am very happy with my life. I have no complaints about life. I am living with my head held high in my merits. In this long struggle, the love of family and friends was the oxygen of his survival. Especially mentor Shamim Hussain's guidance showed him the path of light. Abdul Bached is a living inspiration for our society today. He taught that even if the body stops, if the will is strong, then the wheel of fortune is bound to turn.
