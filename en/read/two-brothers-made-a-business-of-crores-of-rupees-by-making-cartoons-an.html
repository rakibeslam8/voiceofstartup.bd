Two brothers made a business of crores of rupees by making cartoons and memes on Facebook

By VOSB Desk5 November 2026 at 2:50 pm5 min read

Najmus Shahadat started thinking of martyrdom soon after his admission in Jagannath University, he has to raise the expenses himself. The elder brother Nazmus Saqib was then a student of International Islamic University of Chittagong. The two brothers started looking for a way of income. Their house is in Laksam, Comilla.

Shahadat is a student of mass communication and journalism. So he wanted to build a career in this field at the beginning. Worked for some time. But the mind did not last. After that, the two brothers slowly started to venture into online business.

First, Shahadat used to open a channel on YouTube and create a 'slide show' with pictures of various subjects. It did not get much response. Later came up with ideas about 'Facebook Bonus' (income from meta through reels, images and writing), content monetization, etc. At that time they had no plan to sell products online. Rather, the two brothers were looking for what kind of content to create that would get netizens' attention.

At this time, artificial intelligence or AI tools started to become popular in the country. Shahadat used these tools to make cartoonish pictures. The two brothers plan to create memes by mixing funny words with cartoons. Apart from publishing those memes on their Facebook page, they started sharing them in different groups. Gradually some posts become viral. New followers are added to the page. They also formed their own group.

This is how they gradually build their own audience. From the data provided by Facebook (Facebook Insight), the two brothers found out that the majority of their content's "viewers" are US residents, especially the middle-aged and elderly. They prefer jokes made about age, retirement and boredom of everyday life. This popularity of the page once showed the two brothers the possibility of a new business.

Product from Mim
Shahadat and Shakib once thought that what would happen if Mim's funny words were printed on T-shirts and sold. At that time cousin Mohammad Hasan Rafi joined their plan. He knows the work of design well. Well, the journey of making T-shirts begins.

They did not keep the responsibility of making and supplying T-shirts in their own hands. The foreigner has entered into an understanding with a company. Shahadatara only collects orders by presenting their designs on Facebook and website, the company does the work from making the t-shirts to delivering them to the customers.

Shahadat said, "In the beginning, we tried to increase the audience of the page and group without any income for a few months. I didn't want to spend money on advertising. Rather, sharing memes in different groups, creating good content and reaching more people, building a community of our own - these were our focus. Gradually, the results of this effort began to come. Viewers of memes become potential buyers of our products. We started getting quite a response to this plan soon.'

Although it started with t-shirts, now their product list is quite large. They sell sweatshirts, hoodies, and even tumblers (a kind of water flask). Mim's familiar jokes and dialogues are prominent in the design of each product. To sell these products, they have developed their own website - URprintz.com targeting the US market.

Sales increase around the festival
Shahadat said that from October to February is the important time of their business. At this time, they made special design products focusing on festivals like Halloween and Christmas. These designs made in conjunction with the festival received a good response.

The use of various AI tools in product design and content creation has made their work easier. Shahadat said, "With the improvement of these tools with time, it is possible to create quality designs in less time. As a result, apart from publishing regular content, the opportunity to increase the variety of products has also been created.

But technology has not only played a role in this journey. Understanding the age and preferences of the audience, creating regular content and linking products with that content has also played an important role in business expansion.

crores from mim page
Shahadat said that currently their various Facebook pages and groups have more than 4 million followers and members. He claims that in 2024, they have earned about one crore rupees in business in one year. The business has also received recognition from international e-commerce platform Shopbase for its good sales and website popularity. Currently, more than 15 people are getting an opportunity to earn by being associated with this initiative.

Shahadat and Shakib are now earning lakhs of taka per month by selling products in foreign markets from home, starting from the urge to provide living expenses while studying in the university. However, their journey was not always smooth. Due to changes in Facebook's policies and algorithms, it has faced various challenges at times. The two brothers had to do extensive research on content types, audience preferences and algorithm changes. To reduce such risks in the future, they do not want to depend only on Facebook, but also want to expand their business on other platforms.

Co-founder Nazmus Saqib said, "In the future, we want to create viewers and buyers on platforms like Instagram and Pinterest, not just relying on Facebook. Besides, there are plans to strengthen our website, so that customers can buy directly from there and come back to us again and again. They are also thinking of expanding the product list. There are plans to add new products like mugs, caps and bags. They're also thinking about how to leverage new AI tools to design and create content.
