In EPZ, 13 thousand 530 to 16 thousand Tk 605 are offered to the investors

By নিজস্ব প্রতিবেদক19 November 2026 at 4:02 pm4 min read

Investors have proposed fixing separate minimum wage structures for workers depending on the location of the country's Export Processing Zones (EPZs). Although currently all EPZs have the same wage structure.

EPZ investors have proposed a minimum monthly wage of Tk 16,605 for workers in three EPZs in major cities like Dhaka and Chattogram. 15,250 for two EPZs in medium cities like Cumilla and Narayanganj and 13,530 for workers in other proposed EPZs, including three located in rural and relatively low cost areas. In this case, apart from basic wages, food, medical and travel allowances have been kept the same and different house rents have been proposed.

Bangladesh EPZ Investors Association (BEPZA) President Shahdat Musharraf Khan submitted the new wage proposal to the Executive Chairman of Bangladesh Export Processing Zone Authority (BEPZA) last week.

Confirming the matter, Tapan Kumar Majumdar, director of Bepzia's Dhaka region, told Prothom Alo, "We were asked for a wage proposal from Bepzia. We have offered wages for that. Now Bepza will take the final decision.

Bepzia demanded the same wage structure inside and outside the EPZ. Apart from this, the annual wage increase has been demanded to be 5 percent instead of 9 percent. They said, due to the increase in wages at the rate of 9 percent, the operating expenses of the industries are increasing. Besides discouraging new investment, long-term sustainable management of industries is also hindered.

It is known that after the finalization of the wages of the garment industry, Bepza finalized the minimum wage in consultation with the investors of EPZ. In December 2023, the minimum wage of EPZ was fixed at Tk 12,800. Out of this, the basic salary is 6 thousand Tk 950. Currently, the Wage Board is working on re-fixing the wages of the garment industry. Only after that, the minimum wage of EPZ workers will be finalised.

In the wage board formed for the redetermination of wages in the garment industry, the representative of the workers has proposed a wage of Tk 32,000. The owner has not yet offered wages. Meanwhile, yesterday, the private research institute CPD gave an opinion that the wage negotiation of garment workers should be between Tk 18,000 and Tk 24,000.

What's in the Salary Offer

Bepzia said in its wage proposal, there is a significant difference in the cost of living between big cities like Dhaka and Chattogram and other industrial and rural areas of the country. Daily necessities including housing, food, transportation are not the same everywhere. As a result, instead of fixing the same minimum wage in all the EPZs of the country, separate wage structures can be made region wise.

For this, the organization has proposed to divide the country into three regions. The first region or 'A' class will have big cities like Dhaka and Chattogram and high cost industrial areas. The second zone or class 'B' will have medium cost industrial areas. And the third region or 'C' class will have rural and relatively low cost industrial areas.

Bepzia has proposed a monthly minimum wage of Tk 16,605 in Dhaka, Chattogram and Karnaphuli EPZs. In case of Adamji and Cumilla EPZ it will be Tk 15 thousand 250. And in Uttara, Mongla and Ishwardi EPZs, the minimum wage will be Tk 13,530. The organization also proposed to bring planned Jashore, Patuakhali and Rangpur EPZs and Bepza economic zones under this wage structure.

If the proposed wage structure is implemented, the minimum monthly wages of workers in the second region will be lower by Tk 1,355 compared to the first region. And the workers of the third zone will get 3 thousand Tk 75 less wages than the first zone.

According to Bepzia, countries including India, Pakistan, China, Vietnam, Indonesia, Philippines follow different wage structure based on geographical location and cost of living.

HOW ACCEPTABLE OFFER

Babul Akhter, the representative of the labor side in the minimum wage board, told \IProtham Alo\I, "We have proposed a minimum wage of Tk 32,000 for the workers outside the EPZ, but the workers in the EPZ factories should be paid more than that." Because, EPZ factories get higher price of products. They also get benefits from the government. As a result, it seems that the wage offered by them will not be accepted by the common workers.

Babul Akhter also said that if any process of determining the wages of EPZ is taken before the final decision of the wage board, the situation there can also be tense.
