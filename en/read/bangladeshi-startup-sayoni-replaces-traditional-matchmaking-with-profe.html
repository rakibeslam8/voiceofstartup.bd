Bangladeshi startup 'Sayoni' replaces traditional matchmaking with professional matchmaking

By VOSB Desk27 November 2026 at 10:43 pm4 min read

Even today, about two-thirds of people in educated and sophisticated families in Bangladesh rely on family or 'arranged marriage' for very important life decisions like marriage. Once upon a time in the district or divisional towns, relatives, neighbors or neighborhood elders would selflessly look for suitable bridegrooms. But with urbanization, busy lifestyles and migration, that once-familiar circle of social contact has shrunk considerably. On the other hand, many families are facing bitter experience due to unprofessional behavior of traditional ghatak or marriage media, exorbitant registration fees and false promises.
Curated Matchmaking Consultancy Firm 'Saiyonee' started in 2023 to fill this void and bring the matchmaking sector into a disciplined, technological and professional structure. The company is working to find life partner with privacy, compatibility and professionalism for educated, elegant and modern Bangladeshi Muslim families spread across the country and abroad.

The story of leaving a corporate career to become a social entrepreneur
Tanveer Rahman is the mastermind behind Sayoni. A dual citizen of Bangladesh and Canada, Tanveer completed his BBA from Dhaka University and MBA from the University of British Columbia (UBC) in Canada. He has more than a decade of system building and corporate management experience at world-renowned organizations such as TD Bank Canada, GlaxoSmithKline and Lululemon Athletica.

Returning home in 2022 with the goal of building a social enterprise, he noted that the matchmaking sector in Bangladesh is largely based on unorganized factors, paper-based resumes and unprofessional commitment. To eliminate the clutter of common matrimonial websites and people's lack of trust in traditional matchmakers, he founded Sayoni—where marriage matchmaking is not just an intermediary, but a full-fledged professional consultancy service.
Not a traditional Ghatkali, this is a curated consultancy service

The matchmaking service at Saioni is operated as a 'Consultancy Service' with a duration of 1 year. The company's modus operandi is completely different from conventional marriage media:

  • Strict Screening & 20-30% Approval Rate: Not all profiles are approved just by applying to Sayoni. Each candidate's biodata and needs are sent directly to the organization's CEO, and only 20 to 30 percent of profiles are finally approved. Three factors are strictly considered for approval—the educational and family standing of the applicant and family, financial ability to avail the services and realistic needs in selecting a life partner.
  • 100% Authenticity and Confidentiality of Information: Members are required to provide accurate biodata and sign a formal service agreement. Any wrong or untrue information is proved to result in immediate cancellation of membership. Besides, 5 recent unedited (artificial intelligence or AI-free) photos are also submitted to understand the true appearance and fitness of the candidate.
  • PERSONALIZED CONSULTANCY: After registration experienced consultants analyze candidate needs and send shortlisted profiles on weekly basis directly to contact person's WhatsApp, avoiding unnecessary crowding and maintaining maximum privacy.

Industry's first 'Refund Policy' with confidence and transparency

To break the long-standing culture of financial fraud and obfuscation in the matchmaking sector, Saioni has introduced a transparent fee structure and the industry's only 2-month refund policy.

One time registration fee in Sayoni is fixed at Tk 8,000 for boys and Tk 16,000 for girls. Success fee of Tk 50,000 is applicable only when the engagement, cabin or contract is completed through Sayoni. In addition, the company offers a refund guarantee if the desired quality of service is not met within the first two months after receiving the service—an exceptional example in the Bangladeshi matchmaking industry.

Combining Bengali family values ​​and professionalism with modern technology, Sayoni is not just a business but a mission to restore dignity and trust to the country's matchmaking sector.
