Rifa Tashnia took charge of Walton's electric car project

By নিজস্ব প্রতিবেদক19 November 2026 at 8:43 pm2 min read

Young entrepreneur Rifa Tashnia has been appointed by Walton Hitech Industries as the Monitoring Director of the Electric Vehicle (EV) project in the country. She is the younger sister of the late director of the company, Mahbub Alam.

Walton's initiative to manufacture electric cars in the country was Mahbub Alam's dream. This time his sister got the responsibility of supervising that project.

In the 69th meeting of the Executive Committee (EC) of Walton, it was decided to give this responsibility to Rifa Tashnia. His appointment was confirmed in a letter signed by the managing director of the company last Tuesday. Walton said this information in a press release on Wednesday.

According to the company, Rifa Tashnia is the youngest person to take charge of Walton's electric vehicle project. In his new role, he will oversee the overall operation of the project. Also give necessary advice and guidance to the officials involved in the project.

According to Walton, the company has undertaken the project of manufacturing electric vehicles as part of the expansion of the business into new sectors. Rifa Tashnia has been given the responsibility to carry out the activities of this project in a smooth and planned manner.

Walton Group's late director Mahbub Alam dreamed of manufacturing electric vehicles in the country. But he died in 2021. Rifa Tashnia has now got the responsibility of taking forward the unfinished initiative of her brother.

According to the press release, Rifa Tashnia has already been associated with various business ventures. He is the founder of the clothing brand Savata by Rifa. Apart from this, he is the director of Radmin Chemical Industries and one of the founders of Restaurant Kava in Bashundhara residential area of ​​the capital.

Walton authorities hope that under Rifa Tashnia's supervision and guidance, the implementation of the electric vehicle project will move forward.
