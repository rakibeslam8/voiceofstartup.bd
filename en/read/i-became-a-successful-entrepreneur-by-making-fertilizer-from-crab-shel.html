I became a successful entrepreneur by making fertilizer from crab shells

By VOSB Desk12 November 2026 at 9:12 am5 min read

This organic fertilizer called Magiccompost is being used in crop fields, rooftop gardens and shrimp farms. Fertilizer price is 30 rupees per kg. By selling this fertilizer, Aslam Hossain's monthly income is 30 to 50 thousand taka.

Tons of crab shells are dried and ground into powder using machines. It is known as Crab Shell Powder (CSP). This CSP is then mixed in specific proportions with decomposed (processed) vegetables. If left like this, a kind of fertilizer is formed within a few days.

A resident of Nawabenki area of ​​Shyamnagar in Satkhira is making this organic fertilizer rich in potassium called Magiccompost with crab shells. Aslam Hossain This fertilizer is used in field crop production, roof gardens and shrimp farms. Aslam Hossain earns 30,000 to 50,000 taka per month by selling magiccompost.

Crab farming has increased in the past few years in the salinity-prone coastal district of Satkhira. Many have left shrimp farming and turned to crab farming. Crab farming is increasing in Satkhira due to climatic tolerance, low susceptibility to diseases, simple cultivation methods and good commercial value.

With the increase in cultivation, new problems have arisen with crab shells. Sector stakeholders said that two types of crabs are cultivated commercially in the country: hard shell (hard shell) and soft shell (soft shell). However, export demand for soft shell crabs is high; As a result, production is also increasing. In the coastal areas of the country, about 350 farms are producing such crabs. Hard shells of crabs are mainly available in these farms.

There is little commercial use of crab shells within the country. Some of the shells are powdered and exported to China. However, for a long time, most of the shells were dumped in the form of waste in the garbage or rivers, which harmed the environment by spreading stench and pollution. But Aslam has turned this environmental challenge into an opportunity through a sustainable business solution for crab shells.

Palli Karma-Sahayak Foundation (PKSF) and World Bank have jointly given financial support to Aslam Hossain in this project of making organic fertilizer with crab shell powder. The support is provided under PKSF's Sustainable Enterprise Project (SEP). And Nawabenki Ganamukhi Foundation (NGF) supported him locally in this work.

how to start

There are several crab farms under SEP scheme of PKSF in Munshiganj and Atulia Union of Shyamnagar. Soft shell crabs are cultivated in these farms. As a result, a large number of shells are produced there. On the advice of PKSF, the officials of the local Ganamukhi Foundation took up the project of alternative commercial use of these shells.

The time is not long ago, about 2021. With necessary training and financial support from NGF, Aslam took the initiative to make fertilizer from crab shells. With a capital of one lakh taka, he built a separate place and pit (with four big tanks) with a tin canopy next to the house.

Aslam Hossain said that this fertilizer is made by mixing the crab shell powder with certain proportion of rotten vegetables. In some cases cow dung, earthworm manure, ash, neem leaves etc. are added to it. He collected dry crab shells from various local farms to make this fertilizer. Then these shells are ground with the help of machine. Usually 250 kg of shells yield 200 kg of powder.

On the other hand, vegetable waste is collected from local vegetable wholesale markets and households. These vegetables are kept in certain wells for 15 days and then crab shell powder is mixed with it. If left in this way for one and a half to two months, high quality Magicompost organic fertilizer is produced from it. They are then sun-dried into powder and marketed. Production cost per kg of Magicompost is Rs.12-15; And sold it for 30 taka.

Current Status

Aslam Hossain is able to produce this fertilizer throughout the year due to the supply of raw materials. He said that in the first three-four months of the start of production in the middle of 2021, one and a half thousand kg of magiccompost fertilizer was received. They are experimentally applied in rooftop gardens and agriculture under the supervision of Ganamukhi Foundation. This success was followed by experimental application of this fertilizer on two winter vegetables. Apart from this, the quality of this fertilizer has also been verified in the laboratory of Khulna University. At present this fertilizer is being sold through the local Atulia Environment Development Club.

Aslam Hossain said, he wants to sell this fertilizer commercially all over the country under his own brand name. For this, the approval process of Bangladesh Standards and Testing Institution (BSTI) is going on. He said that the demand for the use of Magicompost is increasing. At present, this fertilizer is being sold to the owners of 30 local farms for use in shrimp farming. Again, 20 farmers who are cultivating vegetables for commercial purposes are also using this fertilizer. Apart from this, many people are buying magiccompost fertilizer for their rooftop gardens. Many people are giving purchase orders remotely on social media Facebook.

Aslam Hussain's family business is shrimp farming. At present, he is cultivating shrimps in 7 acres of land. But the success of making magiccompost made him more interested in this sector. Aslam Hossain said, 'I want to produce crab shell fertilizer on a large scale. This requires the purchase of more infrastructure and equipment. If you get financing or investment through the bank, it will be possible to advance this initiative quickly.
