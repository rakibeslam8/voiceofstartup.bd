The central bank wants Tk 2700 crore to refund the money of customers of 9 financial institutions

By বিশেষ প্রতিনিধি20 November 2026 at 0:46 pm3 min read

Bangladesh Bank has asked the government for Tk 2,707 crore to refund the depositors of 9 non-banking financial institutions in crisis. The Central Bank has sent a letter to the Ministry of Finance on November 3 asking for this amount. Earlier, on October 27, the board of directors of the central bank decided to ask for this money to return the deposits of financial institutions.

The financial institutions that have been asked to refund their customers are International Leasing, Aviva Finance, FAS Finance, First Finance, People's Leasing, Prime Finance, GSP Finance, Bangladesh Industrial Finance Company (BIFC) and Premier Leasing. The depositors are not getting their money back in these institutions for a long time. Now Bangladesh Bank has asked for money from the government to return the money to the depositors.

Bangladesh Bank has informed the Ministry of Finance that among the 9 institutions, International Leasing, Aviva Finance, FAS Finance and First Finance will require about Tk 1500 crore on an urgent basis to return the money to the depositors. And for the remaining five institutions, about 1 thousand 257 crores will be required. In the letter, Bangladesh Bank said, under this initiative, 100% of deposits of institutions up to Tk 10 lakh will be returned.

According to Bangladesh Bank sources, among 9 financial institutions, the case related to termination of People's Leasing is ongoing. And International Leasing, Aviva Finance, FAS Finance and Forest Finance have been brought under the resolution. Bangladesh Bank has appointed administrators and assistant administrators in these institutions. Apart from this, the Central Bank has given three months time to Prime Finance, GSP Finance, BIFC and Premier Leasing. During this period, these institutions have to prove their business capability.

Bangladesh Bank has written to the Finance Department of the Ministry of Finance asking for money, but the government has not taken any decision in this regard so far. A top official of the finance department told Prothom Alo yesterday that banks are mainstream. Next in importance are financial institutions. However, bank depositors will get Tk 2 lakh back and financial institution investors will get Tk 10 lakh. It's unfair.

According to the calculation of Bangladesh Bank, 1 thousand Tk 167 crore will be required to be returned to the individual depositors of the four institutions which are in the process of resolution. Apart from this, about Tk 190 crore will be required for refund of provident fund and employee welfare fund, Tk 42 crore for trust fund and Tk 50 crore for cooperative fund. Among the four institutions, Aviva Finance will require the most money for depositors. 745 crore has been asked for the institution. International Leasing has sought Tk 550 crore, FAS Finance Tk 85 crore and Fareast Finance Tk 69 crore.

1 thousand 257 crores will be required to refund the five institutions that are out of the resolution process. Out of this, 712 crores will be required for People's Leasing. However, as the case related to winding up of People's Leasing is ongoing, funds are not being sought for the institution. Apart from this, among the remaining four institutions, Tk 377 crore will be required for Prime Finance, Tk 59 crore for GSP Finance, Tk 58 crore for Premier Leasing and Tk 52 crore for BIFC, according to Bangladesh Bank.

When asked, Bangladesh Bank's spokesperson and executive director Arif Hossain Khan told Prothom Alo yesterday, "Bangladesh Bank has informed the government about the situation of financial institutions, the plight of investors and what to do." The government will now take a decision on this matter.
