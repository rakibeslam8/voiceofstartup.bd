Two-day exhibition of women entrepreneurs in Gulshan ends

By VOSB Desk17 November 2026 at 6:39 am1 min read

The two-day 'Her Powerful Story, November Expo 2026' of women entrepreneurs has ended in Dhaka's Gulshan. The exhibition was organized at Suvarnabhumi Foundation's Islam Lodge in Gulshan-1 on November 14 and 15.

'Her Powerful Story' and 'Rehnuma CWE' jointly organized the fair. In this, women entrepreneurs from different areas of the capital display and sell their own brand products.

According to a press release sent out by the organizers, the fair had stalls of indigenous and modern design clothing, handmade jewellery, organic skin care products, home decor items, house plants and homemade food. The notification also mentions that there has been good response from buyers and visitors.

Companies participating in the fair included Linaz Jewellery, Amira's Creations, Shop Stop, Tafannum Collection, Shubhrata, Ranga, Manha's Garden, Fresh Purple, Girly Boutique, Bridges Creations, Raga by Anindita, Safa Food, Dazzling Attire Fashion House, Rani's Fashion, Nayira Fashion, Bidora and Sumi's. Collection.

On behalf of the organizers, Leena Ferdous, owner of Linaz Jewellery, said, 'Every woman has her own story, which can inspire other women. Many small entrepreneurs do not get the opportunity to participate in large-scale fairs. Therefore, we are organizing area-based fairs, so that local entrepreneurs can easily reach buyers.

The fair was managed by Leena Ferdous, the three entrepreneurs and organizers of 'Her Powerful Story' and Sultana Islam and Shiuli Islam, owners of Dazzling Attire. Rehnuma Sultana, founder of 'Rehnuma CWE' supported.
The organizers said that they will continue to organize such exhibitions every month in different areas of Dhaka to deliver the products of women entrepreneurs to the buyers.
