Nvidia CEO admits affair with Chinese 'spy', investigation begins

Jensen Huang with Alice Huang. Photo: Nownews
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has admitted to having an extramarital affair with a Chinese female spy suspected of being a US intelligence agency.

Huang is known as the head of this leading AI chip manufacturing company and a close ally of US President Donald Trump. "I was unfaithful to my wife," he apologized in a message to company employees, The New York Times reported.

'My actions have hurt him and our children. There can be no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry.'

In a news release Sunday, Nvidia said Jensen Huang has been the target of foreign intelligence activity — the latest news that the company's board of directors is launching an independent investigation.

Late last month, the New York Times reported that Jensen Huang had a close relationship with Alice Huang (also known as Huang Le), a Chinese official at a Singapore-based data center.

The report, citing sources familiar with the surveillance of Alice Huang and other government officials and industry experts, said U.S. officials considered Alice Huang a Chinese spy who smuggled information to Beijing.

When asked about the suspicion of espionage for China, Liu Chang, a spokesman for the Chinese embassy in Washington, told the New York Times that the United States is carrying out "baseless slander and propaganda" against them.

Nvidia's news release on Sunday said its board of directors 'places the national and economic security of the United States as the highest priority. ...even if the Board of Directors has no reason to believe that national security or confidential Nvidia information has been leaked, the Board will consider all information obtained in this investigation.'

The board of directors said CEO Huang supports the investigation and has agreed to fully cooperate with it. There was no indication that Huang's position was in jeopardy; Rather, the board of directors said, 'They have full confidence in Jensen and the team he leads.'

Due to the massive popularity of Nvidia's chips, they are now one of the top and sought-after companies in the stock market. The company's market capitalization is about $4.5 trillion.

Nvidia spokesman John Rizzo told the New York Times on Sunday, 'This news will not slow Nvidia down. Jensen is committed to leading Nvidia as CEO without interruption.'

Huang, like other tech leaders, has become close to Trump as he embraces AI. The Nvidia chief and his wife Laurie Huang attended a state dinner in honor of Chinese President Xi Jinping at the White House in September.

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio declined to say whether Huang had been excluded from the US government's secret briefing in light of the recent news. On Sunday, he told reporters, 'I cannot comment on this due to the sensitive nature of the matter.'

While Huang is a close ally of the US president, it would be hard to ignore the potential leak of security information to a US rival state and main rival in the AI ​​race.

In September, Trump said, 'We are leading China in AI. We are the most technologically advanced country in the world. And frankly, I want to hold this position; Because whoever wins the race of AI, the victory will be his in the final judgment.'
