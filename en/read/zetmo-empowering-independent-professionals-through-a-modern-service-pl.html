ZETMO: Empowering Independent Professionals Through a Modern Service Platform

By VOSB Desk5 November 2026 at 11:38 am2 min read

In our fast-paced daily lives, finding a reliable professional for an urgent everyday task—whether it is home maintenance, cleaning, or technical repair—can often turn into a frustrating hassle. The service sector in Bangladesh has traditionally relied on word-of-mouth or neighborhood networks, leaving both customers and service providers in a highly fragmented ecosystem.

Stepping in to solve this everyday challenge is ZETMO, an emerging Bangladeshi service marketplace platform designed to bridge the gap between service seekers and independent professionals through the power of technology.

The Vision: Convenience Meets Opportunity

The core philosophy behind ZETMO is simple yet impactful: everyday services should be easily accessible, transparent, and hassle-free. The founder of ZETMO recognized a two-sided problem in the local market. On one side, consumers were struggling to find trusted, timely help. On the other side, countless skilled independent professionals were struggling to expand their customer base beyond their immediate physical localities.

ZETMO was built to serve as the digital bridge. For the user, the platform acts as an intuitive, on-demand directory to discover and connect with local service providers in just a few clicks. Whether you need an electrician, a cleaner, or a specialized technician, ZETMO aims to put the solution right at your fingertips.

Empowering Independent Professionals

While customer convenience is a major focus, ZETMO’s mission extends deeply into empowering the local gig economy. By providing a structured digital marketplace, ZETMO allows independent service professionals to build their digital presence, showcase their skills, and reach a much wider audience than they could through traditional offline methods.

This creates a win-win scenario: users get reliable services faster, and local professionals unlock new avenues for steady income and business growth without needing a massive marketing budget.

Looking Ahead: Building a Connected Ecosystem

Currently in its growth phase, ZETMO is continuously refining its platform based on real-world user needs. The founding team is focused on expanding their network of verified professionals and enhancing the user interface to ensure every interaction on the platform is seamless.

ZETMO is not just building an app; they are building a community built on trust, efficiency, and empowerment. As Bangladesh's digital economy continues to mature, platforms like ZETMO are proving that homegrown technological solutions can profoundly improve the quality of everyday life while driving local economic empowerment.
