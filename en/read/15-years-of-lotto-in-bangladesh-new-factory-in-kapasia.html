15 years of Lotto in Bangladesh: New factory in Kapasia

By বিজ্ঞপ্তি24 November 2026 at 6:01 pm2 min read

The sporty lifestyle brand 'Loto' has completed 15 years of its journey in Bangladesh. Representatives of different media, business partners and senior officials of Lotto Bangladesh were present at an event organized at Gulshan Club in Dhaka on October 7. At this time, they highlighted the brand's long 15-year progress, achievements and future plans.

The brand is delivering international quality footwear, apparel and accessories products to the customers of the country. Currently, Lotto has a total of 247 outlets across the country, including 137 flagships and 110 franchisees.

Lotto Bangladesh has inaugurated a new factory at Kapasia in Gazipur on its 15th anniversary. Equipped with modern technology and skilled manpower, this factory will manufacture TPR, EVA, PU and PVC-based sports and leather footwear. The company is hopeful that this world-class factory will play a role in increasing the production capacity of Bangladesh in the international arena beyond the country's market.

It was announced at the event that Lotto Bangladesh is going to launch 'Entrepreneur Development Program' (Entrepreneur Development Program) to create new entrepreneurs and develop local businesses across the country. In this initiative, sustainable business will be outlined by identifying potential youth at different local levels of the country. According to the plan, successful entrepreneurs can build a network of up to 20 stores in phases within 10 years. This will increase the brand's retail network as well as increase employment and efficiency at the local level.

Kazi Jamil Islam, Managing Director of Lotto Bangladesh said, 'The main strength of this long journey that started with one customer was the genuine trust of the customers. Our greatest strength is the youth of this country. We believe that given the right opportunities and guidance, these young entrepreneurs will be able to create new jobs and establish the name of Bangladesh in the world.
