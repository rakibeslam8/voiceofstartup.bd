Traders without a business cannot become trade body leaders

By VOSB Desk16 November 2026 at 1:23 pm9 min read

If there is no business there will be no trade association membership. That is, someone who does not do business cannot be the leader of a trade organization. The government is going to finalize the new trade organization rules keeping provisions to ensure the leadership of real businessmen in the trade organizations of the country.

Apart from this, several changes are being made in the trade association rules to bring back normal activities in the Federation of Bangladesh Industries and Chambers of Commerce (FBCCI), the top organization of traders. The provision of direct voting is being removed for all posts. As in the past, the directorship will be elected first. Later the elected directors will elect the president and vice-president from among themselves. At the same time, the size of FBCCI Board of Directors is being increased from 46 to 52. Two years ago the size of this board was 80 members.

Other trade bodies like the FBCCI are also going to be exempted from direct voting for all posts. They can also vote as per previous rules. However, the provision of one term break after serving two consecutive terms in the board of directors of all trade organizations including FBCCI remains the same as before. No discount is given in this case.

It is known that the Ministry of Commerce has finalized the draft of Trade Organization Rules-2026. The rules are currently pending vetting by the Law Ministry. It will be issued in notification form soon.

By repealing the Trade Organizations Ordinance of 1961, the government enacted a new Trade Organizations Act in 2022. On the basis of this Act, the previous interim government issued the notification of Trade Organization Rules-2025.

After the notification was issued in May last year, besides FBCCI, several trade organizations including Dhaka Chamber and Metropolitan Chamber objected to the rules. In view of these objections, the Ministry of Commerce took the initiative to revise the rules. A meeting on this was held last September but later the process got delayed. Due to the non-amendment of the rules, even after the announcement of the FBCCI election schedule, voting did not take place. Because, multiple writ cases are filed demanding amendment of the rules.

Last February, the BNP government took responsibility and took the initiative to revise the rules. The draft was finalized last July. It is known that new rules are being made by making some changes in it.

* New regulations to bring back genuine traders under the leadership of trade associations.
* The provision of direct voting in FBCCI is being removed.
* Membership suspended if there is no business activity for two consecutive years. The membership is canceled if the transaction is made in the next year as well.
* Mandatory direct voting for president and vice-president positions in all trade associations is being waived.
* A trader can vote for one even if he has more than one membership.
* Board of Directors of FBCCI increased from 46 to 52 members.

No Business No Membership

So far, whether a business has been active or closed, does not affect the suspension or cancellation of trade association membership. However, the new rules are keeping clear provisions in this regard. For example, if you are not associated with a specific business for two consecutive years or if there is no turnover or transaction in that business, the membership of the trade organization will be suspended. Membership will be canceled if no business is transacted for a year thereafter.

Apart from this, if there is a written complaint from a consumer or buyer regarding irregularities, errors or any other matter regarding the business activities of any member, the executive committee or the board of directors of the concerned organization will investigate and resolve it within 30 days. If the complaint is proved, the organization will cancel the membership of the concerned person or organization.

Meanwhile, Taskin Ahmed, the current president of Dhaka Chamber, commented that the initiative to limit the opportunity to become a leader of trade organizations if there is no business. Yesterday Sunday he told Prothom Alo, of course this is a very good initiative. Because there is no shortage of people to lead in business organizations if they do not know the 'B' of business. If there is no business and limited opportunities to be a leader, organizations will be spared from weedy leadership. Real business people will get a chance to lead.

In response to another question, Taskin Ahmed said, "I have heard that the amendments that we proposed in the rules are in place."

If the notification of rules is issued, within three-four days, the appeal board and election board will be formed after meeting with the subsidiary committee. After that the election will be completed as soon as possible.
Fazlul Haque, Administrator, FBCCI

Direct voting is not mandatory for all posts

Mandatory direct voting for all posts including president and vice-president of the executive committee or board of directors of all trade associations including FBCCI is being waived. According to the draft rules, the voting rights, election and leadership of the executive committee or board of directors of the trade organization will be determined according to the articles of association and memorandum of association of the concerned organization.

However, even if the obligation of direct voting has been waived, if a businessman serves two consecutive terms in the board of directors, he will have to take part in the next election with a break of one term. This provision will be applicable to all trade organizations including FBCCI. However, this rule will not be applicable in the case of trade organizations having a general membership of less than 50.

According to the draft rules, even if a person holds more than one membership in a trade organization, he cannot vote more than one. A trader can vote as many factories as he owns in the elections of BGMEA and BKMEA, two associations of garment industry owners. The new rules no longer have that opportunity.

Of course this is a very good initiative. Because there is no shortage of people to lead in business organizations if they do not know the 'B' of business. If there is no business and limited opportunities to be a leader, organizations will be spared from weedy leadership.
Taskin Ahmed, President, Dhaka Chamber

FBCCI board posts are increasing

According to the current rules, the board of directors of the federation has 46 members. The new rules increase the size of the board to 52 by increasing the posts of two vice-chairmen and four nominated directors. Apart from this, the number of nominated directors of Chamber and Association groups will be increased from 12 to 14. Seven people from both groups.

Two years ago, however, FBCCI's board of directors had 80 members. Among them, there were 34 nominated directors. Many of the businessmen were upset that there were so many directorships without elections.

As per the new rules, 36 directorships will be directly voted for. Along with this, 14 members each from the Chamber and Association groups and two nominees each from the Women's Chamber and Association groups will come to the Board of Directors. The Directors shall then elect from among themselves a President, a Senior Vice-President and four Vice-Presidents.

The directors nominated on the board of FBCCI will come from Dhaka Chamber, Chittagong Chamber, Rajshahi Chamber, Khulna Chamber, Sylhet Chamber, Metro Chamber (MCCI) and Bangladesh Chamber of Industry (BCI). The nominated directors of the associations will be drawn from BGMEA, BKMEA, BTMA, Pharmaceutical Industry Association, BAB, BPGMEA and FLAXA. Apart from this, one nominated director from Women Chamber and Women Entrepreneurs Association will be added.

Meanwhile, the number of members of the Board of Directors of FBCCI is increasing but the number of members of the General Board is decreasing. According to the draft rules, three members instead of five from 'A' category commodity trade organizations or associations and two members instead of three from 'B' category associations will come to the general council. On the other hand, instead of six people from five class chambers, four people will get a chance to come to the general council. Apart from this, there will be three members instead of four from 'B' class chambers and one member each from class 'C' associations and chambers as before.

On August 5, 2024, a section of the organization's members demanded the resignation of the then board of FBCCI after the political change in the students' uprising. In view of this, the then president Mahbubul Alam resigned. Later on September 11, the Federation Council was dissolved and the Ministry of Commerce appointed an administrator. Two administrators have completed their terms. But there was no election. At present, Fazlul Haque, a businessman in the ready-made clothing sector, is serving as the administrator.

FBCCI administrator Fazlul Haque told Prothom Alo, "I don't know how the new trade organization rules are going. However, we hope that there will be rules so that real businessmen can come to the leadership of the trade organization. He further said that if the notification of the rules is issued, the appeal board and election board will be formed within three-four days after meeting with the auxiliary committee. After that, the election will be completed as soon as possible.
