Two friends are making cars after leaving their jobs

By VOSB Desk11 November 2026 at 11:48 am6 min read

Two friends started business with only 30 lakh taka capital. Masuduzzaman Khan and Rashed Nasser. The company received more than one crore work orders last year. So far more than 250 cars have been manufactured for half a hundred corporate organizations. Apart from this, they have recently started producing electric cars for commercial establishments.

After completing higher education abroad, two young people have worked in several corporate organizations. After working for more than a century, they started business. Agrani Automobiles established a car conversion company with their own accumulated capital. The company mainly manufactures the main structure of the car and various types of superstructure.

The company, which started its business with a capital of only 30 lakh rupees, received more than 1 crore work orders last year. So far more than 250 cars have been manufactured for half a hundred corporate organizations. Apart from this, they have recently started producing electric cars for commercial establishments.

Recently, the two founders of the company spoke at the factory of Agrani Automobiles in the Durgapur road area near the Demra staff quarters of the capital. With Masuduzzaman Khan and Rashed Nasser. They are the chairman and managing director of the company respectively.

Leave a job and go into business

Rashed Naser, co-founder of Agrani Automobiles, went from Dhaka to Bangalore University, India to pursue higher education in marketing. He returned home after graduation in 2005. Returning to the country, he worked for two years at a student consulting firm. The manufacturer then worked for 11 years in the sales and marketing department of two multinational companies in the United States and China. He later quit that job and went into business.

Another co-founder of the company is Md. Masuduzzaman Khan studied Computer Science from Binary University of Management, Malaysia. After his higher education, he got a job at a Hong Kong-based technology company in Malaysia in 2005. After two years, he returned to the country and started working in the Information Management Department (MIS) of an automobile company. Later, for more than a decade, he worked in the technology department of various companies in the automobile and food sector of the country.

The two said they had been interested in business since their student days. However, it is difficult to do business without a good idea. For this, he previously worked in a corporate organization and accumulated experience. Then they went into business.

Both Rashed and Masuduzzaman are longtime friends. As a result, they sometimes talked about business ventures. Then in 2019, the first two friends started the biofloc fish farming business. Rashed was also involved in the business of making embroidery thread. But due to Corona, the business is facing loss. Then the two thought of doing something different. From that thought joined the car conversion and decoration business.

Starting at Tk 30 Lakhs

Agrani Automobiles was founded by two friends in March 2021. They had their own premises on Durgapur Road adjacent to the Demra staff quarters in the capital. There he set up the company's factory in 14 khata places. The initial capital for setting up the factory was 30 lakh taka. Later they increased this investment.

Masuduzzaman Khan, co-founder of the company, said, "I had good experience in the automobile and technology sector because of the job. Rashed Nasser also had experience in sales and marketing. So we came into the car business with the idea that if we do something together, it will be more likely to do well.'

Masuduzzaman also said, 'It is very costly to import and sell commercial vehicles in the country. For this reason, many make imported chassis with other materials locally. However, there is a shortage of trained workers in this sector. We are trying to fill that space. We are preparing a car according to the necessary standards so that it can be made strong and useful for a long time.

The type of car that is made

Since its inception, the company has received massive response. Arun Automobiles initially imported various types of vehicles such as cargo body trucks, open trucks, dump trucks etc. by importing the chassis. Now in addition to that work, the company has started manufacturing various special purpose vehicles. For example, Agrani Automobiles has built several mobile eye hospitals for a private organization. Importing such cars with necessary facilities from abroad is very costly. However, Agrani Automobiles provided the structure and interior of the mobile hospital at low cost.

Thus, the company has manufactured vehicles such as mobile dental hospital, motorcycle carrier, food card delivery carrier, cylinder carrier, arcade game material carrier, caravan, insulation trolley, hearse, well and milk carrier etc. Apart from this, various commercial hotels, resorts or dormitory rooms are also being made with containers. Inside these air-conditioned containers there are various facilities including office room, rest room, wash room. Apart from this, there are currently orders for the manufacture of several vehicles with facilities for mobile maternity hospitals and dental hospitals.

Rashed Nasser, co-founder of Agrani Automobiles, said, "We manufacture any type of goods transport and service vehicle according to the customer's needs. Currently 14 regular employees are working in Agrani Automobiles. Apart from this, several workers also work on a temporary basis according to the work demand.

Electric Vehicle Manufacturing Initiative
Agrani Automobiles has recently started manufacturing eco-friendly electric cars. The company has given the brand name of this newly produced car as 'Evotech'. They are primarily making small Evotech vehicles for use in various hotels, resorts, residential areas, economic zones, export processing areas and factories.

Passengers can travel comfortably in these cars of two to eight seats. Anyone can drive the car. The six-seater Evotech vehicle has a capacity of 1 tonne. The car can run for 8 to 12 hours on a single charge. Entrepreneurs said that they are selling Evotech at 50 percent less price than such imported cars.

In this regard, Rashed Nasser said that the capacity of the power sector in the country is increasing day by day. On the other hand, there is volatility in the fuel oil market. The use of electric vehicles is also being encouraged from an environmental point of view. So now one of our main goals is to make eco-friendly electric cars.

In the beginning, Agrani Automobiles is making small electric cars with its own technology and indigenous materials. The aim of the company is to gradually manufacture large vehicles from 500 kg to 2 tonnes capacity. This will include various types of commercial vehicles including school and staff buses. However, the company's co-founder said some challenges in this case. Masuduzzaman Khan. He said, "Not getting the necessary financing from the bank is a big challenge for us. Apart from this, the price of every raw material has increased at present. Therefore, government policy support is needed to produce environment-friendly cars.
