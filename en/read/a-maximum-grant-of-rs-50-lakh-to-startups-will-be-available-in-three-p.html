Startups can get grants of up to Tk 50 lakh in three phases

The government will grant a maximum of Tk 50 lakh to startups based on innovation and technology, for which expatriates can also apply. In this regard, the Information and Communication Technology Department has formulated the 'Startup Grant Management and Administration Policy, 2026 related to Information and Communication Technology'.

According to the policy, grants ranging from Tk 1 lakh to Tk 50 lakh will be given considering the various stages of development of the startup. One lakh to three lakh taka for idea development, three lakh to one million taka for prototype and minimum viable product (MVP) development and one million to three lakh taka for investment readiness, bridge and matching funding. However, considering the rationale, in special cases, the amount of this grant can be up to 50 lakh taka.

Individual applicants at the prototype and MVP stages will receive a maximum of five lakh rupees. Information and Communication Technology Department issued the policy on November 11. A gazette was issued in this regard on 12 November.

The policy also states that this grant will be given with the aim of converting innovative ideas of early-stage startups into marketable products and services, creating new enterprises, increasing employment, increasing export capacity and attracting domestic and foreign investment.

It also aims to ensure transparency, competition, accountability and results-based financing in government grant management.

How ​​to get the grant

The policy lays down three steps for the development of startups. The first phase is pre-ideation and idea development, the second phase is prototyping and validation and the third phase is investment readiness and bridge funding.

The first phase will provide assistance in identifying innovative ideas, team building, problem analysis, initial mentoring and concept validation. The second phase will involve prototyping, minimum viable product (MVP) development, user and market validation and technical testing.

The third phase will provide assistance in product development, market expansion, sales growth, investment readiness, due diligence and establishing corporate governance.

There are six types of grants. These are Idea Grants, Prototype Development Grants, Pre-Seed Grants, Seed Grants, Investment Readiness Bridge Grants and Special Innovation Grants.

Idea grant funds can be used for concept validation, team building, market research, preliminary research and design. Prototype grants allow for hardware or software development, testing and technical verification. Pre-seed grants will be awarded for MVP creation, market validation, initial customer acquisition and business model development.
