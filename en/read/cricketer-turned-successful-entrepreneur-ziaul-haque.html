Cricketer-turned-successful entrepreneur Ziaul Haque

By VOSB Desk6 November 2026 at 11:35 am7 min read

Once used to play cricket regularly. But he had to leave the game due to injury. But could not move away from cricket. After leaving the game of cricket, he has now built a factory for manufacturing cricket equipment. Ziaul Haque. This is the original report about this successful entrepreneur.

He used to play cricket regularly since his childhood. Ziaul Haque. I wanted to play for the national team. But in the middle, due to an injury, his dream stumbled. Even though his dream of becoming a cricketer was crushed, he did not leave the company of cricket. Quit the game due to injury and enter the entrepreneur's account. He made various cricket materials.

At present, several types of products including international quality cricket bats, balls, gloves, pads are being manufactured in the Ziaul factory. About half a hundred workers are making these products. And he has invested several crores of rupees so far in setting up factories and producing products. This entrepreneur said that he sells products at 40 percent lower prices than imported cricket products in the country. And in recognition of the success of his factory, he has already received the National SME Award.

He recently told Prothom Alo the story of becoming an entrepreneur from a cricketer. He said, playing cricket was a place of intense passion for me. Not being able to become a player, I wanted to be with cricket in any way. And my school teacher mother Mosammat Taslima Khatun and father Alauddin Ahmed have supported me the most in this journey.

Ziaul has named his organization Tanin Sports or TN Sports in accordance with his nickname. Ziaul's house is in Sadar Upazila of Thakurgaon. And the factory of Tanin Sports is in Rahimanpur Union of Sadar. A graduate from the Electrical and Electronics Engineering department of a private university, this entrepreneur is currently pursuing his master's degree in Dhaka University.

Ziaul Haque said that cricket was like an addiction since childhood. But the first chance to prepare himself for cricket came after the secondary examination. That was in 2002. He left for Dinajpur on the second day after completing the secondary examination. The purpose is to take cricket training from the regional Bangladesh Sports Training Institute (BKSP) there. Besides, he got admission in Dinajpur Polytechnic Institute.

He was admitted to the polytechnic while he was mainly involved in sports. While studying there, Ziaul first started playing in various cricket leagues held in Dinajpur. Later that range started to increase. He used to play tournaments in various surrounding districts including Jaipurhat, Naogaon, Bogra, Rangpur.

Ziaul Haque moved to Dhaka at the beginning of 2007 after studying at Polytechnic; Admitted to the private Eastern University. In between, he played cricket in the third, second and first division in the qualifiers in Dhaka. Everything was going well. But the problem started in 2016. Suffers major spine injury in a cricket match. Ziaul had to stop playing cricket. With that, the dream of playing in the national team ended. He left the game and began to walk the path of becoming an entrepreneur.

how to start
Ziaul Haque said that one of the conditions of the Dhaka League was that each team should have at least two foreign players. As a result, he became friends with some players from India and Pakistan while playing in the league. Based on that, Ziaul went to a coach in India for three months of training in 2012. Later went there and found the city of Meerut in the state of Uttar Pradesh. The city of Meerut is famous for manufacturing various cricket products. There are factories of all the world's biggest cricket equipment manufacturing companies.

Ziaul Haque said that the price of international quality cricket bats and balls imported into the country is very high. For example, let's take the leather ball of cricket. Cricket matches are always played with new balls. Because, the new ball swings better. This is why professional players get used to batting with a new ball during practice. But the price of leather ball is very high. Because of this, many people do not get a chance to play with the new ball during practice.

But in Meerut, India, I saw that these products are sold at half the price. Around 2012, the ball which was sold in Dhaka for 500 taka, was available in Meerut for only 200 taka. Again at that time the price of good quality cricket bat in the country was 12-15 thousand taka. The same bat was sold in Meerut for only 5 thousand rupees.

Ziaul Haque said, seeing this difference in prices basically made me interested in becoming an entrepreneur in the manufacture of cricket bat-balls. I thought, if we can bring raw materials to the country and make these materials, then it will be possible to sell them at a very affordable price.

Initial Investment
Ziaul kept the income earned from playing cricket with his mother. His mother bought a house with the deposited money. Ziaul later sold that house and invested in business. Apart from this, he also took loans from his parents and banks for investment.

In the beginning, he used to import bat-balls and other cricket equipment from India under the name Tanin Traders. While doing this work, he made friends with the experienced craftsmen of Meerut. Apart from this, since 2016, he has started setting up his own factory in Thakurgaon. This entrepreneur has invested several crores of rupees with bank loans so far.

Artisans brought from India
Ziaul brought 12 skilled craftsmen from India to manufacture good quality bats and other cricket equipment in the country. He has to bear all the expenses of their stay in the country starting from the preparation of their passports.

Apart from this, cricket pitch, swimming pool, gymnasium, tennis and badminton court have been made in the factory premises for their convenience. 14 local workers are trained through these artisans. At present, trained local artisans are manufacturing the products in the factory in Jiaul.

Import and product manufacturing time taken
Ziaul Haque imported equipment for making cricket equipment from Ghaziabad, India. But there was a problem in importing bat wood and lavlon. There was no HS code for these two products then. Because of this it takes a long time to generate new code and bring those products.

Ziaul said, I have wasted a lot of raw materials and materials to teach the workers. Apart from this, the import of raw materials was completely stopped for about seven months during Corona. I had to go through such challenges.

Current Status
The Ziaul factory currently manufactures more than five cricket products. These include Tennis, Willow & Autograph Bats, Leather Balls, Gloves, Pads, Thai & Chestguards etc. Among them, Lavlon brings the main raw material of gloves-pads from Meerut and Punjab in India. And wood for the bat was brought from Meerut and Kashmir. Ziaul said that good quality cricket bats are made from Kashmir wood.

He has organized his business a lot. At present Tanin Sports has distributors of products all over the country. There are more than 20 distributors in the capital Dhaka alone. However, Ziaul makes products based on demand. At present about 7000 pieces of cricket bat and more than 3500 other products are manufactured in his factory. Ziaul said that he is giving customers the same quality products at 40 percent lower prices than imported products.

Ziaul has not officially started exporting products yet. However, he said that he has that desire in the future. Ziaul Haque said, "I want to build TN Sports as a global brand."
