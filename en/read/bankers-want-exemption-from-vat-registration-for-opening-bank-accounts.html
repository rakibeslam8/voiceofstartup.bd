Bankers want exemption from VAT registration for opening bank accounts of small entrepreneurs

By VOSB Desk13 November 2026 at 9:50 am4 min read

The current fiscal year's budget mandated Business Registration Number (BIN), also known as VAT registration, for opening bank accounts of all types of businesses, including small entrepreneurs. As a result, small entrepreneurs of the country are not able to open bank accounts without BIN. Many people are forced to take BIN.

In this situation, bankers are in trouble. Banks are not able to open bank accounts of small entrepreneurs without BIN. So they approached the VAT department of the National Board of Revenue (NBR). The Association of Bankers, Bangladesh (ABB), the organization of chief executives of the country's banks, wrote a letter to the NBR on August 17. Bankers want this obligation to be waived for small businesses. But the VAT department of NBR has not yet responded.

ABB's letter was answered by the VAT Implementation Branch of NBR on September 17. In response, it was said that there was no opportunity to consider the application due to legal obligations; Instead, all banks are requested to give prompt instructions to verify VAT registration in case of opening new accounts.

An official of NBR said that there is no scope to exempt anyone as the matter has been incorporated through law. Immediately after the law was passed, its implementation began. However, it would have been better to write a letter to the VAT Policy Branch, said this officer of the VAT Implementation Branch. Because, the letter was sent to the implementation branch by ABB.

What ABB Says
ABB leaders say the issue is primarily about financial inclusion. The purpose of this rule is to bring business into a more formal structure and to increase the scope of taxation. There is no disagreement with that objective. But while implementing it, the reality of small entrepreneurs should also be kept in mind.

ABB Chairman Masrur Arefin was contacted to find out about this. As he is out of the country, he told Prothom Alo in a written statement, 'We see many entrepreneurs every day, whose business starts on a very small scale. Some are small shop businesses, while others are selling products online. Many of them have trade licenses, but no VAT registration. Now if it is mandatory before opening a bank account, then the first step for a new entrepreneur to formally enter banking becomes very difficult.

According to Masrur Arefin, our aim should be to bring small entrepreneurs into the bank, not keep them out. When they come to the banking system, all the details of their transactions will be known, gradually formalized, the ability to get loans will increase and finally the scope of the tax system will be expanded.

Meanwhile, NBR has to collect 6 lakh 4 thousand crore rupees in the current fiscal year 2026-27. Out of this, the target of collection from VAT has been set at 2 lakh 57 thousand crores. As an initiative to achieve this huge goal, NBR has taken the initiative to bring small entrepreneurs under VAT.

What problems do entrepreneurs face
Small entrepreneurs have said that there are complications with VAT registration under the new rules. They say that they have to struggle with Tax Identification Number (TIN). Now you have to go through the trouble of getting VAT registration again. Later comes the matter of giving returns. Apart from this, they said that there is a problem in opening a bank account under the new rules.

Girly Boutique has been doing cloth business for six years in North Badda, Dhaka. When asked, the founder of the company, Rahiatul Jannat, told Prothom Alo, "We don't know much about VAT registration. Most of the small entrepreneurs do not know about this. Therefore, many new entrepreneurs are facing problems as VAT registration is made mandatory for opening bank accounts.

The situation is the same for entrepreneurs outside Dhaka. An organization named 'Tant Gallery' has been working with textile products for 12 years in Sirajganj. The founder of the organization. Omar Farooq told Prothom Alo, "We are not able to do the work of TIN properly. Now it is difficult for us to file returns every month with VAT registration. We work alone. It will become an additional burden to calculate the details every month and give the return.
