Weaving the future with jute: Chittagong startup JUTOPIA's remarkable journey in sustainable innovation

By VOSB Desk4 November 2026 at 11:20 am3 min read

Just as the global market is looking for alternatives to environmentally harmful synthetic materials and single-use plastics, Bangladesh's traditional 'golden fiber' is regaining its lost glory. And this green revolution is being led by social enterprise 'JUTOPIA' created by Chittagong's young entrepreneurs. Founded in 2025, the startup is opening new doors to the aesthetic and commercial potential of jute with a vision of "Weaving the Future with Jute".

Keeping in mind the modern consumer and large scale corporate buyers, Zootopia is transforming jute into highly usable, contemporary and commercially viable products.

The story behind the entrepreneur
Zootopia's innovation journey is led by Sirajus Salekin, its founder and CEO. With a strong corporate background in management and accounting and an Executive MBA from North South University (NSU), Salekin ventured into this sustainable sector to combine traditional assets with modern market demands. Building a value-driven brand through eco-conscious design, long-term socio-economic development and empowerment of local artisans is at the core of its leadership.

The Versatility of Jute: Beyond the Traditional Style
Zootopia is not limited to traditional jute sacks or packaging. They are creating diversified products across multiple categories keeping in mind the needs of modern consumers through innovation:

  • Lifestyle & Fashion: Stylish and durable shopping bags, school bags, laptop sleeves and modern fashion accessories.
  • Home & Living: Everyday homeware, kitchenware and stunning decorative items.
  • Sustainable Packaging: Eco-friendly corporate packaging, customized gift bags and promotional products for modern brands.
  • Educational Materials: Innovative learning materials and school products made of jute.

Blending traditional craftsmanship with modern convenience, Zootopia is working to establish itself as a recognized Bangladeshi sustainable brand for domestic retail, institutional buyers and the international B2B market.

Social Impact: “One Child, One Bag” InitiativeApart from business growth, Zootopia is also having a profound social impact. The main objective of their"One Child, One Bag" initiative is to hand over sustainable school bags to children and create environmental awareness among them from an early age. As part of this vision, they stand by the underprivileged children of SOS Shishu Polli located in Halisahar, Chittagong by providing school bags. Zootopia is also committed to creating youth entrepreneurship, creating local employment and ensuring responsible consumption to prevent plastic pollution.

Market Prospects and Investment Opportunities
Bangladesh has all the geographical and historical advantages of being a global hub of versatile jute products. Zootopia is gearing up to build a strong presence in the domestic e-commerce, corporate gifting, NGO procurement and international sustainable lifestyle markets.

Currently in market-validation and early-stage development, the startup is all set to expand its operations. The organization is currently seeking Seed Funding (Seed Funding), Impact Investments and Startup Grants. Besides, they are also looking at strategic mentorship and institutional partnerships to increase production capacity and create international distribution channels.Chittagong on Global Startup Map
Zootopia is not only a testament to sustainable product innovation; It is also a shining example of the entrepreneurial potential hidden in the outskirts of the capital. By building an export-oriented enterprise from Chittagong, Sirazus Salekin and his team are making a great contribution to making Bangladesh's startup ecosystem more decentralized and inclusive.

Zootopia is a unique amalgamation of eco-friendly innovation, social empowerment and commercial success—which showcases Bangladesh's creativity on the international stage and emerges as a great opportunity for investors.
