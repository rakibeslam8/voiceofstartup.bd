Golden Harvest will hire 200 people, apply without experience

By VOSB Desk22 October 2026 at 10:07 pm1 min read

Golden Harvest Infotech Limited (GHITL) is offering job opportunities without experience. The organization has released a notification to recruit 200 people for their 'Data Entry Operator' post. The application has already started and will continue till November 11. Interested candidates can apply online within the prescribed time limit. Candidate must have passed HSC or Diploma.

Let's take a look at the recruitment notification-

Company Name: Golden Harvest Infotech Limited (GHITL)
Post Name: Data Entry Operator
Number of Posts: Not Determined
Job Type: Private, Full Time
Type of Candidate: Male and Female
Educational Qualification: HSC or Diploma
Experience: Not applicable
Salary & Other Benefits: Negotiable
Age Limit: 18-35 years
Source: bdjobs.com
Application Start: 13 October 2026
Application Last Date: 11 November 2026

Application Procedure: Online

Place of work: Must be willing to work in Dhaka (Mirpur), Sylhet and Tangail districts.
