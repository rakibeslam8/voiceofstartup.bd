A couple quit their jobs and became successful in the hostel business

By VOSB Desk4 November 2026 at 10:09 am5 min read

One of the most difficult tasks in the capital is securing safe accommodation. Especially for students and working bachelors, finding accommodation with all the amenities is quite complicated. To solve this problem, hostel services have been developed in different parts of the city. Although it is quite an old business, with the change of time, the style of hostel business has also changed. People now want to live with all the modern facilities. An organization named Unique Home is providing that arrangement.

At Unique Home's office in the capital's Uttara, I spoke with the company's managing director Sharmin Sultana and chairman Rizwanullah Yusuf recently. They said that their initiative is so that the bachelors do not have to find a house with difficulty. Unique Home provides all kinds of materials and services including reading table, cot, fridge, housekeeping.

This entrepreneurial couple, Sharmin Sultana and Rizwanullah Yusuf, live in Bagha, Rajshahi. Both studied software engineering at Daffodil International University. The two were involved in the hostel business from their student days.

how to start

Sharmin suddenly fell ill during his fourth year. For this you have to stay in the hospital for a few days. At that time, he was staying in a mess house in Tallabagh area of ​​Sobhanbagh of the capital. Due to lack of facilities at home, one has to suffer a lot during illness. Then Sharmin thought of renting such a flat house as a mess.

After a few days of searching, he found a four-room house in Sobhan Bagh. However, an advance of Tk 56,000 was required to rent it. He borrowed that money from his father. Sharmin said, 'In the first week, most of the seats in the flat were rented. After receiving good response, I rented another flat in the next month. From these two flats, there was a profit of 40,000 taka per month.

Sharmin Sultana and Rizwanullah Yusuf got married in 2016 after graduation. Later both of them started handling the hostel business together. Both have taught at Daffodil and Independent Universities. Apart from this, they have also worked in several technology institutes. But in the end, the couple quit their jobs and focused on the hostel business. To increase the scope of the business, at one stage they started renting the entire building instead of flats. By 2020, Unique Homes will be operational in four buildings at Sobhanbagh, Shukrabad and Farmgate in Dhaka.

A big shock to business comes during Corona. When restrictions were imposed in the country, many people left their homes. In such a situation, they left all the three buildings. And only one is kept open with a huge discount on rent. Rizwanullah said that it has been going on like this for about 17 months. At that time, they lost about 16 lakh rupees. But when the situation started to normalize, the business started to see the face of profit again. Again three new buildings were rented.

Rizwanullah said that he had to go through a lot of trouble in this business. For example, one day in 2017, the landlord asked to leave the house. This happened to them several times. Rizwanullah said, "We now have long-term contracts with house owners in renting buildings."

Current Status

The couple has so far invested around four crore rupees for the hostel business. Among them, they have received investment of more than crores of rupees from Bangladesh Venture Capital in three phases. Although a lot of hurdles have to be overcome to get this investment, Sharmin said.

At present, Unique Home has five branches in Dhanmondi, Sobhanbagh, Ashulia and Uttara in the capital. Among them three branches for girls and two branches for boys. These hostels have accommodation for about 1000 people. The company currently has about 80 employees.

BENEFITS THAT ARE

Unique Home has both air-conditioned (AC) and non-AC rooms. The fare per seat for non-AC rooms ranges from Tk 6,500 to Tk 20,000. And seat rent in AC room is from 8 thousand to 30 thousand taka. In both cases, the room has all the usual facilities including table, chair, mattress, pillow, cabinet, lift, fridge, internet, laundry, housekeeping, generator. Apart from this, there are three meals a day, prayer and reading room, breakfast kitchen, gym facilities and play area. There is a separate female security guard in the girls hostel.

Sharmin said, Unique Home also has arrangements for those who come to Dhaka from distant areas for taking various exams or for necessary work. For example, a person can stay in a unique home for a month for a minimum of 349 taka per day. Apart from individual customers, Unique Home has housing contracts with various organizations. Stating that rules are strictly followed in the hostel, Sharmin said, "Drugs are absolutely prohibited in the hostel. Apart from this, the residents of the hostel are regularly monitored. We also have people for counseling if any of them are mentally challenged.

Sharmin also told about future plans in business. He said, 'Within five years, we have planned to open at least 35 branches in the capital Dhaka. Apart from this, we are thinking of expanding the activities in every departmental city and important district of the country.

Sharmin said that getting investment in hostel business is a big challenge. He said, "The problem of housing in Dhaka is increasing gradually. There we are solving a big problem in a densely populated city with security. However, hostel type of business has not yet received institutional acceptance in the country. For this reason, despite having proper documents, we have not received any bank loan till now.
