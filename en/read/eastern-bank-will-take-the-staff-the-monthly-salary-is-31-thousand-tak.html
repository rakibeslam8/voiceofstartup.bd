Eastern Bank will take the staff, the monthly salary is 31 thousand taka

By VOSB Desk14 October 2026 at 8:23 am2 min read

Eastern Bank Plc (EBL), one of the leading private commercial banks of the country has released the recruitment circular. The organization will recruit manpower for the post of Bank Representative (Trainee Assistant Officer) in Agent Banking Department. Graduate candidates in business can apply for this post. Interested candidates can apply online till October 22.

Brief information

  • Post Name: Bank Representative (Trainee Assistant Officer), Agent Banking
  • Number of posts: Not fixed
  • Must have Bachelor Degree in Business Education.
  • Work experience of 6 months or more in a similar position will be preferred; However, fresh graduates are also strongly encouraged to apply.
  • Must have excellent communication skills, especially fluent in English.
  • Must have interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Must have a mindset to work on own initiative and as part of a team and eager to perform assigned responsibilities independently.
  • Must be proficient in using online platforms and Microsoft Office applications
  • Flexibility to work in any geographical area of the country.

Main Responsibilities
These representatives appointed by Eastern Bank will be engaged in agent banking outlets on behalf of the bank. They will be responsible for inspecting and supervising outlets, generating new business and strengthening customer relationships, implementing sales strategies, maintaining data in MIS and records, and providing guidance to outlet employees.
Monthly Salary: Overall gross salary is Rs.31,000.
Job Permanence: After 18 months of joining there is an opportunity to assimilate as a full time permanent employee of the bank based on performance and discipline.
Other benefits: Festival bonus, performance bonus, hospitalization and maternity benefits, leave and other acceptable benefits will be provided as per Bank's 'People Management Policy'.

workplace
Anywhere in Bangladesh.

Application Process

Interested candidates should apply through Eastern Bank's own career portal by 22 October 2026. Only selected candidates will be contacted. Any kind of recommendation will be considered as disqualification of the candidate.

Apply-https://ebl.bdjobs.com/Default.asp
