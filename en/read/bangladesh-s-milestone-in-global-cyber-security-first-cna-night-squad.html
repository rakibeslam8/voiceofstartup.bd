Bangladesh's Milestone in Global Cyber ​​Security: First CNA 'Night Squad'

By VOSB Desk25 November 2026 at 4:10 pm3 min read

A historic chapter has begun in Bangladesh's cyber security and technology sector. On November 17, 2026, Knight Squad Academy was officially recognized as the first CVE Numbering Authority (CNA) in Bangladesh by the international CVE Program. Through this achievement, Bangladesh's name has been added for the first time to the international ecosystem of global software vulnerability detection and disclosure.

# What are CVEs and CNAs—Why is this recognition important?

CVE (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures) is the international standard for identifying, defining and creating a public list of security flaws or vulnerabilities in software and digital systems around the world. Each recognized security flaw is assigned a unique identification number (eg: `CVE-2026-12345`), so that researchers, vendors and security teams around the world can identify and resolve the issue in the same language.

On the other hand, CNA (CVE Numbering Authority) is an organization authorized by the CVE Program, which has the authority to directly assign official CVE IDs and publish public records for software vulnerabilities within their designated scope. 569 institutions from only 44 countries of the world are in this prestigious list under MITER Top-Level Root. Until now, researchers in Bangladesh had to rely on foreign institutions to reveal any major security flaws; This recognition of Knight Squad Academy removed that dependency and took domestic research to the global stage.

# International recognition from the community: Knight Squad's journey

Night Squad's success is the result of six long years of hard work and community building. As a platform for knowledge exchange and joint learning on cyber security in 2020 Knight Squad (Knight Squad) started journey by the hand of Moniruzzaman Pradhan (Md. Moniruzzaman Prodhan)**.

In 2022, the institute organized the first international Capture the Flag (CTF) competition ‘KnightCTF 2022’in the history of Bangladesh, which played a major role in connecting Bangladesh with cyber security researchers around the world. LaterKnight Squad Academy was established in 2026 to institutionalize practical cyber security education, professional certification and advanced research.

# New initiative 'KSVRI' and free VDP service

Along with the CNA accreditation, the organization has officially launched the Night Squad Vulnerability Research Initiative (KSVRI) (`ksvri.com'), which will serve as a secure and structured bridge between independent security researchers and software development organizations. The two main services of this platform are:

  • Independent Research and CVE Numbering: Any independent security researcher can submit their found software vulnerabilities to KSVRI. The Academy's research team will verify and resolve the issue responsibly in coordination with the respective vendors or project maintainers and issue official CVE numbers for defects within the approved scope.
  • Free VDP Hosting for Enterprises:Startups, enterprises, commercial software developers or open-source projects can run their ownVulnerability Disclosure Program (VDP) on the KSVRI platform completely free of charge. As a result, organizations will be able to identify and address security flaws in their systems early.

# Impact on startup and technology sector in Bangladesh

This achievement is highly significant for Bangladesh's rapidly growing fintech, software and startup ecosystem. Domestic startups without expensive infrastructure can now secure their digital infrastructure with free VDP facility through KSVRI. At the same time, new doors of international quality research and recognition were opened for the country's young cyber security researchers and zero-day hunters.

  • Historic Achievement: First CVE Numbering Authority (CNA) of Bangladesh — 17 November 2026
  • Major Initiatives: KnightCTF, Knight Squad Academy, and KSVRI (ksvri.com)
