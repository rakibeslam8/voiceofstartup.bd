Why did the speed of transactions in Bangla QR stopped?

By VOSB Desk7 November 2026 at 7:05 pm5 min read

The 'Bangla QR' initiative launched to popularize digital transactions by reducing the use of cash has largely stalled. Initially, the transaction speed was promising but in recent times it has declined. However, Bangladesh Bank has set targets for increasing transactions to banks and financial institutions.

Meanwhile, Bangla QR code service has been launched for person-to-person transactions from this month.

Bangladesh Bank's six-month data review from last May to October shows that in the last two weeks of last May, Bangla QR was traded in the amount of Tk 20-30 crores daily. Daily transactions increased to Tk 135 crore in the first week of October. After that the transactions started to decrease. In the last week of October, transactions fell to Tk 119 crore. In this situation, without new initiatives, there is no possibility of increasing transactions soon, the people related to the sector think.

Where daily transactions were Tk 20-30 crore in May, it rose to Tk 135 crore in the first week of October. After that it decreased to 119 crore rupees.

Bangladesh Bank's spokesperson Arif Hossain Khan said, 'We also do not understand why the speed of transactions in Bangladesh QR decreased. Bangladesh Bank is trying to increase transactions by removing obstacles. If necessary, more initiatives will be taken to make Bangla QR universal.

WHY TRANSACTION RESPONSE IS LOW

Last July, the use of Bangla QR code was made mandatory for transactions. As a result, all the banks and MFS of the country were forced to set Bengali QR codes at the merchant level instead of their own codes. Against one bank or MFS account of these merchants, various banks and MFS institutions have given up to four-five Bangla QR codes. In this, merchants scattered across the country become an alternative mechanism for cash withdrawals. From village shopkeepers to big service and product vendors are in this merchant list. As a result, the MFS agent ecosystem has collapsed under the influence of a large number of merchants.

We also don't understand why the transaction speed has decreased in Bangla QR. Bangladesh Bank is trying to increase transactions by removing obstacles. Further initiatives will be taken if necessary to make Bangla QR universal.
Arif Hossain Khan, Spokesperson, Bangladesh Bank

From October, Bangladesh Bank zeroed out the cost for merchants to receive money through Bangla QR. As a result, merchants do not have to pay any fee for receiving money from the customer and returning it to the customer in cash. In contrast, cash outs from MFS agents like Bikash, Cash and Rocket have to pay a certain amount of fees. For every 1000 MFS institutes receive more than Rs.3 in revenue from this fee. But there is no cost when withdrawing money through merchants. Merchants do not have to pay any revenue to the government. Hence withdrawing cash from merchants using this QR code is profitable for both the customer and the merchant.

It poses a major business hurdle for the government and MFS institutions. On September 30, Bangladesh Bank announced that the account will be closed if any money is withdrawn through Bangla QR to increase digital transactions. Since then, MFSs and banks have been shutting down cash withdrawals instead of purchases. Since then, QR transactions have slowed down.

Initiatives to increase transactions

An allocation of Tk 100 crore has been set aside in the financial year 2026-27 to encourage banks and MFS institutions through Bangladesh Bank to increase transactions through Bangla QR code. Individual registered small and marginal sellers or service providers are being given incentives for transactions up to Tk 2,000. Under this facility the bank or MFS institution installs the QR code at the merchant point. Organizations that transact using this QR code (Acquiring Organizations) are getting 0.10% incentive on the transaction. On the other hand, the issuing institution through which the customer is paying the money through the bank or MFS is getting an incentive equal to 0.20 percent of the transaction. Still, transactions are not growing as expected.

We have set staff targets to increase the use of Bangla QR. Account opening and transactions are constantly increasing. In this way, if everyone comes forward, the service will grow.
Shaukat Ali Khan, Managing Director, Sonali Bank

Image of TransactionA review of the data showed that 7 weeks out of the last 24 weeks, QR transactions were negative or negative. Several technical and structural weaknesses have been blamed by industry stakeholders for the stagnation in transactions. According to them, a large part of the country's citizens are now customers of mobile banking services Bikash and Cash. The service cannot be popularized without the active participation of these two organizations. Customers should also be made aware. Infrastructure should also be developed. It is also important to make banking apps more user-friendly. The announcement of using Bangla QR app without internet should also be implemented.Who tops in Bangla QR

Islami, Pubali, Janata, Sonali and Rupali Bank are currently leading in opening new merchant accounts in Bangla QR.

When asked, Sonali Bank's Managing Director (MD) Shaukat Ali Khan told Prothom Alo, 'We have set targets for employees to increase the use of Bangla QR. Account opening and transactions are constantly increasing. If everyone comes forward in this way, the service will grow.
