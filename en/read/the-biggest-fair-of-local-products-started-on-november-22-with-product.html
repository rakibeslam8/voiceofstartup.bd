Biggest fair of local products to begin Nov 22 with goods from 400 entrepreneurs

By VOSB Desk17 November 2026 at 8:59 am3 min read

There is an opportunity to buy various products including clothes, shoes, bags, jewelry, cosmetics, jute goods, handicrafts, electronics made by domestic entrepreneurs under one roof. On November 22, the 13th National SME Product Fair is starting at Agargaon in the capital. More than four hundred entrepreneurs will display and sell products made by their companies at this year's SME fair.

The SME Foundation under the Ministry of Industry is organizing the fair. The fair will be inaugurated on November 22 at 10 am at the Bangladesh-China Friendship Conference Center in Agargaon. Will continue till November 28. The fair will be open to all from 10 am to 9 pm every day. There is no fee to enter the fair.

According to the information provided by SME Foundation, entrepreneurs from various sectors including footwear and leather goods, jute goods, handicrafts and handicrafts, fashion and ready-made garments, herbal products and cosmetics, ornaments, agricultural and processed food products, light engineering, plastic and rubber products, electronics and information technology will participate in this fair.

Only domestic manufacturers and entrepreneurs of service micro, small and medium industries will get the opportunity to display and sell their products in the fair. No foreign or imported goods can be displayed or sold in the fair.

Loan application facility

According to SME Foundation, in addition to buying and selling products in the fair, there is a special opportunity for entrepreneurs to learn about bank loans. Entrepreneurs can learn about SME loan conditions, application process and other issues by directly discussing with representatives of banks and financial institutions participating in the fair.

Even if the bank and the entrepreneur agree, the loan application can be made at the fair premises itself. Apart from this, entrepreneurs can take advice about the possibility of getting a loan and the necessary preparations.

The seven-day fair will host several seminars for entrepreneurs and visitors on easy financing, diversification of export products, creation of new entrepreneurs and development of women entrepreneurship. These seminars will be organized at Windy Town Hall of Bangladesh-China Friendship Conference Centre.

In the previous 12 fairs, products worth 72 crores were sold

SME Foundation has been organizing the National SME Product Fair in Dhaka since 2012. About 3,500 entrepreneurs have participated in the 12 fairs held so far. In these fairs, entrepreneurs have sold products worth about 72 crore rupees.

Apart from the national fair, the company also organizes SME product fairs in different regions of the country. So far, about 5 thousand 100 entrepreneurs have participated in 94 regional and departmental fairs.

Apart from this, SME Foundation is giving National SME Entrepreneur Award to encourage entrepreneurs. So far 71 entrepreneurs have received this award. Among them 37 women, 33 men and one third gender entrepreneur.

According to the Economic Survey 2024 data, the country has about 1.17 million industrial establishments. 99 percent of these belong to cottage, micro, small and medium industries. About 85 percent of the total employment in the industrial sector is in this sector. More than three crore people work in these institutions.
