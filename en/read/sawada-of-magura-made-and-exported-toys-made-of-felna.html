Sawada of Magura made and exported toys made of felna

By VOSB Desk7 November 2026 at 8:35 pm6 min read

At a glance BOLD PARTNERS LIMITED

  • The journey started in 2018. So far the export of goods is about 34 crore rupees.
  • 400 types of pet products are made from papaya tube, mango and neem stalks, sedge leaves and grass.
  • Employment of 500 local women directly and indirectly. Another 100 villagers gave raw material.
  • Products are exported to Japan, Canada, USA, Germany and South Korea.

Sawada Islam, an entrepreneur of Angardah village in Jagla area of ​​Magura, set a unique example of making products suitable for the global market with common materials lying around the house. He makes 400 types of products including pet food, clothing and toys from local materials such as papaya tuber, sajan leaves, coconut bark, mango and neem tree twigs or pakchong grass. And they are exported to developed countries like Japan, Canada, USA, Germany and South Korea.

Saat Chui Chui Sawada Islam has opened new doors of possibilities in the rural economy. Also playing an important role in empowering women. For example, about 500 local women have been directly and indirectly employed in the company named 'Bold Partners Limited' established by him in Angardah village of Jagla area of ​​Magura.

It is known that after a long exile, Sawada Islam returned to the country and established this factory based on 100% local raw materials, where a lot of export-oriented products are produced. According to National Board of Revenue (NBR) data, from 2018 to April last year, Bold Partners Limited exported products worth a total of 2.736 million dollars, which is equivalent to 33.76 crore rupees in local currency (123 rupees 40 paise per dollar). By far the largest export is to Japan. Canada is the second largest buyer. Apart from this, his products are being exported to USA, Germany and South Korea.

Disciplined workplace in the village
Recently, going to Angardah village, 12 km from Magura Sadar, it can be seen that hundreds of women workers are working in a very orderly manner in this factory established on about 25 acres of land in a completely natural environment. Some are busy making attractive belts for dogs out of cloth and leather. Some are making artistic bird nests by weaving grass with skillful hands. Again, women in groups are drying grass, branches, stalks and leaves.

Officials said the factory has four parts. One makes pet clothes and one makes dog and cat toys. The third segment is made up of pet food and snacks. Another part houses the grass factory, where toys and food for rabbits, guinea pigs and mice are made. They said that papaya tuber is processed to make food for rabbits, guinea pigs and hamsters. Neem pods are used to clean the teeth of pets. Dogs chew these branches and throw them away. It cleans the dog's teeth. Rat houses made of grass are going to Canada. In total, about 400 types of pet food and toys are being manufactured so far.

Started with five employees
Born in Keshav Mor area of Magura city, Sawada Islam completed his studies in the United States and started a small business there. He left the US for family reasons and moved to Japan. But he left the 'American Dream' or the better life in Japan and finally returned home.

Sawada Islam said, "The soil where a person is born, he owes a huge debt to the soil." And the best way to repay this debt is to provide employment to people. I am doing that now.

Sawada Islam said that he came back to his native Magura because of the philosophy of 'teaching someone to fish with a spear without giving them a fish' and to be with his elderly parents. Then, out of love for pets, he came up with the idea of ​​making food and toys for them. Accordingly, Bold Partners Limited was established in 2018. His journey started with only four or five workers.

He started his activities by making only two types of toys. In the same year, the company exported products worth three thousand US dollars to Japan in the first shipment. Then new workers are needed as demand increases. In this case, priority is given to the underprivileged women of the area. They are trained and prepared in the factory. Seeing Sawada Islam succeeding in this way, later two companies from Japan and Singapore invested a large amount of money in Bold Partners Limited.

New momentum in rural economy
Sawada Islam told Prothom Alo that his project is running on about 25 acres of land. Various trees and grasses have been planted there as raw material for pet food and toys. Due to the huge demand of products from abroad, raw materials are now procured from others to produce more products. Papaya tuber, mango and neem stalks or grass are bought in cash when these things are brought to the factory. As a result of this innovative initiative, hundreds of people from the village now collect papaya tuber, mango and neem stalks or grass etc. and bring them to the factory. It has added a new dimension to the rural economy.

Supriya Rani has been working in Bold Partners Limited since its inception. He told Prothom Alo, "I have worked here and bought land, built a house, many needs of the family have been fulfilled. The biggest advantage is that I can work from home.

About 200 women workers like Supriya Rani are directly employed here. The organization has its own transportation system for their transportation. Apart from this, another 300 women make various products at home, said the officials of the company.

Entrepreneur Sawada Islam said that there is a huge market for pet food and toy products around the world. Currently, India, China and Vietnam occupy a large part of this market. But Bangladesh is still almost at zero.

Sawada Islam wants numerous such institutions to be established in every district of Bangladesh within the next 5 to 10 years. Because, to meet the huge demand in the global market, there is a need for numerous entrepreneurs across the country.
