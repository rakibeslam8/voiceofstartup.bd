Let Your Startup Story Inspire Everyone: Opportunity to Publish Free Articles on 'Voice of Startup Bangladesh'

By VOSB Desk23 October 2026 at 9:15 pm2 min read

'Voice of Startup Bangladesh' is a leading digital platform for startup, business and technology news in the country. We are working to highlight the people, ideas and innovations that build the country's startup ecosystem. We deliver verified information and insights from government agencies like BIDA, BEPZA, BEZA, BSCIC and ICT to entrepreneurs first.

We have brought a great opportunity to accompany entrepreneurs in this journey. Recently we have received more than 780 founding mails in just 3 weeks and are currently in full swing to cover all the startup stories.

In line with this, we invite new and old entrepreneurs from all parts of the country to showcase their business stories on our platform. Any person or founder can publish their article by mail us absolutely free. But there is only one condition—the venture must be a valid business or startup.

Featured on our platform is what you get:

​Free Marketing & Awareness:Your initiative will reach thousands of readers, customers and policy makers, increasing your brand awareness and promotion.

​Investment Opportunities:Our platform can bring your venture to the attention of potential investors, which will accelerate your chances of getting funding in the future.

​Story Writing Help:If you can't write a coherent article, don't worry! Our skilled team will fully assist you in crafting your startup story and creating appropriate articles and posts.
