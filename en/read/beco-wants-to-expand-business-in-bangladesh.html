Beco wants to expand business in Bangladesh

By VOSB Desk10 November 2026 at 3:25 pm1 min read

Beco, a subsidiary of Turkey's Koch Group, has pledged to continue investing in expanding production capacity and business expansion in Bangladesh. Recently, senior officials of Koch Holding and Beco visited Bangladesh and visited Singer Bangladesh's manufacturing plant, concept store and corporate office. This information was given in a press release.

The delegation included Polat Sen, President, Consumer Durables Group, Koch Holding; Zafar Ustuner, Chief Commercial Officer of Beko Asia, Russia and South Africa and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Arcelic-Hitachi, and Nihat Bayez, Chief Manufacturing and Technology Officer of Beko.

The company's Managing Director (MD) and CEO MHM Fairoz highlighted the business activities of Singer Bangladesh and the importance of investment in Bangladesh before the visiting officials. He said that Singer Bangladesh is working to bring about change in these three areas - production, business management and working environment of employees.
