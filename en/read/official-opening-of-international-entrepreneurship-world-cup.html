Official opening of International Entrepreneurship World Cup

By VOSB Desk11 April 2025 at 8:34 am2 min read

Inauguration and formal campaign of 'Entrepreneurship World Cup' or 'International Entrepreneurship World Cup (EWC) 2025' has started in Bangladesh. The program started with a 'meet the press' program organized at Daffodil Plaza in Dhanmondi, capital.

The event was jointly organized by Daffodil International University and Global Entrepreneurship Network (GEN) Bangladesh.

The organizers said that the aim of organizing the Entrepreneurship World Cup is to highlight the business plans (ideas) of new entrepreneurs, establish networking with global investors and mentors and introduce the startup ecosystem of Bangladesh at the global level.

Chairman of Global Entrepreneurship Network (GEN) Bangladesh and Daffodil International University presided over the opening ceremony. Sabur Khan

Apart from this, Prothom Alo's digital transformation and youth program chief coordinator Munir Hasan, Daffodil Family Chief Executive Officer Mohammad Nuruzzaman, Bangladesh Venture Capital Limited Chief Executive Officer Md. Shaukat Hossain and Jane Bangladesh Managing Director KM Hasan Ripon.

Organizers say the Entrepreneurship World Cup (EWC) is one of the world's largest startup pitching competitions. Here, participants will have a chance to win prizes and investment opportunities worth USD 3 million by presenting their innovative ideas and business plans.

In this year's competition, startups from Bangladesh will be selected in three categories – idea stage, early stage and growth stage.

Chairman of Global Entrepreneurship Network (GEN) Bangladesh and Daffodil International University. Sabur Khan said that innovation will build the Bangladesh of the future. And Entrepreneurship World Cup is the first step to realize that dream.

Speakers at the opening ceremony said that this World Cup is a huge opportunity for the Bangladeshi startup ecosystem. Here entrepreneurs can connect with investors, consultants and technology experts from around the world.

Global Entrepreneurship Network (GEN) Bangladesh, Bangladesh Venture Capital Limited, Knowledge Veil and Department of Innovation and Entrepreneurship of Daffodil International University are associated as organizers of this event. In addition, it has the support of other international partner organizations including Microsoft, Neom and Monshat.

In the discussion of the inaugural function, the participants called for increasing the startup practice by organizing awareness campaigns, seminars and workshops at the university level. Those interested in participating in the International Entrepreneurship World Cup must register. Detailed information about the participation process can be known from the respective website.
