511 entrepreneurs get BSCIC loans, over half of them new

By VOSB Desk16 November 2026 at 1:14 pm4 min read

Bangladesh Small and Cottage Industries Corporation (BCIC) is giving loans of Tk.14 crore 330 thousand to 511 entrepreneurs of cottage, micro, small and medium industries (CMSME) of the country. Among these entrepreneurs, 276 are new and 235 are old, that is, the number of new entrepreneurs is more than half of the entrepreneurs who got loan facility. There are 232 new and old women entrepreneurs and 279 men.

Industries Minister Khandkar Abdul Muktadir inaugurated this loan distribution program at the conference room of Bisik Bhavan in Tejgaon of the capital on Monday. Chairman of BCIC Benazir Ahmed presided over the event.

According to Bisik, this loan is being given with the aim of building new industrial establishments, increasing the production capacity of existing establishments and creating employment. Through this, new and existing industries will create employment opportunities for 2 thousand 448 people.

According to Bisik, the allocation for new 276 entrepreneurs is 6 crore 71 lakh 80 thousand taka. On the other hand, the old 235 entrepreneurs will get 7 crore 31 lakh 50 thousand taka. 6 crore 40 lakh 30 thousand taka allocated for women entrepreneurs. Male entrepreneurs will get 7 crore 63 lakh taka.

At the loan disbursement ceremony, Industries Minister Khandkar Abdul Muktadir said that Bisik can provide unsecured loans up to Tk 2 lakh at present. If it is more than 2 lakh rupees, you have to take security. At the district level, the loan amount is up to Tk 5 lakh and above that, BCIC can give up to Tk 20 lakh. It will be reviewed in the future, to what extent the amount of unsecured loans can be increased. The loan recovery rate is more than 90 percent.

BCIC Chairman Benazir Ahmed said, BCIC has been working relentlessly for decades in the development of small and cottage industry, creation of entrepreneurship and expansion of employment. More than half of the 511 entrepreneurs in today's program are new, opening up new possibilities and innovative initiatives to the country's economy.

How much debt in which region
According to BCIC data, loans are being distributed under these four regional offices – Dhaka, Chittagong, Rajshahi and Khulna. Among them, the largest loan disbursement plan is in Dhaka region. 181 entrepreneurs of this region will get 4 crore 79 lakh taka. 3 crore 73 lakh taka allocated for 123 people of Khulna region. Apart from this, 118 people of Rajshahi region will get 3 crore 35 lakh taka and 89 people of Chittagong region will get 2 crore 16 lakh 30 thousand taka.

Entrepreneurs of 14 industrial sectors will get benefits under this loan program of BCIC. Among them, maximum 220 are food and food industry entrepreneurs. Textile and textile industry entrepreneurs have the second highest number of 122 people, i.e. almost 67 percent of the total loan entrepreneurs belong to these two sectors. Apart from this, the entrepreneurs of light engineering, agriculture and agricultural products, forest and forest products, leather and leather products, jute and jute products, plastics, electronics, services and other sectors are also getting loans.

More Tk 350 crore will be given
At present three loan programs of BCIC are in operation. These are own fund loan program, 100 crore financial incentive package loan program and beekeeping development loan program through application of modern technology.

Under these programs, BSIC has planned to disburse another Tk 350 crore of loans in the next five years. The organization expects to create employment for about 20 thousand people.

Apart from this, BCCI has plans to create a loan fund of 5 thousand crore rupees for entrepreneurs inside and outside the industrial cities. At the same time, a plan has been taken to re-open sick and closed industries and create a special loan fund for the development of light engineering industries.
