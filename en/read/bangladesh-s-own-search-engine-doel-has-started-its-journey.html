Bangladesh's own search engine 'Doel' has started its journey

By VOSB Desk27 October 2026 at 0:12 pm2 min read

Another new feather has been added to the technology sector of Bangladesh through the 'Doel' search engine. Built entirely on indigenous technology, this platform brings modern search facilities to global users. 'Duel' is not just a simple search engine, but is providing a modern solution combining web search as well as Artificial Intelligence (AI).

Key Features of 'Duel' Search Engine:

  • Web Search & AI Answers: With 'Duel', users can view AI-generated relevant answers on the same screen alongside normal web searches.
  • Image and Video Search: Apart from general web information, the platform also has advanced image and video search facilities.
  • Live News Headlines: It displays real-time live Bengali news headlines based on user's IP or location.
  • Weather and Namaz Schedule: Based on location, users can get live weather updates and exact daily Namaz schedule.
  • Multilingual Support: The platform supports multiple languages ​​including Bengali and English, which makes it easy to use for all types of users, both domestic and foreign.

The biggest advantage is that there is no need to download any separate app to use Doyle. All its features can be enjoyed directly by visiting https://doelbd.xyz website from any browser of mobile phone, tablet, laptop or desktop.

Entrepreneur's Vision:

Deen Mohammad Nasim, the founder of 'Doyle' search engine, said, "Our goal is to show the world that Bangladesh is also capable of creating international quality search technology. We want to create a platform for local and international users that will bring the useful information of daily life very easily at hand."

Independent ventures like 'Doyle' have great potential in the technology ecosystem of a developing country like Bangladesh. Public or private investment and promotional support is needed to encourage such initiatives, so that they can gain a bigger foothold in the international arena.
