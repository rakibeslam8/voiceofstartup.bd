Nazma Akhter earns lakhs of taka per month by making fish chips

By VOSB Desk9 November 2026 at 8:29 am3 min read

Many people started online business at the beginning of Corona. He also made a profit. From that idea, in 2020, entrepreneur Nazma Akhter started an online business of girls' beauty products. Palangki launched a platform called Kanya for online business. At that time, he made a profit of Tk 50,000 in two months by selling beauty products. This profit changed Nazma Akhter's life.

Nazma Akhtar lived in Kalatali Adarshagram of Cox's Bazar with her family members. It is a difficult situation to run the family with the pension money of the retired father. So Najma started an online business out of a desire to do something herself. Once he plans to do business with new products. At that time there was a fig tree near the house. However, due to the lack of practice of eating this fruit, most of the time Peke was lying on the street. So he tried to make pickle with this fruit. Although later the fig tree was cut down. Therefore, due to lack of raw material, the production of this product is stopped.

Nazma Akhtar had to close two businesses in a row. Then he started looking for new business ideas on the internet and YouTube. At one point Jonaki watches a video of making insect powder. Then came the idea of ​​making sea fish powder. Failed several times in the first attempt. But once he started making and selling fish powder. Online sales are also increasing. While doing business, he faced major hurdles in the preservation of this product. So they do not make this product except during the specific fishing season.

Nazma Akhter plans to make fish chips in 2024 to grow the business. He tries to make chips with different species of marine and freshwater fish at his home. First try making tuna and poa fish chips. He succeeded in it after several attempts. Then he developed a brand of chips made from sea fish called 'Fiso Fish Chips'.

Currently, Najma has a factory and office in a three-room rented house. He made these fish chips there. Currently sells 700 kg of fish chips per month. Najma has a profit of more than lakhs of rupees per month excluding all expenses. Seven family members work in his company. Apart from this, 30 women have been trained in making fish chips.

Nazma Akhter said, "With these chips, children's eagerness to eat chips is fulfilled. On the other hand, nutritional needs are also met. Many people tell me to export these chips abroad. But I want to meet the needs of the country first. I will think about export later. In the next few years, I would like to go into the production of chips on a larger scale.'

Meanwhile, marketing and management is a major challenge in commercial production. Nazma Akhtar admits it. For this, BRAC Bank came forward to help Najma. In October of this year, he took the 'Amrai Tara' training from BRAC Bank. This training is helping to improve the management of the fish chips business and increase the sales of the product, said the entrepreneur. He said, 'Business is not only about producing products. Marketing and management should be done to reach the customer. How to take products to customers, how to implement any decision more effectively, I learned from BRAC Bank training. This three-day training has boosted my confidence a lot.

According to BRAC Bank, ``Amrai Tara'' training provides practical guidance to women entrepreneurs on business management, digital skills, financial literacy and market access. This training helps in starting, developing and expanding the business. Under 'Amrai Tara', more than 3 thousand women entrepreneurs have been trained in 20 districts of the country so far.
