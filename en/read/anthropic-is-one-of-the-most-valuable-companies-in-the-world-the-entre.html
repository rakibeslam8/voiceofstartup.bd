Anthropic is one of the most valuable companies in the world, the entrepreneurs are millionaires

By বাণিজ্য ডেস্ক21 November 2026 at 9:37 am4 min read

Artificial intelligence (AI) firm Anthropic has become one of the world's most valuable companies shortly after its IPO. After the start of trading in Nasdaq, the market value of the company is about 1 lakh 76 thousand billion dollars. As a result, the entrepreneurs of the company have become millionaires. Their combined wealth has increased to 6,370 million dollars.

AI chatbot Claude's parent company made its debut on the US Nasdaq stock exchange on Thursday. BBC news.

Anthropic's stock market debut is important for the AI ​​industry amid huge investment in the technology sector. Big tech companies around the world are investing huge amounts of money in developing AI technology and building infrastructure.

After trading on the stock market, Anthropic's share price exceeded the initial price of $83. But Anthropic will be under scrutiny from investors for the next few months. The big question now with AI is how much profit will ultimately be made by investing huge sums of money behind this technology.

Anthropic's share price fluctuated in the first few hours of trading. The price initially rose to $111.53. In this, the market value of the company increased to about 2 lakh billion dollars. However, at the end of the day, the share price fell to 97 dollars 17 cents. This volatility in the shares suggests that there is uncertainty among investors about the future of AI.

According to some investors, the first day's trading was successful. By the end of the day, Anthropic's share price was up nearly 17 percent. However, the picture for the overall technology sector was different. The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite Index ended the day down about 2.3 percent. Shares of major technology companies fell. Shares of Google's parent company Alphabet fell 2.9 percent. Amazon's share price fell about 3.9 percent.

The investment in infrastructure to develop AI technology is one of the most expensive ventures in the history of technology. In the last one year alone, some big tech companies have spent more than 1 trillion dollars in this sector. Concerns are growing whether the rapid expansion of investment is creating a bubble of hope for the AI ​​sector.

Richard Whittle, an economist at the University of Salford in the UK, said financial bubbles usually don't burst on the day of the biggest investment. When positive expectations about the future are called into question, there is a risk of a bubble bursting.

The concept of AI technology is not new. It has been in the works for decades. However, thanks to advanced computers and technological devices, it is now possible to analyze more data faster than ever before. The result is an AI chatbot that can speak human language. It can also speed up writing software code, creating digital images and searching for information on the Internet.

Anthropic made rapid progress based on this new way of using computers. The company, which started its journey just five years ago, is now a household name in the AI ​​sector. Claude Chatbot developed by them is also quite popular.

According to Ben Barringer, head of technology research at technology research firm Quilter Cheviot, Anthropic's share price could see big upside as it becomes the first pure AI company to list on the stock market. According to him, the initial investment enthusiasm will eventually decrease.

Barringer cited the example of Elon Musk's space technology company SpaceX's debut on the stock market. The company's share price rose nearly 10 percent on its first day. In this, SpaceX's market value reaches a record 220 billion dollars; But within a month the share price fell below the initial trading price. Since then, prices have mostly fallen further.

Entrepreneur's Poabaro
Anthropic's founders and co-founders are now poobaro as the shares soared on day one. CEO Dario Amodei and his six co-founders have a combined net worth of $1.63 billion. Average wealth of each person is about 2 thousand 330 million dollars. News Forbes.

Anthropic's initial three investors Eric Schmitt, Hussain Sajwani and Jan Talin have also collectively increased their fortunes by $1,440 million.

Investors were bullish on Anthropic's valuation from the start. In 2021, seven employees of OpenAI founded the company. The company's latest private equity round last May valued each share (after stock split adjustments) at $58.90. Anthropic was valued at $96,500 at the time.

Some investors expected the company's valuation to reach $2 trillion at the time of the IPO. However, the tech company's stock prices have fallen over the past month, dampening those expectations. Last week, Anthropic said it would price its shares in the IPO between $70 and $78. In this, the company's possible valuation stood at 1 lakh 30 thousand crores to 1 lakh 40 thousand crores. The share price was finally set at $83.
