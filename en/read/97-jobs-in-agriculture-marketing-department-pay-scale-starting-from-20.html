97 jobs in Agriculture Marketing Department, pay scale starting from 20 thousand

By VOSB Desk14 November 2026 at 10:06 am2 min read

A notification has been released for direct recruitment in the Directorate of Agriculture Marketing under the Ministry of Agriculture. The organization will hire a total of 97 people in 8 different categories. Interested eligible candidates can apply online.

97 recruitment circulars for 8 category posts have been published in the Directorate of Agriculture Marketing. Graduate degree holders can apply from 8th class pass according to post. Salary scale starts from Tk 20 thousand. Application will start from 16 November. Interested candidates can apply till 10 December 2026.

According to the information in the recruitment circular, maximum 39 candidates will be recruited for the post of Office Assistant-cum-Computer Numerologist. Apart from this, 38 people will be appointed as office assistants, 9 people as typists-cum-computer operators, 5 people as field and market inspectors and 3 people as accountants. The remaining three posts will be filled by one.

Post Name and Pay Scale—

  1. Field and Market Inspector—5 posts. Pay Scale 24 thousand 300 to 59 thousand 700 Tk (Grade-12).
  1. Typist-cum-Computer Operator—9 Posts. Pay Scale 24 thousand to 58 thousand taka (Grade-13).
  1. Computer Operator—1 post. Pay Scale 24 thousand to 58 thousand taka (Grade-13).
  1. Accountant—3 posts. Pay Scale 23 thousand 500 to 56 thousand 900 taka (Grade-14).
  1. Driver (Heavy)—1 post. Pay Scale 22 thousand 800 to 55 thousand 200 taka (Grade-15).
  1. Office Assistant-cum-Computer Numismatics—39 Posts. Pay Scale 21 thousand 900 to 52 thousand 900 Tk (Grade-16).
  1. Cashier—1 post. Pay Scale 21 thousand 900 to 52 thousand 900 Tk (Grade-16).
  1. Office Assistant—38 posts. Pay Scale 20 thousand to 48 thousand 400 taka (Grade-20).

Age Information—

Candidates age should be between 18 to 32 years as on 1-11-2026. But for the post of Serial No. 5 (Driver) age is acceptable from 18 to 35 years.

Application Process—

Interested candidates have to fill the application form online through the website. After submitting the application, the exam fee should be deposited through the Teletalk Prepaid SIM within the stipulated time.

Application Form

Application Deadline—

Application Start: 16 November 2026, 10 AM

Last date of application: 10 December 2026, 4 PM

Examination Fee—

Depending on the type of post, the fee ranges from Tk 56 to Tk 168 (including teletalk service charges).

  • Details of recruitment notification and application are available on the website of Directorate of Agriculture Marketing.
