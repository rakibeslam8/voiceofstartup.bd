The governor wants 35 percent of the bank's total loans to the SME sector

By বিশেষ প্রতিনিধি23 November 2026 at 11:13 am3 min read

According to the Governor of Bangladesh Bank, 35 percent of the total loans of commercial banks should be in the small and medium industries (SME) sector and 20 percent in the retail banking sector. Mostakur Rahman.

At the same time, he emphasized on bringing down the bank's loan to 40 percent in the corporate sector. The governor said, in the future, the investment money of the corporate institutions should be collected from the capital market.

Governor Mostaqur Rahman said these things in the discussion meeting in view of the monetary policy for the third quarter (January-March) of the current fiscal year 2026-27. This exchange meeting was held at Chattogram office of Bangladesh Bank on Friday.

In the meeting, the business leaders suggested to reduce the interest rate of the loan to reduce the cost of production. They also demanded to keep the exchange rate stable against the dollar.

Salim Rahman, Vice President of BGMEA, said that traders can handle the pressure of cost increase. But can't handle uncertainty. According to him, in the last few years, the energy crisis has arisen due to the effects of the Corona epidemic, the Russia-Ukraine war and the US-Iran war. The cost of production has increased a lot.

Chattogram Women's Chamber of Commerce and Industry President Abida Mostafa said that foreign investment will not come if the exchange rate of rupees against the dollar is not stable. In this regard, he suggested to take into consideration the experience of Malaysia, Singapore and Vietnam.

Regarding price inflation, Governor Mostaqur Rahman said that in order to reduce prices, the supply of products in the market should be increased along with the increase in production. He hopes that the 60 thousand crore Tk incentive package announced by Bangladesh Bank will play a role in this regard. He insisted on releasing the incentive money in the current financial year.

In response to the question of AM Mahbub Chowdhury, the senior vice president of Chattogram Metropolitan Chamber of Commerce and Industry, the governor said that the assistance of Bangladesh embassies and various organizations abroad is being taken to bring the export income which has not yet arrived in the country. Work is also underway to recover the smuggled money. Assets of various individuals have been identified and seized in several countries. The process of returning these assets to the country is underway.

Regarding the schedule of monetary policy formulation, the governor said that Bangladesh Bank has taken the initiative of formulating monetary policy every three months on the advice of the parliamentary standing committee to make the monetary policy more effective.

The meeting was chaired by Bangladesh Bank's Chattogram Office Executive Director Md. Hanif Mia. Deputy Governor was present. Habibur Rahman and Executive Director of Monetary Policy Department Imam Abu Saeed. The director of that department Mohammad Monirul Islam Sarkar gave a presentation on various instruments of monetary policy and their effectiveness.

Former President of Chattogram Chamber Amir Humayun Mahmud Chowdhury, Professor Mohammad Saiful Islam and Mohammad Tashlim Uddin of Economics Department of Chattogram University, Professor Mohammad Jamal Uddin of Finance Department and Professor Tunazzina Sultana of Marketing Department, Vice President of BKMEA Gaohar Siraj Jamil and others were present in the meeting.
