Separate digital shopping malls for men and women: Hevalier and Chivalier's revolution in e-commerce

By VOSB Desk4 November 2026 at 6:08 am3 min read

In the e-commerce sector of Bangladesh, when buyers are often confused in the crowd of common or generic marketplaces, Valy Next Venture has come up with an exceptional and specific solution. The company has developed two distinct and specialized AI-powered social e-commerce platforms—Hevalyfor men andShevalyfor women—to revolutionize the online shopping experience in the country.Startup Story and Entrepreneur's VisionWith everything under one umbrella on conventional e-commerce sites, fashion and lifestyle conscious shoppers often do not get the shopping experience they desire. This gap is expertly identified by Civil Engineer by profession and internationally recognized Project Management Professional Engineer N Amin (Engr N Amin, PMP). Combining engineering fine planning and modern project management skills with digital commerce, he founded Valley Next Ventures. His main aim was to create two dedicated digital shopping malls where male and female shoppers can shop on separate platforms according to their taste, needs and convenience.Why two separate platforms?

  • Hevaly: Men's shopping style is usually simple, fast and targeted. With that in mind, Hevaly (www.hevaly.com) is decorated with monochromatic and minimalist designs. Here men's clothing, grooming products, accessories and lifestyle products are curated in such a way that one can choose the best quality products in less time.
  • Shevaly:Shevaly (www.shevaly.com) specializes in a wide variety of women's fashion, beauty and lifestyle products. From apparels, cosmetics, jewelery to daily essential lifestyle products are available with a single click through aesthetic design and easy navigation.DTC & MTUC AI-Driven Social E-Commerce Model

Hevali & Shivally are not just ordinary online stores; Two platforms are working on the AI-driven social e-commerce model, DTUC (Direct to Consumer) and MTUC (Manufacturer to Consumer). As a result, it is possible to reduce the violence of middlemen and deliver genuine products directly from manufacturers and trusted vendors to consumers at the right price. Its key features include:

  1. Quality Control: Every product is subjected to strict quality checks before being added to the platform, which is playing a major role in eliminating the consumer confidence crisis in e-commerce.
  2. Entrepreneur and Creator Ecosystem: A powerful platform is being created for indigenous women entrepreneurs, vendors and content creators, especially through Shivally, where they can showcase their brands and products directly to millions of consumers.
  3. Technology-Enhanced Experience:Shoppers get the premium digital taste of modern shopping malls thanks to fast loading speeds, mobile-friendly interfaces and data-driven personalization.Entrepreneur's Idea

Engineer N Amin, founder and managing director of Valley Next Ventures said, "We didn't want to create just another online marketplace; we wanted the people of Bangladesh to experience a clean, trusted and premium shopping mall while shopping online. With Hevali and Shivali, we are building two distinct lifestyle destinations for men and women, where quality and trust are not compromised."

Future Plans
Already, Hevally and Shivally have created their own unique position in the brand directory and digital platforms of Bangladesh. In the future, Valley Next Ventures aims to engage more local manufacturers, expand the fastest delivery network and establish themselves as two of the leading trusted brands in fashion and lifestyle e-commerce across Bangladesh.
