5 types of punishment including disconnection of gas and electricity for non-payment of income tax return

By VOSB Desk7 November 2026 at 8:07 pm3 min read

You have not filed income tax return. Think what will happen to you. If the tax officials are strict, the fine can be up to 50 thousand rupees. Even the gas-electricity connection may be cut off.

There are 120 crore Tax Identification Number holders (TIN holders) in the country. About 50 lakh taxpayers file annual returns. The remaining 75 lakh taxpayers do not file returns.

As per law, it is not mandatory for all TIN holders to file returns. If there is taxable income, return has to be filed. And 45 types of public-private services have to be returned.

NBR officials feel that a large number of people have not filed returns despite having taxable income. Now let's see what is the penalty for not filing income tax return.

What happens if a return is not madeAccording to the Income Tax Act, there are five types of pitfalls taxpayers can face. These are –1. Penalty up to Rs 50,000Tax officials can take action under Section 266 of the Income Tax Act if they fail to submit the return within the specified time. According to this section, failure to file income tax return can be imposed at the rate of 10 percent of the tax levied on the last assessed income of the taxpayer. However, the minimum fine is 1000 taka and the maximum is 50 thousand taka. Apart from this, if the failure continues, an additional penalty can be imposed at the rate of Tk 50 per day. Failure to provide return information when requested in case of any investigation may result in a fine of Tk 50,000.2. Tax exemption not availableTax exemption will not be availed if returns are not filed on time. According to Section 174 of the Income Tax Act, the area of ​​tax exemption will be narrowed. You used to get tax every year by investing in government prescribed sectors with savings certificates. But you will not get this tax exemption if you do not file the return on time.3. Additional 2 percent taxAdditional tax at the rate of 2 percent on the tax levied every month will be payable if the return is not filed on time.4. Gas-electricity disconnected

Tax officials have been empowered to cut off the gas-electricity connection to your home if you fail to file income tax returns on time. Even if the industrial owners do not file income tax returns of their establishments, then they can disconnect the gas-electricity connection.

Not only electricity - disconnection, but any state services including gas, water can be disconnected.

5. Trouble getting paidFiling of income tax returns has been made mandatory with a limit of prescribed higher grade (Class I Officers) officers in government-private sector jobs. Therefore, if the return is not given on time, there may be complications in getting the salary. A copy of the return deposit is requested from the office, otherwise it is announced that the salary will be deducted. The same rule applies to mid-level officers in the private sector.When is the deadline for returns

Returns can be given throughout the year from the current financial year. First three months (July-September) return tax exemption available. If the return is filed between October and December, only the tax due will be paid. Tax and penalty will be levied if returns are filed from January to June.

Therefore, only after the end of the fiscal year, penal action will be taken including return-related penalties, additional taxes.
