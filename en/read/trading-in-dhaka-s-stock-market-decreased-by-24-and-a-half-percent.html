Trading in Dhaka's stock market decreased by 24 and a half percent

By নিজস্ব প্রতিবেদক26 November 2026 at 4:51 pm2 min read

The country's main stock market, Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE), saw a drop of Tk 300 crore or 24.5 percent in trading in a single day. Today, on Monday, the trading in the Dhaka market decreased again and fell below a thousand crore rupees. At the end of the day, the transaction volume was Tk 921 crore. 1 thousand Tk 220 crore were traded in this market yesterday on Sunday.

In addition to the transaction, the index also fell. At the end of the day yesterday, the main index of DSE, DSEX, decreased by 8 points to 5 thousand 557 points.

People related to the market say that the biggest role behind the decrease in indices and transactions in the Dhaka market yesterday was the fall in the share price of the banking sector. Due to fall in prices, the transactions of companies in this sector have also decreased. 14 out of 31 banks traded in Dhaka market today have decreased in price. Prices increased in 6 and remained unchanged in 11.

According to the report of the brokerage house Lankabangla Securities, 6 of the 10 companies whose shares played the biggest role in the decline of the index in the Dhaka market yesterday were banks. These are Islami Bank, Pubali Bank, BRAC Bank, Southeast Bank, Prime Bank and Mutual Trust Bank. The DSEX index fell by more than 8 points on the day due to the decline in the share prices of these six banks.

Meanwhile, although there was an excess of banks in the market of Dhaka on Sunday, it was interrupted yesterday. Banks were among the top 10 companies traded on DSE that day. The two banks are BRAC Bank and City Bank. Transactions of both the banks decreased from the previous day. BRAC Bank's transaction volume in the Dhaka market yesterday was Tk 16 crore, while City Bank's transaction volume was around Tk 14 crore.

People related to the market say that the share prices of some companies including banks increased on Sunday. This creates a tendency among investors to take profits. As a result, selling pressure was high in the market yesterday.
