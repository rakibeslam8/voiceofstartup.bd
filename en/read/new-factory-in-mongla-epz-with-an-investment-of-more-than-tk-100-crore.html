450 people will be employed in the new factory in Mongla EPZ with an investment of over Tk 100 crore

By নিজস্ব প্রতিবেদক20 November 2026 at 9:30 pm2 min read

Domestic company Blue Mangrove Packaging Limited will set up a factory at Mongla Export Processing Zone (EPZ) to manufacture a variety of packaging, labels and ancillary products for the apparel industry.

For that purpose, the company will invest 80 lakh 70 thousand US dollars. Its amount in Bangladeshi currency is more than 100 crore Tk 67 lakh. The new factory will employ 450 people.

Blue Mangrove Packaging signed a land lease agreement with the Bangladesh Export Processing Zone Authority (BEPZA) on Thursday to set up the factory. The agreement signing ceremony was held at Bepza Complex in the capital. Bepza informed this information in a press release.

According to Bepza, the proposed factory of Blue Mangrove Packaging plans to produce 1 crore 95 lakh kg of garment accessories annually. These products are used in garment packaging, product identification, price information display and supply management.

These products include polybags and sheets, gum tape, scotch tape, packing tape, masking tape, various cartons, hang tags, price tags, label paper, header cards, barcode stickers, numbering labels, radio frequency identification (RFID) labels and self-adhesive stickers.

The agreement was signed by Organization Member (Engineering) Abdullah Al Mamun on behalf of Bepza and Managing Director (MD) Tajuddin Ahmed on behalf of Blue Mangrove Packaging.

At that time, Bepza Executive Chairman Major General Mohammad Moazzem Hossain, Executive Director Lt. Col. Md. Mohsin Hasan, Mosihuddin Bin Mesbah, ASM Anwar Parvez and other senior officials of the organization were present. At that time, the executive chairman of Bepza urged the company of Jashore and Patuakhali to invest in EPZ as well.

39 factories are operational in Mongla EPZ

According to Bepza data, 39 industries are running in Mongla EPZ till October this year. Apart from this, the construction work of six more industrial establishments is under implementation.

A total of USD 1,704,400 worth of goods have been exported from Mongla EPZ till October this year. At the same time, the actual investment in this EPZ is 27 crore 19 lakh 80 thousand dollars.

According to Bepza data, 16 thousand 167 Bangladeshis are working in the industry of Mongla EPZ so far.
