Nvidia won't bring back Jensen Huang, doubles revenue in third quarter

By বিবিসি19 November 2026 at 4:16 pm4 min read

The company has no plans to bring back Nvidia co-founder and former CEO Jensen Huang. After his resignation, the chip company said business operations are normal despite the change in leadership. Meanwhile, Nvidia reported record revenue and profit. In this first quarter, the company's income exceeded 115 billion or 11 thousand 5 billion dollars.

In the third quarter ended October 25, Nvidia's revenue was 115.8 billion or 11,580 million dollars; Which is double compared to the same period of the previous year. At this time, the company's profit doubled to 65 billion or 6,500 million dollars. Nvidia shares rose 4.8 percent in after-hours trading on Tuesday following the release of the financial report. However, at that time of the last quarter, Huang was still in the company.

This is Nvidia's first financial report since the resignation of Jensen Huang. He resigned after allegations of extramarital affairs surfaced against him. Among these are allegations of having a relationship with a suspected Chinese spy.

Meanwhile, Huang himself said on Tuesday that he is interested in returning to the leadership of Nvidia. He said he would feel honored if it was taken back. World's richest man Elon Musk and even US President Donald Trump supported him. However, chairman of the company's board of directors Harvey Jones rejected this possibility. In a meeting with analysts, he said the board has no plans to reinstate Huang.

Nvidia's interim CEO Colette Kress, however, doesn't see cause for concern about the change in leadership. He said that the activities of the organization are going on normally. Partners, suppliers, employees—everyone is on Nvidia's side. In his words, "we can handle the situation."

Kress also said that in the past week, Nvidia's management has handled everything with a firm hand. There was no disruption in the work of various departments and projects.

Meanwhile, the bulk of Nvidia's total revenue comes from selling chips for data centers. In the last quarter, the income of this sector doubled to 107 billion or 10 thousand 7 billion dollars. The company said this trend of growth will continue. Their income may increase by 13 percent in the next quarter.

According to market analysts, Nvidia's financial report largely dispelled fears of a slowdown in demand in the AI ​​infrastructure sector. Daniela Hathorne, senior analyst at investment and trading platform Capital.com, said the report suggests that business activity has not slowed in Huang's absence; Rather, it has become stronger.

However, the big question is how much the departure of Huang will affect the company's strategic position in the long term. According to Hathorne, orders that came before Huang's departure are less likely to be affected. However, his absence could become crucial in future decisions on new products, partnerships and investments.

Meanwhile, Nvidia has stopped supplying chips to a data center in China. Interim CEO Colette Kress said they are no longer shipping these chips to China. This technology is part of Nvidia's advanced H200 processor. Huang had been lobbying the US administration for a long time to get approval to sell these chips to China. However, Kress did not explain the reason for the stoppage of supply.

Meanwhile, Nvidia is in the process of hiring a permanent CEO. Chairman Harvey Jones said that the help of recruiting organizations has been taken for that purpose. Potential candidates are being considered from both inside and outside the organization. However, the board of directors does not want to appoint anyone in a hurry.

Jones said the new chief executive must have the ability to deal with the challenges of advanced technology and national security. So finding the right person will take time.

On the other hand, Michael Monaghan of Nvidia's investment firm Founder ETFs thinks that Huang should be considered. According to him, it is necessary to wait for a full investigation report on Huang's activities rather than relying on allegations published in the media.

According to Monaghan, Nvidia's success is largely a result of Huang's leadership and the business structure he built. When an organization's iconic founder departs, the organization's unique vision and potential often fades.
