Threats of Internet-World Chaos with Meta's New AI Agent

By VOSB Desk3 November 2026 at 4:54 am1 min read

Meta recently unveiled a new AI agent named 'Muse' to ease users' daily tasks in the new era of artificial intelligence in the technology world. The AI ​​agent can automatically perform complex tasks like browsing the web, using Gmail, managing calendars and even shopping for everyday products online. However, technology experts have warned that such automated AI agents could create major disruptions and security threats to Internet infrastructure.

According to Meta, the Muse AI agent can automate various tasks for users. However, MIT associate professor Ramesh Bhaskar compared Muse's ability to automate various tasks to a self-driving car. According to him, there is a situation where hundreds of vehicles are left on the roads without any specific rules or traffic laws, which can cause great danger to the common people. In terms of security and privacy too, the Muse AI agent demands highly sensitive information and control from the user.

Patrick Wardell, co-founder of the Objective-C Foundation, expressed deep concern about the security flaws in the Muse AI agent. According to him, due to the flaw, anyone could have secretly accessed users' devices by taking full control of the Muse AI agent through a cyber attack. Although Meta Authority quickly addressed the security flaw, doubts remain about its commitment to protecting users' personal information.

According to security researchers and experts, new laws and structural changes are needed to regulate AI agents developed by various organizations. Otherwise the privacy and digital security of common users will be at extreme risk. Users may also have to pay a heavy price for taking advantage of AI agents.
