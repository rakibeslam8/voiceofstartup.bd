Musk will hire Jensen Huang, says it's an honor to hire him

By VOSB Desk13 November 2026 at 9:36 am3 min read

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang isn't having a good time. His situation is complicated after he admits to an extramarital affair with Alice Huang, an accused Chinese spy. According to various reports, the company's board of directors is planning to remove the founding CEO of Nvidia soon. Elon Musk has indicated to stand by him in such a situation.

An Xer (formerly Twitter) user urged Elon Musk to hire Jensen Huang as the head of Nvidia as his future became uncertain. Elon Musk agreed. In response, he wrote, "It is honorable." India Today's news

Earlier, Elon Musk expressed support for Jensen Huang. After Huang admitted to an extramarital affair for the first time in an internal message sent to Nvidia employees, Musk expressed his support for Huang in a post. In the post, he said Huang was "incredibly stupid" but had no bad intentions. In the post requesting Huang's job, Huang was described as one of the best entrepreneurs of this generation and an essential person for the US artificial intelligence sector. In response, Musk said, he agreed.

Elon Musk and Jensen Huang's relationship has been going on for a long time. They have worked together for several years. In 2016, when Musk was involved with OpenAI, he bought Nvidia's first artificial intelligence-based supercomputer, the DGX-1. Huang even went to the OpenAI office in San Francisco to hand over the computer to Musk.

Not only Elon Musk, US President Donald Trump also spoke for Jensen Huang. Earlier this week, he referred to the head of Nvidia as an "honorable person". Trump said Huang has done a great job for the United States. He also said, 'All I know is that he is a good person. I think so. I have not heard that he has done anything wrong. Trump is said to have known about Huang's relationship with Alice for about a year. However, he did not take action in this regard.

Multiple extramarital affairs

Huang was involved in extramarital affairs before. According to a New York Times report, he also had an extramarital affair with Kimberly Powell, vice president of Nvidia's healthcare division. In March this year, during a conference, they were seen in a close relationship at a hotel in San Jose, according to the report. Their moment was videotaped. The person who captured the video was also offered to sign a non-disclosure agreement in this regard.

Meanwhile, the company's board of directors is considering removing Huang as Nvidia's share price continues to fall. Nvidia is working with an outside firm to find a possible successor. There is also the possibility of appointing an interim CEO until a permanent CEO is appointed.

Jensen Huang has not yet commented on these recent allegations. His only public statement on the matter was an internal message sent to Nvidia employees. There he admitted to cheating with his wife. Huang wrote, 'I have been unfaithful to my wife. My behavior has hurt him and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I deeply regret.' But he did not mention Alice Huang's name in that message.

Meanwhile, Nvidia is investigating Alice Huang's relationship with Jensen Huang. It is also being investigated whether there is any risk to national security in this incident. Senior Republican members of the US House of Representatives have also asked for detailed information from Huang. On the other hand, China rejected the allegations of espionage as 'baseless speculation'.
