It started with a capital of 10 taka, now the monthly income is lakhs of taka

By VOSB Desk1 November 2026 at 11:11 am4 min read

Irene Parveen started tailoring with only 10 rupees capital in the world of poverty. Now he is the owner of two big clothing manufacturing companies called 'Ananya Boutiques' and 'Ananya Exclusive'. He overcame poverty and achieved success through relentless efforts. Apart from business, he is serving poor and helpless people through various social organizations.

Irene Parveen is the daughter of late Idris Mia, a member of the police in Kharampur area of ​​Sherpur city. As there were 9 brothers and sisters, there was tension in their family. Even if the father received government ration, it would not have been enough. For that, his late mother Khodeja Begum used to earn some money by making clothes for local girls. Irene Parveen learned sewing from her mother.

Irene Parveen passed SSC in 1983 and got admission in higher secondary. Then she got married to Farid Uddin, a small businessman of Sherpur. Shortly after the marriage, Irene's father-in-law died. As a result, all the responsibilities of the family fell on her husband Farid Uddin. Farid was struggling to run a family of 14-15 people depending on a small manihari shop. So Irene wanted to help the family by doing the sewing she learned from her mother. But the in-laws did not want to accept that the wife of the house would work as a tailor. In the end, husband Farid Uddin gave permission on the condition that he married first. Agreeing to this, Farid bought a sewing machine in installments. Then Irene started the journey with a capital of 10 taka in cash. Day by day the work orders are also increasing. Then he thought of something bigger.

In 1998, he received training from the Directorate of Youth Development. Along with this, he took a loan of 25 thousand rupees and set up a sewing training center in a house in Digharpar area of ​​the city. There she trained women on block-batik work. Currently, the number of employees in his organization is 200. For becoming a successful woman entrepreneur, she was awarded Shrestha Jayita at the district level in the category of economically independent women in 2018. Earlier in 2002, Irene received an award from the Department of Youth Development as a successful self-worker.

Recently, at the Ananya Sewing Training and Garment Making Center established by Irene Parveen in Digharpar area of ​​Sherpur municipality, it can be seen that women workers are making three-piece, fatwa, punjabi, saree, nakshikantha, koti, table cover, sofa cushion, curtain, table runner, tissue box, chadar etc. These products are sold in two showrooms named Ananya Boutiques and Ananya Exclusive in Sherpur Paur New Market of Irene Parveen. Irene said that she participated in the SME fair in Dhaka every year to attract buyers by displaying these products.

Irene Parveen told Prothom Alo, "I started tailoring with just 10 taka capital. At present, the amount of investment in the business is around 50 lakhs. Excluding expenses, the income is about one lakh taka per month.

Preeti Parveen, a female worker there, told Prothom Alo, "I have received training from Parveen Apa for a long time. Now he earns about 40 thousand rupees a month by doing various clothes making jobs. I became self-reliant by learning work from Parveen Apa.'

Another person named Jasmin Begum said that her husband is a sugarcane worker. The husband's income does not support the family. So he worked here. Now he earns 5-6 thousand rupees. As a result, it becomes easier to run the family.

Successful woman entrepreneur Irene Parveen is also involved in various social and service organizations. Currently, she is the Organizing Secretary of Sherpur District Branch of Bangladesh Mahila Parishad, Vice President of Sherpur District Parliament of Udichi Artist Group and Director of Sherpur Women Chamber. Through these organizations and personal initiatives, he supports the needy people in various ways including legal assistance. Besides, works to prevent violence against women, empower women, implement women's rights.

District Women's Affairs Officer. Lutful Kabir told Prothom Alo, Irene Parveen is a successful woman entrepreneur and organizer. Through her organization and work, other women are also becoming self-reliant. He is also playing an important role in creating new entrepreneurs.
