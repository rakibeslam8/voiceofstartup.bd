Cenbank selects 640 young entrepreneurs for collateral-free loans of up to Tk20 lakh

By @tbsnewsdotnet26 November 2026 at 9:09 pm1 min read

Selected applicants from 64 upazilas across eight districts can receive loans of up to Tk20 lakh under the central bank's Uddokta programme.

TBS Report

26 November, 2026, 08:00 pm

Last modified: 26 November, 2026, 08:03 pm

The Bangladesh Bank has published the list of 640 young entrepreneurs selected for collateral-free loans of up to Tk20 lakh under its Uddokta programme.

The list was published on the central bank's website today (26 November).

The selected entrepreneurs are from 64 upazilas across eight districts: Manikganj, Feni, Naogaon, Jhenaidah, Bhola, Habiganj, Rangpur and Netrokona.

Ten entrepreneurs have been selected from each of the eight upazilas in every district, bringing the total number of selected applicants to 640.

In a notice, Bangladesh Bank said applications are still being accepted from the remaining 56 districts.

Interested young entrepreneurs can submit their applications at any scheduled bank branch in their respective upazilas.
