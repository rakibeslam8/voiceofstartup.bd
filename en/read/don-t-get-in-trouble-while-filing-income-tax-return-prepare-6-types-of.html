Don't get in trouble while filing income tax return, prepare 6 types of information in advance

By বিশেষ প্রতিবেদক21 November 2026 at 9:49 am2 min read

The time for filing income tax returns for individual taxpayers is on. There is no penalty if income tax returns are filed by December 31. Whatever tax comes, must be paid. All taxpayers will have to file returns online this year.

Taxpayers do not know what information is required to prepare the return. As a result, it stopped in the middle of making the return. Therefore, it is beneficial to know what information is needed in advance. It is possible to prepare in advance.

Do not hide your income, expenses, assets, liabilities, donations and gifts while preparing your income tax return. Any omission of information may lead to complications in the future.

Now let's see what information is needed.

1. Income Statement

In this part of the return, your income usually comes from any sector. Information required for this-

  1. Job or salary
  1. business
  1. Rent a house or flat
  1. Agricultural income
  1. Bank interest or profit
  1. Profit on savings bonds
  1. Income of FDR and DPS
  1. Any other income.

2. Cost Statement

In the income tax return, not only the income must be reported, but also the expenses must be reported. You should also know what your expenses are throughout the year. For this you need to know the cost of some sectors. For example –

  1. Daily expenses of the family
  1. house rent
  1. Child's education expenses
  1. Medical and travel expenses
  1. Other personal and family expenses

3. Statement of Your Assets

The amount of assets in your name or ownership should be disclosed. If you have more than 50 lakhs of assets, you must submit a statement of assets. All that needs to be said there is –

  1. Land and flats
  1. Gold and ornaments
  1. Furniture
  1. Laptops and computers
  1. mobile phone
  1. motorcycle
  1. Private car
  1. savings account
  1. FDR and DPS etc
  1. Bank account money
  1. Other immovable and immovable assets.

Nominee of savings certificate and bank account does not mean the owner of the entire money, what will the heirs do

4. Accounts PayableIncome tax returns also have to account for your liabilities. For this, if there is any bank loan, personal loan, any other financial liability, its details should be known.5. Donations and GiftsIf you donate money or assets to someone, you must declare it on your income tax return. For example, if you have received land, money, gold or any other asset as a gift from someone, it must be shown in the return. Gifts from parents, spouse, children and siblings are not taxed.6. Other Required Information

While filing income tax return, know the details of investment, income tax deducted at source, investment eligible for tax rebate and other applicable financial information.
