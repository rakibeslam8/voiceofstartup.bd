Jensen Huang's wealth falls by Tk 2.5 lakh crore in five days

By VOSB Desk17 November 2026 at 9:25 am3 min read

Jensen Huang, the former CEO of Nvidia, has faced a huge financial loss due to controversy over personal relationships and a series of accusations. In the last five days, his wealth has decreased by 20.3 billion dollars or 2 thousand 30 million dollars. As a result, he has dropped from number 8 to number 13 in the list of the world's top richest people.

Huang Jensen's loss of wealth in the last five days is about two and a half lakh crores in Bangladeshi currency at the current market rate. This amount is much more than the money spent in the Annual Development Program (ADP) of Bangladesh. That is, the amount of money spent by the Bangladesh government on development projects throughout the year, Huang's wealth has decreased in the last five days. In the last financial year, the government spent 1 lakh 41 thousand crores on ADP.

According to the latest Forbes Real Time Billionaires List, Huang's wealth as of November 13 stood at 141.9 billion or $14,900 million. In one day, his wealth decreased by 2.8 billion dollars or 1.97 percent. In the list of rich people whose wealth has increased or decreased, yesterday he was in the third position in the list of wealth loss. Friday is the last working day in the United States. Stock market is closed on Saturday and Sunday.

Earlier in the week, before the market opened on Monday, Jensen Huang's net worth was $162.2 billion or $16,220 million. Accordingly, in five days, his wealth decreased by about 12.5 percent.

The main reason for Huang's decline in wealth is Nvidia's share price. After the controversy about his personal life came to light, the shares of the company raised concern among investors. A complicated situation arose after reports of Huang's extramarital affair with a woman accused of spying for China emerged. Later, allegations of his relationship with two other female Nvidia executives surfaced.

Huang admitted his extramarital affair to staff last Sunday. The woman's name is Alice Huang. He is a former executive of Singapore-based data center company Megaspeed. The United States suspects that China's Ministry of State Security employed him to collect information, although the espionage allegations have not yet been proven.

Before this, at the end of last October, the New York Times published the news of Alice's relationship with Huang. Nvidia's board of directors then launched an investigation into the matter. Another New York Times report on Wednesday alleged that Huang also had an affair with Nvidia executive Kimberly Powell. Then on Thursday, Reuters reported that Huang had a close relationship with another Nvidia employee.

Amidst the turmoil, Huang resigned as Nvidia's president and CEO on Thursday. He also resigned from the board of directors. He has led this company for 33 years. Following his resignation, the company's chief financial officer, Colette Kress, has been named interim CEO.

In 1993, Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowski and Curtis Priem founded Nvidia. The company was listed on the stock market in 1999. Huang owns about 3.5 percent of Nvidia. As a result, when the company's share price rises or falls, Huang's personal wealth naturally increases or decreases.

At the beginning of last October, Nvidia's market value rose to a maximum of 5.8 trillion or 580 thousand billion dollars. Analysts then estimated that the company's market value could exceed $6 trillion before the end of the month. However, volatility in the artificial intelligence sector, weak financial reports from some tech companies and an increase in investor selling after the US mid-term elections weighed on Nvidia's shares. This incident of Huang is connected with him.
