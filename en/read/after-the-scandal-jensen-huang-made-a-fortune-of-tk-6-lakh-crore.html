After the scandal, Jensen Huang made a fortune of Tk 6 lakh crore

By নিজস্ব প্রতিবেদক24 November 2026 at 6:00 pm3 min read

Jensen Huang, the former chief executive officer (CEO) of chip maker Nvidia, has seen his net worth plummet following a romance scandal. His position in the list of the top rich is gradually going backwards.

Jensen Huang's extramarital affair with a woman was revealed in the media. Then on November 9, Nvidia authorities began an investigation. Since then, Nvidia's share price has started to decline. Immediately, Jensen Huang's asset value dropped.

According to a report of Forbes magazine, since November 9, the value of his wealth has decreased by 47.9 billion or 4 thousand 790 million dollars. During this period, his net worth decreased by 29.5 percent. To put it more simply, his wealth in Bangladeshi currency has decreased by about 6 lakh crore taka, which is about two-thirds of Bangladesh's entire annual budget. The budget of Bangladesh for the current financial year is Tk 9 lakh 38 thousand crores. Jensen Huang dropped to 17th position on Forbes' list of the richest people. Ahead of him are Latin America's richest man Carlos Slim Helu and his family, Microsoft's Bill Gates and Changpeng Zhao, former CEO of cryptocurrency exchange Binance.

At the end of Friday, Jensen Huang's assets stood at 110.1 billion dollars or 11 thousand 10 million taka.

why lotus

The main reason for Jensen Huang's decline in wealth is Nvidia's share price. After the controversy about his personal life came to light, the shares of the company raised concern among investors. A complicated situation arose after reports of Huang's extramarital affair with a woman accused of spying for China emerged. Later, allegations of his relationship with two other female executives of Nvidia surfaced.

Huang later admitted to his extramarital affair. The woman's name is Alice Huang. He is a former executive of Singapore-based data center company Megaspeed. The United States suspects that China's Ministry of State Security employed him to collect information, although the espionage allegations have not yet been proven.

Before this, at the end of last October, the New York Times published the news of Alice's relationship with Huang. Nvidia's board of directors then launched an investigation into the matter. Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's president, CEO and board of directors following the investigation.

What about nvidia

According to Forbes magazine, Nvidia's third quarter financial results were quite good. The company's earnings beat analysts' forecasts and its own forecast last August. Earnings per share (EPS) stood at $2.68, beating analysts' expectations. The company's data center business earned $107.6 billion or $10,760 million.

In a conference after the release of financial results last Tuesday, the chairman of Nvidia's board of directors said that bringing Jensen Huang back to the position of CEO is not being considered. Recruitment firm Heidrick & Struggles has been tasked with finding a permanent CEO. In this case, candidates will be sought from both inside and outside the company.

Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Colette Kress took over as the company's interim CEO after Jensen Huang stepped down. Last Tuesday, he spoke for the first time at the post-financial results conference.

Nvidia's share price has fallen almost consistently over the past two weeks. Among them, the share price increased on Wednesday. CoreWeave's strong financial results had a positive impact on AI sector shares that day before Huang's resignation.
