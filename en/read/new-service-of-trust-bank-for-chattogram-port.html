New service of Trust Bank for Chattogram port

By বাণিজ্য ডেস্ক26 November 2026 at 4:49 pm1 min read

Trust Bank has launched a new revenue collection and payment automation service called 'Trustport Connect' with Chattogram Port Authority. The new service was officially launched recently with the motto 'Smarter Transaction, Stronger Trade' in front. Trust Bank has informed this information in a notification.

It is said in the circular that Rear Admiral SM Moniruzzaman, Chairman of Chattogram Port Authority, was the chief guest at the event organized on the occasion of launching the new service. Trust Bank's Managing Director (MD) and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Ahsan Zaman Chowdhury were also present at the event along with top officials of both institutions. After signing the agreement at the ceremony it was officially handed over between the two parties.

The notification also informed that the signing ceremony was attended by top officials of Trust Bank and Chattogram Port Authority as well as representatives of customs authorities, C&F agents, shipping agents and various port-related organizations.

According to Trust Bank, port users can now do all financial transactions of the port more quickly, easily and transparently through this new digital platform. As a result, this service will play an important role in the digital transformation of the country's overall trade system as the speed of import-export activities will increase.
