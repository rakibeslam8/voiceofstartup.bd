From Rental Mess to Successful IT Institute: 'LifeSkills IT' and Redwan Ahmed's Inspiring Story of Entrepreneurship

By VOSB Desk3 November 2026 at 0:05 pm3 min read

At the beginning of any successful journey is a simple vision, limited resources and indomitable will. In 2023, the path of 'Lifeskills IT Institute' (Lifeskills IT Institute) started from a rented mess (Bachelor Room) in Badda, Dhaka. The objective was very clear—to make practical IT education and professional skill development more accessible to the youth of Bangladesh.

The company's founder and CEO Redwan Ahmed started the venture with just a vision and the courage to take risks without any huge capital, established infrastructure or a large business network.

Overcoming Obstacles to Dreams
Building an IT institute from scratch was not an easy task at all. Managing operations, attracting students, finding competent mentors and maintaining the quality of education—every step was a new challenge.

According to Redwan Ahmed, "My aim was not just to sell traditional courses. I wanted to create a learning environment where students can acquire practical skills, learn about the real-world industry and get proper guidance outside the classroom."

Transition and Success from Mess to Corporate Office
Gradually this small enterprise started to grow. From a small rented house, LifeSkills IT Institute has today transformed into its own corporate office in Badda, Dhaka.

Over time the institute has expanded its activities to various career-oriented skill development programs like graphic design, digital marketing, web design and development, video editing. Having trained nearly 8,000 students till now, LifeSkills has built a strong learning community based on IT and practical education and freelancing guidance.

Not only at the individual level, the institute is also involved in various government training initiatives and institutional projects. They are also successfully contributing to various institutional workshops and professional training programs on Artificial Intelligence (AI) with educational institutions.

Future Plan: A Greater Ecosystem Beyond Traditional IT Training
While Bangladesh has huge potential in the digital economy, access to quality practical education and timely skill development is still a major challenge.

To meet this challenge, Redwan Ahmed does not want to confine Lifeskills IT Institute to the confines of a traditional training center. His vision is to build a larger 'learning ecosystem' where people from any background can learn professional skills and prepare themselves for the changing job market.

As part of future plans, they continue to expand online learning opportunities, add new educational categories and develop a modern digital learning platform.

Message for New Entrepreneurs
Redwan Ahmed considers this journey from rented mess to corporate office just the beginning of a bigger dream. According to him, "Being an entrepreneur is not about where you start or how much money you have. It's about having a vision, taking responsibility, adapting to challenges and constantly moving towards something bigger."
