Pet-parenting is now at your fingertips: Bangladesh's digital pet-care startup 'Petmamma'

By VOSB Desk5 November 2026 at 3:59 pm3 min read

People's love for pets is eternal. But when a beloved pet suddenly falls ill, finding the right doctor or ensuring quality care on an urgent basis is a difficult challenge for many pet parents. Inadequacy of veterinary services especially in areas outside Dhaka makes this problem more pronounced.

Inspired by one such personal experience, three friends created 'PetMamma'—a complete digital care platform for pets in Bangladesh.

Problem to SolutionThe idea of PetMamma was born out of the founders' own experiences. When it comes to finding the right doctor at the right time when their pet is sick, they realize that countless pet-parents across the country face similar problems every day. The journey of 'Petmamma' began with the idea of ​​how to solve these problems using technology.whose hand is held by Petmamma
Petmamma is a joint venture of three friends.

  • S.M. Emamul Hasan (CEO): A software engineer and project manager, working on strategy, product development and business growth.
  • Md. Sajal Khalifa (Managing Partner): A game designer with expertise in 3D game development as well as business operations and partner management. He leads Petmamma's Marketing and Partnerships.
  • Fahim Abrar (CTO):Experienced in mobile and web application development, this software engineer handles the entire technical side of Petmamma.Integrated Pet-Care Services at the Edge of Technology

Petmamma basically works through two separate applications—for customers and service providers.

  • PetMamma Customer App: Through this app pet-parents can take initial consultation with veterinary doctors over video call. In addition, it has clinic appointment booking, grooming services and pet-product shopping facilities. The biggest advantage is that users can store all their pet's information and medical records in one place.
  • PetMamma Business App:It is primarily designed for veterinarians and other service providers. Through this they can manage their professional profile and services digitally.Milestones & Recognition

As an independent startup, Petmamma has already achieved some significant milestones. The Petmamma team has successfully graduated from Cohort-5 of 'Orange Corners Bangladesh', an initiative of the Government of the Netherlands and supported by YY Ventures and SAJIDA Foundation.

After the soft-launch in May 2026, the company participated in the 'BASAVA Pet Summit' held in Cox's Bazar, which helped them build a strong network with the veterinary community.

Vision of the Future
Petmamma is currently self-funded (Bootstrapped). Their current goal is to reach more pet-parents and partner with vets and clinics to improve service quality. They are particularly committed to providing emergency support and veterinary services outside Dhaka.

The main vision of Petmamma is to prevent other pet-parents of the country from having to go through the same problems they faced when they started this initiative.

For more details about the platform visit: https://petmamma.com/
