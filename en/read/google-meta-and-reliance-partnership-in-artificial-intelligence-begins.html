Google, Meta and Reliance: Partnership in artificial intelligence begins with joint investment

By VOSB Desk3 November 2026 at 10:07 am3 min read

Two of the world's leading digital and technology companies Google and Facebook (now Meta) invested a large amount of money in India's leading industrial group Reliance Industries' digital services company Jio Platforms in 2020. They made the investment considering the potential of India's rapidly expanding digital economy, News Minute said.

That investment by Google and Facebook turned into a strategic partnership five years later. Now they have partnered to implement Reliance's plans for Artificial Intelligence (AI), Enterprise Technology and Digital Infrastructure sectors.

In 2020, Jio Platforms raised a total of Rs 152,000 crore from 13 investors. In return, the company gives a total of 32.94 percent shares to those investors. According to PhoneArena, Facebook has invested Rs 43,574 crore in Jio Platforms. And Google's investment amount is 33 thousand 737 crore rupees.

Reliance's telecom company Jio has played an important role in making mobile internet access affordable in India.

Through that investment five years ago, the foundation of the financial relationship of Jio Platforms and Reliance Industries with Google and Facebook was created. But with artificial intelligence (AI) becoming a key competitive area in the technology sector, Reliance's vast business footprint, communications and infrastructure have created opportunities for deeper collaboration.

The launch of a new initiative called 'Reliance Intelligence' was announced at Reliance's Annual General Meeting (AGM) held on 29 August 2025. It aims to build AI infrastructure, platforms and services for consumers, businesses and government agencies.

Meta's partnership with Reliance will take the form of a joint venture to provide AI services at the enterprise level. Initially the two institutions have jointly committed to invest around Rs 855 crore. Reliance will own 70 percent and Meta 30 percent.

The joint venture is planned to be based on the Mater Lama AI model. It aims to create AI platforms and easy-to-use technology tools for businesses. These tools can be used for various purposes including sales, marketing, customer service, financial management and information technology.

On the other hand, Google's main area of ​​cooperation with Reliance is cloud infrastructure. In August 2025, Reliance and Google Cloud announced plans to build an AI-centric cloud region in Jamnagar, Gujarat, India. This infrastructure will be built exclusively for Reliance.

As per the plan, Reliance will be responsible for infrastructure design, construction and power supply. Google Cloud, on the other hand, will provide AI computing capabilities and cloud technology. Jio will provide high speed and large capacity communication system. Besides, Reliance's clean energy initiative will help meet the power needs of the infrastructure.

The goals of the two partnerships are different but complementary. Meta provides AI models and software development capabilities for businesses. And Google is providing cloud computing and AI infrastructure. Reliance has the industry experience, connectivity, fuel capacity and customer base to deploy these technologies on a large scale.

This shift from shareholder to strategic partner is a reflection of a larger transformation in India's technology sector. A 2020 investment saw two global tech giants (Google and Meta) partner in a fast-growing digital business. In that sequence, they have now come together to create and deploy AI products and infrastructure.

How successful this partnership will be in the long run will depend on the implementation of the plan, the cost-effective use of the technology and its acceptance among customers. However, through these initiatives, it is clear that Reliance's relationship with global technology companies is no longer limited to financial investment. Rather, this relationship is moving towards building India's AI ecosystem.
