Only happy stories fill Fahmida's diary now

By VOSB Desk16 November 2026 at 1:49 pm7 min read

When the girl came from school, she said, she will return by rickshaw. But I don't even have 10 rupees. The baby boy says, he will eat the banana. I said to my daughter, I have no money. I brought my son home saying that I will buy it from the front. I was upset. God, why are we suffering so much!

Fahmida Jannat (Happy) of Sylhet wrote this bitter experience in her diary on June 25, 2014. An era later, on June 26, 2026, Fahmida wrote in her Facebook status, 'Every hardship, every engagement and every small success teaches me to be stronger. Love your work. Combine honesty and hard work. Insha Allah, success will knock on the door one day.

Success has also knocked on Fahmida's door. He is now independent. He said, 'I was able to survive because I was patient, honest and hardworking. Otherwise, I would have floated like straw!'

Sylhet Women's Chamber of Commerce and Industry president Lubana Yasmin commented that Fahmida's life story "beats the story of cinema". She told Prothom Alo that her husband suddenly left Fahmida with two children. With constant hard work and concentration, he finally succeeded. The struggling life of this successful woman entrepreneur is now an inspiration to others.

Story of a life thrown away
Fahmida lives on the second floor of a house in Mirabazar, Sylhet city. On the evening of June 9, I spoke to him sitting at that house. At one point of speaking, Fahmida showed some diaries in front of the reporter. In these diaries, he has written the story of his abandoned life on page after page.

Fahmida allowed this reporter to read the diaries. From reading the diary and talking to her, it is known that Fahmida had to go through a lot of pain, financial hardship, family tension and hard struggle. How he became successful is also in his diary.

Talking to Fahmida, it is known that her house is in Biyanibazar, Sylhet. He was married into a highly educated and Banedi family of Dargagate, Sylhet city. The groom is a bank officer, owns a house in the city—that's why his family members married the high school graduate in 2007. She spends the first two and a half years well in her husband's family. Suddenly the in-laws started misbehaving. At one point the husband also started behaving badly.

Fahmida said, her first child came in the third year after marriage. On the advice of her in-laws, she took her daughter directly from the hospital to her father's house after birth. Even though he stays at his father's house day after day, his husband hesitates to bring him to his in-laws' house. Devar, Nandra also forbade him to come. Six months passed like this. One day he forcibly came to his in-laws home with the child. At that time, he stayed in a separate house under construction of his in-laws. The in-laws started harassing him constantly. Once a second child was born. Although the second child was a boy, the husband's mind did not change; Instead, he quit his job and got into debt. He got six to seven lakh taka from his father's house and relatives and paid some of the debt. To pay the debt, he sold at least 14 gold ornaments from his father's house and in-laws' house. In August 2014, her husband left Sylhet and moved to Dhaka saying that he would work. In the beginning there was some communication. Later the husband stopped contact. At one stage Devar, Nondera increased the mental torture. They pressured her to leave her husband's family and go to her father's house.

Describing that time, Fahmida wrote in her diary, 'The boy cries a lot for milk. But there is not even a single money in hand.' He wrote the incident of another day in his diary like this, 'Today is the first day of fasting. Couldn't make any market. My forehead is bad. What should I do?'

On November 25, 2016, Fahmida wrote in her diary, 'I feel very bad today. I don't have any money. The boy will eat fish and meat, he is very serious. So is the girl. what to do I am crying alone.

How Happiness Came
Fahmida said, "I learned sewing and block batik while studying in school. I used that in 2017. I used to send my daughter to school and I used to bring various sewing jobs to other parents and do them at home. This is how earnings start. Gradually, I started sewing the clothes of many people, including neighbors, in exchange for money. At some point, I started a three-piece business with financial help from my brothers and sisters. At that time, the in-laws used to taunt him with various epithets including 'Selai Apa'. Unable to suppress him, the in-laws took another route. Apart from threatening to leave the in-laws, they often harassed and harassed a group of people at night by arranging 'swami's creditors', including disconnecting water and gas connection lines. At that time, the police also regularly came to the house, accusing her husband of a 'check fraud case'. The children were afraid of it. Due to constant fear and frightening environment, mental diseases of girls and boys started to appear. He himself suffered a brain stroke in 2019. Later in 2021, he rented a house in Mirabazar of the city. He started a new life by enrolling his children in an educational institution in that area.

Fahmida increased the scope of the business by coming to the rented house. He launched an online page called 'Happy Kitchen' and a YouTube channel and started providing home-made healthy and delicious food. Akhni, rice-curry, biryani, pitha, cake and more than half a hundred dishes were cooked and sold. The reputation of providing healthy food is increasing day by day. Now he regularly delivers food to various institutions and events.

About two years ago Fahmida delivered cooked food daily for Tk 10,000 per month to a popular online food and grocery delivery company. He used to prepare food for the online food delivery company as per the demand. Later, when his own online page became popular, he quit that job. Now his company is well known.

"Especially from 2014 to 2018, Fahmida did not tell the people of her father's house about the unbearable suffering she endured due to lack of food. Fahmida said, 'I used to take my daughter to school on foot at that time. The girl was serious about getting into the rickshaw, I could not make her ride in the rickshaw. My son wanted chocolate, but I couldn't give him that. I could not buy fish or meat for months. I was a helpless mother. Let's see, in the end Allah looked up.

Prosperity has come, joy in mind
Fahmida received the Entrepreneur and E-Commerce Platform Award in 2024. Children are also doing well in studies. His daughter has passed the secondary examination with 'A' grade, his son is studying in class VIII.

Fahmida has hired a helper for ``Happy Kitchen'' organization for three thousand taka per month. Apart from this, five temporary delivery men (they have no fixed salary, they are paid on the basis of work) are also working. With the money he earns now, he has more than five thousand rupees a month even after meeting all his needs including house rent, family expenses, children's education.

Fahmida now writes about happiness in her diary, stories of happiness. Now he has only one word, and I don't want to look back. Now the only goal is to move forward.
