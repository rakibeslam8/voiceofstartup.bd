The basic salary of three army chiefs is 1 lakh 72 thousand, soldiers 21 thousand Tk 400

By বিশেষ প্রতিনিধি24 November 2026 at 5:59 pm6 min read

The government has decided the new pay structure for the armed forces almost two months after issuing the notification of new pay structure for civil servants. In this, the basic salary of the three chiefs of the army, air force and navy has been fixed at 1 lakh Tk 72 thousand. And the basic salary of soldiers will be 21 thousand Tk 400.

The Ministry of Defense has issued separate pay and allowance instructions for members of the armed forces in the light of the National Pay Scale 2026 on November 15. The 84-page directive outlines the new rules for determining pay and allowances for members of the Army, Navy and Air Force.

Separate pay scales have been fixed for Army Chiefs to Commissioned Officers, JCOs, Soldiers, Recruits and Cadets. Like government employees, the new basic pay for military personnel will be implemented in a phased manner, effective July 1, 2026, the directive said. Apart from this, there are separate instructions for the officers of Army Medical Corps, Dental Corps, Engineering, Signals and EME Corps. Similarly, the pay and allowances for Navy and Air Force officers have been fixed under the new structure according to rank.

According to the instructions, the army chief to warrant officer will get 40 percent of the new salary in the first six months and another 30 percent in the next six months. The rate will be 50 percent and 25 percent for cadets and sergeants below, respectively. After that, everyone will get full salary from July 1, 2027. However, the new rate of additional allowance will be effective from January 1, 2028. Until then, the allowance will be paid at the prevailing rate on June 30, 2026.

The total number of members of the three forces was not available on any official website. However, on June 7, 2017, the National Parliament was informed that at that time there were 1 lakh 62 thousand 125 people in the army, 25 thousand 81 people in the navy and 17 thousand 390 in the air force. At that time, the total number of military personnel in the three forces was 2 lakh 4 thousand 596 people. Apart from this, 20 thousand 894 civilian personnel were employed in the three forces.

What is the salary for top and other positions

According to the instructions, the fixed salary of each army chief is 1 lakh Tk 72 thousand. Apart from this, the new salary for the post of Lieutenant General has been fixed at 1 lakh Tk 64 thousand and 1 lakh Tk 56 thousand for Major General. And for the post of Vice Admiral of the Navy, the salary will be 1 lakh Tk 64 thousand and for the post of rear admiral the salary will be 1 lakh Tk 56 thousand. The fixed salary for the post of Air Marshal of the Air Force will be Tk 1 lakh 64 thousand and for the post of Air Vice Marshal Tk 1 lakh 56 thousand.

Apart from this, the pay scale has been fixed according to the years of service in the posts below Brigadier General. The basic salary of Brigadier General in the 17th year will be Tk 1 lakh 32 thousand 300. In the 17th year, the salary for the post of colonel will be 1 lakh 25 thousand Tk 300, 1 lakh Tk 22 thousand for the post of lieutenant colonel and 1 lakh 17 thousand Tk 700 for the post of major. The basic salary in the first year of the post of captain will be Tk 58 thousand. 48 thousand Tk 300 in the first year for the post of lieutenant and 46 thousand Tk 200 for the post of second lieutenant. And the basic salary of the soldier is fixed at 21 thousand Tk 400. The same pay structure will be applicable to members of equivalent rank of Navy and Air Force.

How much is Command and Staff AllowanceApart from the basic pay, the rates of command, staff, charges and teaching allowances have also been increased. The allowance for the army chief is 3 thousand Tk 750 per month. Lieutenant General and equivalent officers will get 2 thousand Tk 860. This allowance has been set at 2 thousand Tk 421 for major general and officers of equivalent rank, 2 thousand Tk 200 for brigadier general, 1 thousand Tk 982 for colonel and 1 thousand Tk 764 for lieutenant colonel. Major and equivalent officials will get 1 thousand Tk 330. This allowance is Tk 880 for captain and below. The eligibility allowance has also been increased, ranging from Tk 554 to Tk 880 per month.Flight and Expert Allowance

According to the instructions, in the case of army pilots and flight crews in the rank of Brigadier General and above, the flying allowance is Tk 12,000 per month and Tk 10,000 in the rank of Colonel and below. In this case, Navy commander and above officers will get Tk 12,000 and captain and below officers will get Tk 10,000. And in the case of the Air Force, a flight allowance of Tk 12,000 has been set for Air Commodore and above, Tk 10,000 for group captains and below and Tk 2,000 for ground instructors.

Specialist Allowance of Medical Officers is also fixed according to rank. 2 thousand Tk 830 per month for major general and above, 2 thousand Tk 200 for brigadier general level, 1 thousand Tk 982 for colonel level, 1 thousand Tk 764 for lieutenant colonel level and 1 thousand Tk 330 for major level. Apart from this, parachute allowance has been increased from Tk 664 to Tk 715, submarine allowance from Tk 3 thousand to Tk 4 thousand 500, scuba diving allowance from Tk 664 to Tk 715 and sniper allowance from Tk 650 to Tk 715.

NEW ALLOWANCES AND TIME OF EFFECTIVENESS

In the new guidelines, the Defense Service Allowance has been increased from Tk 6,000 to Tk 7,200 at the General level and Tk 6,690 at the Second Lieutenant level from Tk 5,575. Kit allowance has been increased from Tk 1,200 to Tk 1,320, Batman allowance from Tk 600 to Tk 1,200 and Disturbance allowance from Tk 2,000 to Tk 2,200. The risk allowance has been increased from Tk 110 per day to Tk 121.

New Mobile Allowance, Disabled Child Allowance, Haor-Dwip-Char Allowance and Radiological Risk Allowance have also been added in the guidelines. Major and above officers will get mobile allowance of Tk 500 per month and Captain and below Tk 150.

A monthly allowance of Tk 3,000 for a disabled child will be available for a maximum of two children. Haor, Dwip and Char Allowance will be 20 percent of the basic salary, subject to a maximum of Tk 5,000. Radioactive risk allowance of Tk 2,000 per month will be given to those working in Radar and Rooppur nuclear power plants.
