The story of building a multi-crore venture from a college student to a 200-employee industrial enterprise

By Daily Desher Potro25 November 2026 at 9:30 pm3 min read

Ayesha Siddika Kaya of Santhia Upazila, Pabna, is a unique example of how a college student can build a business worth crores of rupees in just seven and a half years by overcoming the conventional views of the society, severe financial crisis and the pressure of studies. With his indomitable willpower and hard work, he established Keya Food Products, where today around 200 people have permanent employment.

Story of Struggle and Beginnings
At the age of 23-24, while studying in college, Ayesha Siddiqa decided to become an entrepreneur instead of pursuing a traditional job and provide employment to others. His business journey started by manually burning ghee with only 30 kg of cream in the small scale of his home without any big capital.

The early days were of extreme struggle. During the day, he used to burn ghee and pack with his own hands from 10:00 to 12:00 at night, after managing college and studies. After that, he would take the night bus and rush to the markets of Dhaka, Cumilla, Chattogram and Sylhet. He used to go door to door to the customers all day and after finishing the marketing, he returned home and got down to production with new enthusiasm.

Financial hardship was once so evident that he did not even have money to buy food on the street or to cross rivers and rent CNG on the way from Dhaka to Pabna. On top of that there were negative comments and non-cooperation from the society and relatives about a woman doing business in rural areas. However, no obstacle could stop him.

Business Expansion & Current Location
Relying on the trust of the customers and the purity of the products, that small enterprise has turned into a huge industrial enterprise in a span of seven and a half years.

  • Production and Sales: Initially operating with only 30 kg of cream, now selling around 15 to 25 tonnes of ghee per month besides supplying around 18,000 liters of pure cow's milk per day,,,.
  • Versatile Food Products: Apart from ghee, Kaya Food Products currently manufactures butter, paneer and cheese. Besides, pure mustard oil and coconut oil are being produced through own mills and traditional wood mills.
  • Employment and Social Impact: About 200 employees are working under the organization across the country, through which financial solvency has returned to numerous families. Relatives and neighbors who once criticized his ventures are now proud of his success.

Success Principles and Leadership Styles
According to Ayesha Siddiqa Kaya, the only keys to long-term success in business are product quality, honesty and hard work. He believes that once the quality product reaches the hands of the customer, no one can stop the expansion of the business.
Despite being the owner of a huge company, he is equally dedicated to work. After four long years of hand-burning ghee himself, although the factory now employs several skilled artisans, he directly supervises production on a day-to-day basis, helps in packing the products and keeps track of the workers' family and finances.

Ayesha Siddika Care's journey conveys a powerful message to young and female entrepreneurs in Bangladesh—it is possible to rise from zero to the pinnacle of success if you have the courage to dream and work with integrity in the face of adversity.
