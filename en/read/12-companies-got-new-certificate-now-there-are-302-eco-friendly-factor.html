12 companies got new certificate, now there are 302 eco-friendly factories in Bangladesh

By নিজস্ব প্রতিবেদক21 November 2026 at 9:50 am2 min read

12 more factories manufacturing ready-made garments, textiles and equipment have recently received eco-friendly certification. Through this, Bangladesh crossed the milestone of 300 environment-friendly factories. At present there are 302 eco-friendly factories in the country.

Factories in Bangladesh's garment and textile sector have received US Green Building Council (USGBC) LEAD certification. According to the organization, out of 12 newly certified factories, 9 have received Lead Platinum and 3 Lead Gold certificates. Among these factories, Adji Trims of Dhamrai, Dhaka, got the lead platinum certificate by getting the maximum 97 points. The company manufactures essential equipment and packaging products for the readymade garment sector.

If you want to know, the managing director of Adji Trims. Shahriar told Pratham Alo that Adji Trims has the highest number of points among the eco-friendly accessories and packaging factories in the world. He said, 1 thousand 800 workers work in their environment friendly factory. They produce accessories and packaging products for 42 brands. Adji Trims exported accessories and packaging products worth 3.4 million dollars in the last financial year.

The other factories that got the LEAD Platinum certificate are D&S Pretty Fashions in Gazipur, Pramukshti Apparels in Narayanganj, FKL Printing Building, L'Usine Fashion in Gazipur, Visual Eco Stylewear in Chattogram and Textile and Washing Unit of KDS Apparels in Savar, Paxar Bangladesh in Savar and Expo Accessories in Gazipur. Apart from this, the factories that got Lead Gold certificate are Varnali Collections in Narayanganj, Bestek BD in Chattogram and Export Building-2 in Tangail.

Several organizations around the world certify environmentally friendly establishments. One of them is the US Green Building Council (USGBC). Established in 1993, USGBC gives the certificate called 'LEAD'. The full form of LEAD is Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design. To get this certificate, every project has to maintain quality at various stages from construction to production under the supervision of USGBC.

According to USGBC data, out of 302 environmentally friendly factories in the country so far, 134 have obtained Lead Platinum, 148 Lead Gold, 16 Lead Silver and 4 Lead Certified level certificates. No other country in the world has more lead certified factories than Bangladesh.
