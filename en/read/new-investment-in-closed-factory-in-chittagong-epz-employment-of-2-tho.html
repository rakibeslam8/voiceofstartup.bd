Closed factory in Chattogram EPZ gets fresh investment, 2,000 jobs expected

By নিজস্ব প্রতিবেদক19 November 2026 at 9:27 am2 min read

A factory in the Chittagong Export Processing Zone (EPZ) that has been closed for more than two years is set to resume production. A Singapore-China-owned company called Joyful (Bangladesh) Garment Company will produce high-end ready-made garments there.

Joyful (Bangladesh) Garment Company will invest USD 6 million in the closed factory. Its amount in Bangladeshi currency is about 75 crore taka (124 taka 75 paise per dollar). If the factory is opened, there will be employment of 2 thousand 230 Bangladeshi citizens.

On Wednesday, Joyful (Bangladesh) Garment Company signed a land lease agreement with Bangladesh Export Processing Zone Authority (BEPZA). The agreement was signed at Bepza Complex on Green Road in the capital. Bepza informed this information in a press release.

According to Bepza, Joyful Garment will make new investment in the factory of Siam's Superior Limited, one of the early companies of Chittagong EPZ. Siam's Superior closes in August 2024. Recently Bepza auctioned the equipment and infrastructure of the company. Joyful (Bangladesh) Garment bought the machinery and infrastructure of the factory as the highest bidder.

The new investment firm will use the existing industrial infrastructure in three plots of Chittagong EPZ. Production will start there after necessary renovations and modernization. For this, the company has planned to invest 6 million dollars.

According to Bepza data, the Joyful Garment factory plans to produce 2.5 million pieces of high quality ready-to-wear garments annually. These garments include hunting suits, waterproof outerwear, windbreakers, combat jackets, trench coats, overcoats, padded jackets, down jackets, denim jackets, casual jackets and ski jackets. Apart from this, various types of winter wear, puffer parkas and other specialized garments will also be produced.

The company has plans to export the manufactured garments to various countries including European Union (EU), UK, USA, Japan, Canada, Australia, Mexico, South Africa.

At the contract signing ceremony, Joyful (Bangladesh) Garment Company Managing Director Han Junjiao said that they are going to start production operations in an existing industrial infrastructure. Due to this, some renovation work has to be done in the factory. They expect to start production by March 2027 after completing necessary renovations.

Bepza Member (Engineering) Abdullah Al Mamun and Managing Director Han Junjia also signed the land lease agreement on behalf of Joyful Garment.

At that time, Bepza Executive Chairman Major General Mohammad Moazzem Hossain was present. He said that Bepza is committed to ensure all kinds of legitimate facilities and assistance to investors.

Bepza Executive Director Lt. Col. Mohsin Hasan, ASM Anwar Parvez and senior officers of the organization were present in the event.
