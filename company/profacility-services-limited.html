ProFacility Services Limited

Your Facility, Our Priority

ProFacility Services Limited is a Bangladesh-based integrated facility management company established in 2024. We provide comprehensive facility solutions for residential, commercial, and institutional properties, including security, cleaning, AC and HVAC maintenance, lift servicing, landscaping, staffing, technical support, and other facility-related services. Our centralized service model simplifies property operations by providing clients with a single, reliable service partner. With 106+ annual maintenance contracts and 40+ managed properties, we prioritize operational efficiency, professional service delivery, transparency, and customer satisfaction. Our mission is to make facility management dependable and hassle-free. “Your Facility, Our Priority.”

  • Stage: Established business
  • Location: Dhaka, Dhaka
  • Founded: 2024
