JAT AI LAB

Bangladesh-based technology initiative building AI-powered and practical digital solutions for everyday life.

**JAT AI LAB — Technology with a Human Purpose**

JAT AI LAB is an emerging technology and AI innovation initiative based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, founded by Jahangir Alam Talukder, a Principal SDET and software engineering professional with over 16 years of experience in software development, quality assurance, automation and cloud technologies.

Our mission is to develop accessible, affordable and practical digital solutions that improve everyday life, strengthen family connections, support agriculture, enhance safety and make technology more inclusive.

**Our Eight Digital Products:**

1. **JAT Bangla AI** — A Bangla-first, multilingual AI assistance initiative designed to make digital information and AI-powered tools more accessible to Bangla speakers and international users.

2. **JAT Messenger** — A browser-based communication platform supporting messaging and one-to-one voice and video calling, with a focus on accessible global communication. https://jatmessenger.purbachalracing.com/

3. **Amar Sathi** — An elder-assistance platform designed to help older adults stay connected with family members through simplified communication, safety features, reminders and everyday support. https://amar-sathi.purbachalracing.com/

4. **JAT Farmer Doctor** — A multilingual agricultural assistance platform providing crop-image analysis, plant-health guidance and access to farming information to help farmers make more informed decisions. https://jat-farmerdoctor.purbachalracing.com/

5. **JAT Global Live Watch** — An environmental and disaster-awareness platform providing information about weather hazards, earthquakes, floods and other potential risks. https://jat-globallivewatch.purbachalracing.com/

6. **Find Near Me** — A multilingual location-discovery application helping users find nearby hospitals, pharmacies, restaurants, transportation and essential services. https://findnearme.purbachalracing.com/

7. **Purbachal Racing** — A Bangladesh-made browser racing game combining interactive gameplay, changing weather conditions and locally inspired visual elements. https://purbachalracing.com/

8. **JAT Global Shop** — An affiliate-based product discovery platform connecting users with products from international online marketplaces. https://jat-global-shop.purbachalracing.com/

**Who We Serve**

Our solutions are designed for elderly people and families, farmers, everyday technology users, travelers, gamers and online shoppers in Bangladesh and worldwide.

**What Makes Us Different**

JAT AI LAB brings together multiple socially useful and commercially oriented digital products under one technology initiative. We emphasize multilingual accessibility, practical user needs, software quality, security-conscious development and cost-efficient cloud technologies.

Several products are already accessible as working web applications, while others continue through development, testing and validation.

**Our Vision**

To establish JAT AI LAB as a globally recognized technology company originating from Bangladesh, creating useful digital products and building international partnerships.

We welcome opportunities for startup incubation, mentorship, product demonstrations, pilot projects, media coverage, strategic partnerships and potential investment.
